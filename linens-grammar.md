## Intent

Keep **sleep and body contact layers** clean, rotated, and **replaced before they fail** — not only when something rips. **Make new pieces on a habit cadence** even when old stock still passes inspection; demote the surplus to **backup / trail / rag** instead of throwing good cloth away.

**Fiber doctrine (Y8):** **Flax** = fine/soft (sheets · pillowcases · comfort fill) · **Hemp** = strength (rope · canvas · coarse outer layers where named)

## Wash cadence

| Item | Cadence | Method |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Top sheet (in use)** | **Weekly** class · **2×/wk** if sweat/oil heavy | Warm rinse · mild soap · line dry · **no mold store wet** |
| **Pillowcase (in use)** | **Weekly** · with sheet rotation | Same |
| **Blanket / coverlet** | **Monthly** touch · **seasonal** deep when named | Beat · sun · spot wash · full wash when smoke/larder smell loads |
| **Body tunics / breech** | **Weekly** wear rotation minimum | Wash tub · line dry |
| **Trail demoted pieces** | **Before pack** only | Quick rinse if muddy · not campus rotation |

**Rule:** **Wet bundle → line same day → dry loft or rack** — no overnight damp pile @ cubiculum.

## Replace vs repair

| Read | Action |
|------|--------|
| **PASS** | Keep in rotation |
| **AMBER** — thin patch · loose hem · one fray | **Repair same week** · stay in rotation |
| **RED** — hole · permanent stain · smell lock · seam blowout | **Retire from body contact** → trail demotion or rag |
| **Structural (rope bed)** | Frayed hemp lattice → **splice or re-lace** before frame upgrade |

**Do not** patch a **RED** sheet back onto the respawn bed without player override.

---

## Habitual make *(even when stock OK)*

| Cadence | Target | Why |
|---------|--------|-----|
| **~every 2–3 years** | **×1 white sheet** · **×1 pillowcase** | Wear is gradual — bank spare before RED |
| **~every 3–4 years** | **×1 blanket** or **duvet class** when named | Loft compresses · moths · smoke |
| **~every 2 years** | **×1 body tunic** or **sock batch** | Same grammar · smaller loom time |
| **On upgrade hero** | **Full set for new bed frame** | **PROPER-BED-FRAME-2** runway — sheets + rope lattice together |

**Surplus rule:** If the new piece passes and the old still **PASS** → old goes to **SET-C peg** or **trail bin** — **not midden**.

## Demotion ladder

```
IN USE (bed / body)
    ↓ wash + inspect
SPARE SET (peg · horreum textile shelf)
    ↓ still PASS but superseded by habitual make
BACKUP / CAMPUS RESERVE (labeled shelf · emergency guest)
    ↓ AMBER or player choice
TRAIL GEAR (wagon · foot pack · bridge kit)
    ↓ RED
RAG / FIRE STARTER / PACKING (culina · chem wipe · forge)
    ↓
MIDDEN (only when unsalvageable)
```
