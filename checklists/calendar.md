# Season calendar

Recurring annual dates and windows only. This file says **when**; [government/procedures](../government/procedures/) says **how**; [map](../map/index.md) says **where**. One-off scheduling belongs in `now.md` and `plans/next-items.md`.

Every sow and harvest is mandatory unless the player explicitly defers it. Name outstanding items before a band closes.

## January
- **20 Jan** — Ice haul opens · `ICE-HAUL`
- **~18 Jan** — Annual holding walk and care-doc tick · `STOCK-CARE-AUDIT`

## February
- **Feb – Apr** — Goat kidding / freshen window · `GOAT-KID` *(milking blocked until freshen)*
- **8 Feb** — Ice max haul (peak) · `ICE-HAUL`
- **10 Feb** — Ice max haul (peak) · `ICE-HAUL`
- **14 Feb** — Ice haul tail · `ICE-HAUL`
- **24 Feb** — Test every bank in the seed-vault roster · `SEED-RAG-TEST`
- **24 Feb** — Seed-bank regeneration check · `SEED-REGEN`
- **26 Feb** — Spring sow opens · `SPRING-SOW`
- **26 Feb – 16 Mar** — Parsley / fava seed scout lap · `SEED-SCOUT` *(beyond campus — acquire before sow close)*

## March
- **11 Mar** — Exped / cart trips open
- **16 Mar** — Spring sow closes · `SPRING-SOW` *(emmer · lentil · field flax · chickpea · hemp · fava · culina herbs)*
- **Mar** — Mark the wild einkorn and barley stands while green · `GRAIN-GATHER-WILD` *(`P-05-A/B` · `P-06-A/B/C`)*
- **Sow day + 3 weeks** — Bird establishment window · `BIRD-WATCH` · `BIRD-DEVICE-RESET`
- **to 16 Mar** — Oak orchard spring read / replacement window · `OAK-ORCHARD-READ`
- **Mar – Apr** — Shoot thin · `GRAPE-SHOOT-THIN` *(the budbreak job)*
- **21 Mar** — Apiary expand / swarm / wax harvest opens · `HIVE-SPLIT` · `WAX-RENDER`
- **21 Mar – 20 Apr** — Campus herb seed designation · `HERB-SEED-DESIGNATE`

## April

- **20 Apr** — Apiary / swarm / wax harvest closes
