# Conditional checklist

Checks that fire on a **plan** rather than on an observation or a date — what has to be true before a given kind of day can start.

Distinct from [triggers.md](triggers.md): those fire on something read off the world *(it rained, the crock is full)*. These fire on something the player decides to do.

## `SOW-PREP` — before any sow day

Procedure detail in [sowing.md](../government/procedures/sowing.md). Windows in [calendar.md](calendar.md).

| ID | When | Check | A miss costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SEED-RAG-TEST` | Two days before the gate | Germination-test **every** bank going in, separately | The sowing rate. The same harvest in two stores can differ by half, and a dead bank is only findable while there is still time |
| `SOIL-READ` | Gate day, morning and afternoon | Soil temperature and moisture **at seed depth** — not air, not the calendar | Seed rotted in a cold wet bed. Invisible until nothing comes up |
| `SEED-RESERVE` | Before anything is drilled | Hold a reserve from every elite bank. Check bed area against the bank at the same time | A selected line, permanently. One bad season ends it |
| `SOW-ROLL` | Straight after rolling | Walk the bed for seed sitting proud; re-rake and hand-press. A roller bridges over hollows in stony ground | Whole square metres, for ten minutes of work |
| `BIRD-WATCH` | First 3 days after sowing | Rattle line up from day one; brush soil over fine seed | The birds decide in those three days whether the field is worth three weeks of visits |
| `BIRD-DEVICE-RESET` | Every ~4 days until rooted | Move it, change it, layer it — see [periodic.md](periodic.md) | The sown seed. They learn any single device in four days |
