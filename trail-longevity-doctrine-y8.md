## Hydrology first *(GRADE before FILL)*

Water destroys geometry faster than wheels destroy crown.

### Pinch / terrace sequence

1. **Read flow** — where sheet water enters the wheel line?
2. **GRADE** — shallow **V-ditch** or **off-crown channel** to low side · **drain pinch** before rut fill.
3. **FILL** — rut · wash · ford exit line · only after drain path exists.
4. **MARK** — char re-stake if crown moved.

### Berms and small walls

| Structure | Where | Grammar |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Upslope berm** | Uphill of crown · slow sheet flow before track | Local bank pick · not on crown |
| **Cheek wall** | Terrace cut · inside bend · bridge upstream face | Dry-stack or lime-pinch · **30–80 cm** · keeps flow off wheel line |
| **Flood train** | Bridge abutment · ford exit | Bank pick + gravel wedge @ scour — **REINFORCE** tier |

**Not:** continuous retaining walls · Roman cut-and-fill heroes · berm **on** the crown.

## Bridge before leg

Crossings are the decade tier. Leg TQ follows.

| Tier | Longevity role |
|------|----------------|
| **Concrete abut + pozz wing** | **Decade+** · scour cheek · listen-only most years |
| **Reinforced wood BQ ~3.5** | **Decade class** abutments · redeck/touch on deck cycle |
| **Live wood early** | **TOUCH** aprons · plan redeck ~5–8 yr class |
| **Ford FQ GO** | **Seasonal** · eliminate on pozz route — **TRAIL-FORD-1-BRIDGE-1** |

**Outbound:** stone + mortar for **BRIDGE-TOUCH / REINFORCE**. **Return:** GRADE · CLEAR · FILL when kit ×0.
