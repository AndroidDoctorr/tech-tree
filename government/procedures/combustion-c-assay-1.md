# Combustion carbon assay *(campus procedure)*

**Opened:** d3744 · **Apparatus:** **`COMBUSTION-C-TRAIN-Y12-1`**

## Method

1. **Purge** the O₂ line from **`GASHOLDER-2` Bell B** — first draw is air · discard · **outdoor · no flame near open H₂**
2. **Caustic trap** — **`WATER-CELL-1` cathode liquor** diluted to **~10% NaOH** · tared **`P-LAB-FLASK-BOIL-1`** + gas sparge tube · weigh trap **before**
3. **Combustion tube** — stoneware **`GLASS-TUBE-HEAT-BREAK-1`** train · **O₂ ~40 ml/min class** · sample boat from **`P-LAB-TRAY-2`**
4. **Burn to completion** — no char tail · CO₂ through caustic · **re-weigh trap**
5. **Carbon mass** from trap gain *(blank subtracted)* · **C% = m_C / m_sample**
