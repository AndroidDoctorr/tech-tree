# Audience & tone

---

## POV contract

**Promise:** The audience learns rules **with** characters — no opening text crawl explaining respawn.

**Exceptions (thin context only):**

- Alien cold open — mission exists; rules not fully shown
- Pre-abduction vignettes — who these people were
- Occasional observer cutaway — reaction shots, not exposition dumps

---

## Tone sliders

| Dial | Setting |
|------|---------|
| **Realism** | High for craft/survival; compress time for montages |
| **Violence** | Implied > shown; white flash > blood |
| **Humor** | Dry — engineer redundancy, assistant deadpan, corpse-pile fail |
| **Wonder** | First fire, first smelt, first view of compound another player built |
| **Horror** | Starvation, betrayal, repeated death psychology |
| **Alien frame** | **Mysterious, not hostile** — *Star Trek* professionalism; finale apology |

---

## Alien tone (*Star Trek vibe*)

| Principle | Execution |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Mysterious early** | Presence felt; rules not explained by them |
| **Never hostile** | No mockery; no betting on deaths; cutaways are **clinical + burdened** |
| **Professionals** | They disagree internally; they don't cackle |
| **Good intentions** | Contact protocol protects **both** sides |
| **Free will** | Assistants advise; finale briefing is **optional** |
| **Apology at end** | Regret for stress; clear **why** — see [scenes/alien-reveal-apology.md](../scenes/alien-reveal-apology.md) |

**Audience should leave:** awe + hope, not tricked.

---

## What to hide vs reveal

| Hide early | Reveal mid | Reveal late |
|------------|------------|-------------|
| Respawn | Death counter meaning | Observers exist |
| Other players exist | Immortality rules | **Full why + apology** |
| Device limits | Cooperation = scored | Contact invitation terms |

---

## Geography literacy (optional character)

Protagonist who **recognizes terrain** (VR, maps, war coverage, Egypt trips) — plays as intuition, not superpower:

- *"This feels like the Orontes drainage."*
- Chooses camp like city founders chose Antioch — **water, plain, pass**

Audience doesn't need Google Earth; **one line** of recognition sells obsession.
