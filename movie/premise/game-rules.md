## Win condition (hybrid draft)

**Visible:** reach threshold (orbit / beacon / probe — TBD)

**Hidden score includes:**

| Metric | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Tech tree nodes (merged knowledge) | High |
| **Teaching events** (charcoal shown, salt shared) | High |
| **Trade / shared winter store** | High |
| Solo hoarding | Neutral or negative |
| Violence | Logged; not rewarded |

**Theme:** Solo speedrun fails or stalls. **Together** ≈ journal protagonist — **that may be the point.**

## Assistant behavior

- Answers **how** and **what to look for** — not full autopilot
- May **withhold** if questions are vague (plot: frustration)
- Same core OS, or **slight personality drift** per user?
- **Alien back-channel?** Observers can listen; maybe rarely nudge — use sparingly

---

## Multiplayer dynamics (built-in, not emergency)

- Trade, murder, alliance, religion, bureaucracy — all on table
- **No respawn farming** keeps economy weird
- Meeting another player = **biggest midgame twist** for loner protagonist types
