# Alien observers

Mission control for the trial — **mysterious, not hostile.** **Professional, not playful.** **Star Trek first-contact energy:** they know what they're doing, they mean well, and they will **apologize** when it's over.

---

## Core philosophy

| Value | On screen |
|-------|-----------|
| **Good intentions** | Contact only when humanity can handle it **without breaking itself** |
| **Independence** | Assistants inform; they don't command. Players can refuse the final briefing. |
| **Free will** | Non-interference during trial — even when it's ugly |
| **Professionalism** | Calm voices; metrics; dissent among observers allowed |
| **Accountability** | Finale: **explicit apology** for stress + explanation of **why** |

**Not:** Squid Game vipers · Prometheus idiots · Men-in-black sneer  
**Yes:** *Star Trek* briefing room · *Arrival* patience · Prime Directive weight

---

## Room design (open)

- **Avoid:** Generic grey council chamber; cackling monitors.
- **Consider:** Biomorphic architecture; water/light motifs; silhouettes; translation as **subtitle ripple**; **competence** in every frame.

## What they're actually testing

**Draft thesis:** Solo humans stall. **Complementary strangers** can bootstrap faster **if** they don't murder the process.

Hidden score includes:

- Tech tree nodes (regional merge)
- **Cooperation events** (trade, teaching, shared winter store)
- Violence without permanent harm (logged, not rewarded)
- **Free choice** at finale (show up to briefing or not)

**The point (kind of):** Contact is offered to a species that proves it can **we**, not just **I**.

## Finale — reveal & apology

Full beat sheet: [../scenes/alien-reveal-apology.md](../scenes/alien-reveal-apology.md)

**Must include:**

1. **Mystery lifts** — presence, not invasion
2. **Apology** — stress was imposed; regret is sincere
3. **Explanation** — why trial / why past / why bounded immortality
4. **Free will** — players may walk away
5. **Star Trek hope** — universe is bigger; humanity not owned
