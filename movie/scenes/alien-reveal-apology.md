# Finale — alien reveal & apology

**Circle step:** CHANGE  
**POV:** Survivors + observers  
**Purpose for audience:** **Star Trek pay-off** — mysterious was never hostile; stress was instrument; free will respected.

---

## Tone contract

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| **Professional** calm | Condescending parent |
| **Mysterious** until reveal | Snarky quip villains |
| **Regret for suffering caused** | "You needed to learn" lecture |
| **Explain **why** contact protocol exists** | Info-dump physics |
| **Leave wonder** — not every answer | Tie every bow |

**Vibe:** *Star Trek* first-contact briefing · *Arrival* patience · Prime-Directive energy — **they waited because rushing contact harms both sides.**

---

## Beat

1. **Threshold crossed** — beacon / orbit / cooperation score — however win is defined.
2. **Sky changes subtly** — not Death Star; light refraction, silent presence.
3. **Assistants pause** — then: *"Your observers request a conversation. You may decline."* **Free will beat #1.**
4. **Players choose** — gather or ignore; at least **Torr + Rosa + Elena** say yes.
5. **Contact form** — not humanoid required; **translated presence** (light, water shape, orbit ring) — **competent, still**.
6. **Apology first:** *"We imposed fear. That was necessary to measure truth. We regret the cost to your peace of mind."*
7. **Explanation:** First contact policy — species must demonstrate **cooperative survival + tech** without **central coercion**; trial simulates deep past to avoid contaminating present cultures.
8. **Immortality framed:** *"Bounded reset — so one mistake wouldn't end your story. Not a toy. A safeguard."*
9. **Cannibalism / deaths acknowledged** — not mocked; *"We recorded. We did not intervene. Non-interference is also a burden on us."*
10. **Outcome:** Contact **approved** / **deferred** / **conditional** — Earth not conquered; **invitation extended** when humanity is ready **as a whole**.
11. **Final line option:** *"You were never alone. You were never owned. Walk forward."*
12. **Cut:** Players back at camp / compound — **changed** — choose to stay together or disperse.

---

## Observer dialogue (scraps)

- *"We do not harvest suffering. We measure whether suffering teaches cooperation."*
- *"Your species names valleys for cities before cities exist. That impulse is why we waited."*
- *"Independence was never revoked. The device answers. It does not command."*
- *"We apologize for the hunger. Not for the question."*

---

## Audience feeling target

Leave theater with **hope + awe**, not **trickery hangover**. The game was real; the stress was **instrument**; the players **earned** the conversation.
