# Cannibalism intro — Death count: 1

**Circle step:** FIND (immortality reveal)  
**POV:** Two players (D + victim) → audience surrogate  
**Purpose for audience:** Learn respawn **with** characters; horror via implication; rules land hard.

---

## Setup

- Two players, same biome, **days** without food (or injured hunter lost kit).
- Delirium honesty: *"We're going to die anyway."*
- Pact: one kills; one eats; **maybe** both agree victim "volunteers" — messy morality on purpose.

---

## Beat

1. **Exhausted camp** — fire dead. Dialogue thin. Audience feels corner.
2. **Decision** — not romanticized. Practical terror.
3. **Victim lies still** — or stands — **killer lifts rock** (or equivalent tool).
4. **Off-screen kill:** Rock up → face or hands → **CUT WHITE** — one sharp sound, not repeated.
5. **Hold white** ~2 sec. Silence.
6. **Fade in** — **slightly different camera angle**, same location. Killer alone. Meat **gone** (corpse vanished — killer doesn't understand yet).
7. **Victim voice behind them:** *"What the fuck."*
8. **Victim** standing **~2 m away**, intact, clothes maybe wrong detail (continuity easter egg).
9. **On-screen text (minimal UI):** **`Death count: 1`**
10. **Killer** drops rock. Long stare. No joke yet.
11. **Assistant (both devices, out of sync):** *"Respawn anchor: last sleep site. Your body was reset."*
12. **Alien cutaway (optional 5 sec):** Observer notes aggression + discovery latency.

---

## Lines / scraps

- *"You were dead. I saw—"*
- *"I felt it too."*
- *"So it doesn't… count?"* / *"It counted. You're back."*
- *"Don't ever ask me to agree to that again."*

---

## Notes

- **Do not show** eating if avoidable — implication + respawn is enough; censors + taste.
- **Death count** only appears **after** first respawn — audience learns UI = real.
- Journal echo: [rules.md](../../rules.md) — no cliff-farming; this teaches why.
- Tone: horror → numb → dark humor later when someone dies a second time casually.
