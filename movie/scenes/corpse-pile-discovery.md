# Corpse pile discovery

**Circle step:** SEARCH  
**POV:** Player E (+ witness F optional)  
**Purpose for audience:** Rule #2 without death — comedy + engineer brain punished.

---

## Beat

1. Player has **seen** or **heard about** respawn by now — OR this scene is **pre-cannibalism** for a player who didn't witness (parallel timeline trickier).
2. **Logic:** *"If bodies reset, I kill the same guy ten times and stack—"* OR animal deaths only at first.
3. **Montage:** Pile grows. No bones accumulate. Pile is **empty clothes** that **fade** or **single corpse flickers away**.
4. **Assistant:** *"Remains are not persisted. This is not a rendering error."*
5. **Witness:** *"How long were you going to do that?"*
6. **Player E:** Quietly stops. Beat. *"…For science."*
