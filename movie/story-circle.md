# Story circle — Dan Harmon template

*You · Need · Go · Search · Find · Take · Return · Change*

Each step is a **zone of comfort → unfamiliar → adaptation**. Draft below; strike through or replace freely.

---

## 1. YOU — comfort zone

**Draft:** Cold open on **aliens** (comfort *for them* — control room, metrics). Hard cut to **pre-abduction vignettes**: 5–12 ordinary humans in ordinary trouble or boredom. One we follow most (engineer brain, geography nerd, maps in their head — optional journal echo).

**Status:** Protagonist POV candidate clear; ensemble TBD.

---

## 2. NEED — want something

**Draft:** Each player wants **out** — home, answers, phone signal, food. Under that: **survive the night**. Aliens need **data** — is this species worth contact?

**Gap:** Does any player *want* to win the game before they know it's a game?

---

## 3. GO — cross threshold

**Draft:** Simultaneous (or staggered) wakeups. Wilderness. **Assistant boot:** *"Good morning. You are alive."* No tutorial. First death within Act 1 for someone reckless or unlucky.

**Obvious beat:** Wake sequence montage — different biomes, same panic.

---

## 4. SEARCH — adapt, road of trials

**Draft:** Solo survival montage intercut — fire, water, shelter, first kill, first mistake. **Journal parallel:** weir, kiln, goat trap — but with **wrong** shortcuts (cannibalism, corpse pile, hero cliff).

**Ensemble:** Some find each other; some avoid contact. Geography determines who meets first (river corridors, smoke columns).

**Gap:** How long before first player-player contact?

---

## 5. FIND — meet goal (midpoint)

**Draft:** **Find** can be double:

- **External:** First **major tech** or **first proof of others** (smoke on horizon / bronze glint / donkey bray).
- **Internal:** **Find** the respawn rule — cannibalism scene OR corpse-pile fail OR witnessed death + resurrection.

**Candidate midpoint:** Two starving players → pact → implied kill → white → **Death count: 1** → both understand immortality differently (predator guilt vs relief).

---

## 6. TAKE — pay the price

**Draft:** Knowledge has cost even with respawn:

- Trust broken between players.
- Alien observers **raise difficulty** or **narrow win window**.
- Someone **wastes a century** on revenge instead of tech.
- Winter / flood / predator takes someone **dozen times** — psychological price.

**Take:** Winning solo vs winning together — faction splits.

---

## 7. RETURN — chase intensifies

**Draft:** Race to finish line with **partial map of the rules**. Maybe players who met early have **compound** vs loners with **head start**. Assistants may **diverge** in personality (same OS, different users — or different alien sponsors?).

**Gap:** What is the **visible finish line** on Earth before space?

---

## 8. CHANGE — new equilibrium

**Draft:** Aliens **reveal** — mysterious, **not hostile**. **Apology** for imposed stress. **Explanation:** contact protocol, bounded immortality, why the past. Players choose to listen. Earth gets **hope**, not conquest.

Full scene: [scenes/alien-reveal-apology.md](scenes/alien-reveal-apology.md)

| Ending branch | Change |
|---------------|--------|
| **Invitation deferred** | Players changed; world not told — **Star Trek hope** |
| **Compound stays** | They **choose** Bronze-age village together |
| **Solo walkaway** | Priya alone with map — valid; observers accept |

**Change:** Humanity is a **team sport** — or one person learns that and still wins the argument.

---
