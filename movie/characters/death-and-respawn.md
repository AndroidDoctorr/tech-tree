# Death & respawn — character reactions

*Same rules, different psychology.*

---

## Rule reminder (film)

- Die → wake where you last slept
- Corpse gone
- Optional on-screen: **Death count: N**

---

## Reaction matrix (draft)

| Character | First death | After cannibalism reveal | Late-game pattern |
|-----------|-------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| **Engineer (A)** | Cautious; tests once deliberately | Horrified; notes corpse vanish | Minimizes deaths; documents rules |
| **Partner (D)** | Accidental | **Killer** — guilt vs "it didn't count" | May use murder as conflict tool until others punish socially |
| **Cooperator** | Sacrifices for others | Forgives? or never trusts D | Dies many times for village |
| **Denier** | Refused to believe | Breakdown | — |

---

## Scene: corpse pile fail

**Player E** stacks corpses (from repeated murders or animal kills?) expecting bone/hide farm.

**Assistant:** *"Decomposition isn't your bottleneck. The body isn't there."*

**Player F** (witness): *"How long were you planning to—"* 

**Comedy beat:** E quietly dismantles pile like a kid caught stacking pillows.

**Audience:** Learns rule #2 without anyone dying on screen.

---

## Scene: "it doesn't count" debate

After respawn, killer and victim argue:

- **Killer:** *"You're fine. I didn't mean—"*
- **Victim:** *"I felt it. It counted for me."*

Immortality ≠ consequence-free **for story purposes.**

## Death counter as character

- Some never look

## Off-screen death grammar

- Rock lifted → cut white → fade in → angle shift → **Death count**
- No gore requirement; sound design carries weight once, then white becomes familiar (dark joke)
