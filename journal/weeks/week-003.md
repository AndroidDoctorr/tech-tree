---

## Arc in one paragraph

Week three shifted from **building the workshop** to **running it**. You finished paths (T-1 **100%**, T-5 hut↔camp **100%**), closed the farm fence to **~95%**, cleared **~96 m²** of sowing beds, and **planted elite seed** (~46 g emmer, einkorn, barley, flax) on Day 16. Sprouts showed by Day 18–19; a scare line went up on Day 17. Ceramics graduated from first test tiles to a **grog formula**, a **P-δ crucible v1**, and reliable firings — then you pivoted hard into **brick production**: brick mold, **26 stackable fired bricks** by Day 21 (~14% of the ~180 hypocaust budget), grog mortar **kiln lining** that survived its first hot cycle, and a **materials roadmap** for lime mortar and higher temps. Charcoal batches **#2–#5** kept pace; store C peaked near **~49 kg** and settled at **~40 kg** after heavy kiln days. Smelting still waits. **You have crops, charcoal, and a brick stack.**

- **Farm + kiln + charcoal** in parallel is realistic for an immortal with ADHD pacing — but **clay haul becomes the bottleneck** once reserve drops below one press run.
- **Lime** needs proof of temperature before slaking pits go near the hut.
