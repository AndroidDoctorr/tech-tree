## Close

**THE WEEK OF ONE CHANGE AT A TIME — AND OF THE SAME FAILURE IN FOUR CRAFTS.**

Seven days, three arcs closed, and a rule discovered that is bigger than any of them.

**The apiary aim of the whole year closed in eleven days.** HIVE-3 cut over, SKEP-2 driven into HIVE-7, **every colony on movable comb** — and **two emptied baskets queued to render at d3265 and d3274, which ends the wax constraint that has throttled the electrical work all of Y10.** The method improved as it went: HIVE-5 took a fortnight to lay because **a driven colony carries no wax budget and must build its house out of its own body at 8:1**, so HIVE-7 was **furnished before they arrived** — drawn comb, brood, feed from the first hour. **HIVE-5 was the experiment; HIVE-7 got the result.** And ★★ **movable comb turned out to be INSURANCE, not just harvest** — a queenless colony is rescued with a comb of eggs, and until d3252 no colony here had a way out.

**The generator got measured and then improved **one variable at a time**, which is the discipline the week was really about. **Replaceable parts are not mainly about repair — they are how you isolate a variable in a thing that has to keep working** — and it only became a method once there was a baseline, because **before d3248 "better" was a feeling and now it is a subtraction.** Closing the air gap bought **+25% volts and +50% power for an afternoon and no materials**, and better than the gain, **it named the next bottleneck: the magnet has its own reluctance, so the lever is now FIELD, not gap.** Caught one of my own bad entries too — **a flywheel cannot fix a steady droop.

**And the rule of the week, which I did not go looking for.** The jerky case-hardened: a sealed shell over a wet middle, **dark and firm and dry to the thumb and completely rotten by the time it mattered.** Four days later a raw hide nearly went into strong tan liquor and would have done **exactly the same thing.** So it is not a fact about meat or about leather: ★★ **ANY PROCESS THAT WORKS FROM THE OUTSIDE IN IS RUINED BY HURRY, BECAUSE HURRY SEALS THE SURFACE AND STRANDS THE MIDDLE** — drying, tanning, curing lime, carburising steel. **And in every case the finished-looking outside is the lie.**

**Which pairs with the other thread: **measure, do not judge.** The jerky was **the first food I have ever made to a number** — and the scale is precisely what caught the batch my eye had passed. The wire was checked by resistance rather than believed. The rope was **not** certified, because **a rope is only as strong as its most-loaded strand and this one closed unevenly.
