## Arc in one paragraph

Week six was **running the plant**. You locked in **staggered firing** — **Kiln A** for lime, **Kiln B** for bricks, never both the same afternoon — and treated it like a job: haul clay, dual-press **+14** most make days, load **ten**, bank **nine**. **Four** production fires in B added **36** stackable bricks (**54 → 90**); with **35** in B's lining you're **~50%** of the mid **~250** hut v2 budget. **Lime trials #3–#5** all went **2/2**; quicklime stock hit **~1.81 kg**. **P-δ** passed the **sand heat test** — metal prep unlocked at the crucible, not the furnace. **Pit B-2** and **B-3** opened; charcoal store stayed **~65–70 kg**. You built a **bellows reed frame**, sketched **Kiln C** (tuyere + goat hide bag), logged **goat prep → goat day** on two future factory pauses, and closed the **corpse-cliff** loophole in [rules.md](../../rules.md). The gorge waited. **Boring won.**
