Week four was **throughput engineering**. You stockpiled **~62 kg clay** in one blitz, fired **36 stackable bricks** (26 → 62), collected **11 floor stones** for hut v2, built a **reliable pot set** (P-ζ cooking bowl, P-η slaking dish), and crossed from "making ceramics" to **running a factory**: dual brick molds, **~64 kg charcoal** in store, **Kiln B pad staked**, and **charcoal pit B** laid out. Next: **pit B, Kiln B build, quicklime feedstock**, and **2×** firing capacity.
