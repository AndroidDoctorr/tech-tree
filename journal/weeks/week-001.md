# Week 1 summary

**Days 1–7 · ~10,000 BC · Orontes valley (near future Antioch)**  
**Character:** Immortal; respawns at bed pit; hunger but no starvation  
**Play style:** Industrious collector; parallel long projects; realism over speed

---

## Arc in one paragraph

You woke alone on a terrace above the Orontes with a handheld assistant and modern clothes. Seven days later you have a mapped base camp, daily protein from a fish weir, fire and **17 kg charcoal** in dry storage, sorted material yards, elite seed selection for early breeding, a **70%-fenced** farm plot (unplanted), a **1 m charcoal kiln** that works (~31% yield), and the **corner post holes** of a planned sunken hut with kiln niche and hypocaust reserved for v2. You are not smelting. You are not hungry. You are laying Roman infrastructure at Natufian pace — which is fine; you're immortal and lazy, not stupid.

*Day 8 addendum: full-force hut day → **~65%** complete standing structure; see [day-008.md](../days/week-002/day-008.md).*

## Realism notes (ancestor perspective)

- **Solo is viable** so far for fire, trap, cordage, charcoal — labor-heavy but doable.
- **Half-burnt charcoal corners** and **grey clay cracks** — craft is iterative, not blueprint.
- **Seed selection by taste/smell** is crude genetics — but it's exactly how selection starts.
- **Hut + hypocaust + wall kiln** is ambitious for week 2; assistant keeps v1 scoped to dry pit house + cook niche.
- **Smelting** remains weeks/months away — 17 kg charcoal is fuel, not a copper industry.
