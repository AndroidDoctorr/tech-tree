Week eight opened with **lime #6** and **batch #11**, hit **bellows v1** on Day 51 (goat hide + reed frame), then split focus: **brick fire #10–11** (**108 → 136** stackable) and **Kiln C** from pad stake to **smoke-cured cap** (**~75%**, **22** lining bricks). **Tuyere** fired, mounted, **cold blast pass** Day 54. **Lime #7** brought quicklime to **~2.47 kg**. **194** green bricks in pipeline. Week ends with Kiln C **ready for hot test** — Week 9 proves it.
