`P_gross = ρgQH ≈ 1000 × 9.81 × 0.0254 × 7.4 ≈ 1.84 kW`

That is the **site resource**, not present shaft power. The present open-race undershot arrangement captures only a fraction. The nearly unchanged generator load line after the forebay proves that better water availability did not become comparable wheel power.
