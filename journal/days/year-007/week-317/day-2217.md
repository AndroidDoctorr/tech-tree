### HUB-FLUE-CU-RADIATOR-SLATE-2217 *(player Q · engineering read)*

**Question:** Flat **copper plates** through the chimney wall — vanes/radiators into the room — reclaim heat from smoke?

**Short answer:** **Yes, it would add room heat** — but **copper plates in the flue wall are not the old-world pattern**. Better precedents are **masonry heat storage** and **iron pipe radiators** (much later). **File as optional upgrade** after the stack is built and smoke-tested.

| Approach | Precedent | Verdict @ Turris hub |
|----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Long masonry flue path** | **Kachelofen / masonry heater** — smoke maze through brick/tile mass; mass radiates for hours | **Primary path you're on** — cheek mass + island hood = free reclaim |
| **Metal in smoke path** | **19th-c cast-iron stove-pipe radiators** (e.g. Wilmot Castle ~1887) — boxes/tubes on **stove pipe**, not ancient chimneys | **Concept valid · iron preferred over copper in hot/sooty zone** |
| **Copper fins through wall** | **No strong ancient precedent** · modern copper **flue liners** = corrosion resistance, not room radiators | **Prototype OK @ your copper tier · durability/soot/creosote risk** |
| **Franklin / Rumford class** | **Baffles + shallow radiant firebox** — 18th-c | **Island hood + cheek mass covers part of this** |

**Physics (honest):** Hot gas touches metal in the flue → metal conducts → room-side vane radiates/convects. **Gain is real but modest** unless you **slow the gas** (long path, baffles) or **add surface area** without killing draft.

**Copper-specific risks:** Soot insulation · creosote condensation if flue runs too cool · oxide scale · thermal expansion vs mortar · **penetration leaks** · copper **steals heat from draft** — can weaken updraft if overdone.

**Recommendation:** Finish **brick cheek + hood mass** first (matches culina + Kachelofen grammar). If you still want metal reclaim after **live smoke PASS**, slate **HUB-FLUE-HEAT-RECLAIM-1** — **forged iron fins** @ mid-stack (hotter zone) or **thin copper vanes** @ upper warm band only · **not** through the throat. Defer until bond-beam penetration geometry is fixed.
