### Hour-by-hour (compressed)

Mid-wall holes on long sides — **4 new** plus corners re-opened. **Eight posts** set: corners get the 3.0–3.2 m poles; mids get 2.2–2.5 m. Gravel tamp. Willow bark lash collars at top.

**Ridge (1100–1300)**

**Floor pit (1300–1600)**

**Walls (1600–1800)**

**Thatch (1800–2000)**
