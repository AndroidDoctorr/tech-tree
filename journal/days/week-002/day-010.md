### Dawn — weir, then hut

### Ridge cap (~0900–1030)

| Task | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Gap before | **~40 cm** open strip at apex |

You stand on the ladder at full height and pat the ridge. It doesn't leak when you pour a bark cup of water on it. Sample size: one cup. Mood: invincible.

### Move the bed (~1400–1630)

| Haul | From camp bed pit |
|------|-------------------|

**Respawn anchor moved.** You say it aloud: *I sleep here now.* Camp bed pit left empty — emergency bench only.

No rain. **Dry night #1.**

Assistant at lights-out: "One night isn't statistics. Two is habit. You're officially ** housed**. Walls still leak wind on north/west. Oven niche still a hole. Fix those when lazy ends."
