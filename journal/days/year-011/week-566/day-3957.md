## Discoveries

1. **`ST-MAG-1` poles file concave at orange, not yellow** — the cert lift is in the rod body; overheating the face costs magnetism faster than it saves filing time.
2. **Soft-mount on the yoke is not laziness** — the core has to drop into the gap; final pole lock waits until the armature exists and spins true.
3. **~28 mm pole span @ ~3 mm gap matches the d3951 layout** — scaled from **`PEDAL-GEN-1`** grammar and the bench motor is the same machine class, just grid-fed.
