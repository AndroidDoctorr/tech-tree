### Assistant slate — cobalt and neodymium geography

- **Hatay / Kızıldağ:** local ophiolite and chromite carry trace Ni–Co, but no demonstrated cobalt ore body worth this metallurgy.
- **Cobalt:** nearest credible Turkish targets are **Eskişehir–Mihalıççık** and **Küre / western Ni–Co laterites** — hundreds of kilometres, not a local forage.
- **Neodymium-bearing REE:** **Kuluncak, Malatya** is the nearest serious occurrence; **Beylikova, Eskişehir** is the much larger, better-defined deposit.
- **Extraction gate:** finding REE-bearing fluorite, bastnäsite, monazite, or britholite is only the first step. Separating Nd from neighbouring lanthanides and reducing it to metal remains the real blocker.
