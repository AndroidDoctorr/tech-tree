### Fence (T-2 farm plot)

Afternoon on the plot. You are not planting yet; you are **closing the perimeter**:

- **North and west sides:** Stake row now continuous — willow posts every forearm, driven with rock hammer
- **South side:** Half done — gap where terrain dips wet
- **East (camp side):** Lowest priority; birds come from plain, not camp

You lash **horizontal runners** on the north side with C-2 willow bark — three lines knee to chest height. Not bird-proof; **bird-annoying**. A partridge walks along the outside and finds the gap immediately. You note the south dip for tomorrow.

Fence status: **~70%** — enough to feel progress, not enough to plant.
