### The squeeze, and the call

| Option | Read |
|--------|------|
| **Sow bulk at ~2× rate** | **Covers the beds — but **eats nearly the whole 2.53 kg** and leaves no eating grain |
| **Shrink emmer area** | **Fewer beds of grain this year, seed left in the barn |
| ****CALL**** | ****Both, split.** **Reduce emmer area ~⅓** · sow the remaining area at a **thickened rate** from bulk · put **Emmer elite Y9 ~118 g into a small dedicated seed-increase block** at wide spacing. **Give the freed ground to lentil and chickpea**, which both tested strong and fix nitrogen into the bed for next year |
| **Also** | ****Re-store the bulk bay properly** — sealed jars or a lidded bin, not an open bay. This is a cheap mistake to never repeat |
