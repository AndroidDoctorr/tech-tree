| **Output** | ****~28 m**** |
| ****Problem**** | ****The lay is uneven** **—** **ROPE-LAY-CLEAN MISS.** Soft spots where the strands took unequal twist |
| ****Why**** | ****I was laying heavier rope than I usually make**, and laying it **by hand off pegs**. Light cordage forgives that. **Heavy rope does not** — with three fat strands you cannot keep the tension even across all three by feel |
| ****The real fix**** | ****A ROPEWALK.** Fixed length · a **whirler** turning all three strands together · and a grooved ****TOP**** *(a cone with three grooves)* riding back along the lay, **holding the strands apart at a fixed angle so the twist can only go in evenly.** That top is the whole trick — **it is why rope is made in a long shed and not on a bench** |
| **Downrated to** | ****LASH CLASS.** Perfectly good for cart lashings, baskets, ties |
| ****Not for**** | ****Load-bearing lines.** No fixed line, no climb, no lift — **I do not trust a soft spot with weight on it** |
