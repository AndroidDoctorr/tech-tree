#### Step 2 · boil-down · **BOILOVER-SCORCH HIT**

| What happened | Notes |
|---------------|-------|
| ****Boiled it too hard and too far**** | **It **crept up and over**, and what stayed in the pot **scorched brown** on the hot side |
| **Loss** | **Maybe a third of the batch, and the rest **fouled with burnt organics**|
| ****Second boil-over in three days**** | ****Cheese d3219, liquor d3222.** I am making the same mistake twice: **cooking to the endpoint instead of stopping short of it** |
| ****The right method**** | ****Boil only to first crust, then pull it and let it CRYSTALLISE AS IT COOLS.** Nitre's solubility falls steeply as the liquor cools, so cooling drops nitre out while common salt stays dissolved — **cooling is a separation, boiling to dryness throws everything down together and ruins the sort** |
| **Redo** | **Second run done properly · pale crystals recovered |

#### Step 3 · **the coal test**

| Test | Result |
|------|--------|
| **Pinch of crystals onto **glowing charcoal** | ****A feeble sputter. No flare.**** |
| **What nitre does** | ****Nitre deflagrates** — it hisses, flares yellow-white, and drives the coal to a fierce glow. There is no mistaking it |
| **Read** | ****This is not nitre**, or the nitre in it is a trace riding on something else |

#### Step 4 · **the glowing-splint test** *(the one that settled it)*

| Test | Result |
|------|--------|
| **Heated a pinch hard on a shard and held a **glowing splint** in the fume | ****Nothing. It stayed dull.**** |
| ****Why this is decisive**** | ****I have spent a fortnight making oxygen and I know exactly what it does to a glowing splint — it relights it instantly.** A **nitrate gives up oxygen when you heat it**. This gave up none. **There is no nitrate here worth the name** |
| **Note** | ****Knowledge from the gas rig just paid for itself in a completely unrelated assay.** That is the whole argument for building tools before you have a use for them |

#### Step 5 · ash swap · **ASH-SWAP-CLEAN HIT**

| Work | Result |
|------|--------|
| **Ran it anyway on fresh liquor with wood-ash lye | ****Heavy white curd dropped out**, filtered **clean and fast** |

| **Read** | **The swap **worked** — the method is sound and I now know how to run it. **The feedstock is simply wrong** |
