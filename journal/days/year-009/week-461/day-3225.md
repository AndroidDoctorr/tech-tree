## PLASTER-PURIFY-1

| Step | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Hand-sort** | **Obvious clay skins and dirty faces knocked off first — **cheapest purification there is** |
| **Crush** | **Soft enough to crush with a wooden maul. **No forge work needed** |
| ****Wash — settle in stages**** | **Slurried in a tub, swirled, **let it stand a few heartbeats**: ****the grit and sand drop first**** → poured the cloudy liquor off them → **let that stand much longer** so the gypsum settled → ****poured off the last thin clay water, which never settles**** |
| **Why it works** | ****Three things, three settling speeds.** Sand drops at once, gypsum drops slowly, **clay effectively never does**. Time is the sieve |
| **Loss** | **Slight — gypsum dissolves a little. **Not enough to care about** |
| **Dry · grind · sieve** | **Through fine linen |
| ****Yield**** | ****~24 kg clean white powder** from ~31 kg raw |

## PLASTER-BURN-LADDER-1 · **HIT**

**No thermometer. So: three small batches, same pan, pulled at three different times, and test-set each one.**

| Batch | Pulled | Result |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **A** | ****While still steaming hard**** | **Sets, but **slow and weak** — gritty, and part of it is clearly still raw stone |
| ****B**** | ****Just after it stopped**** | ****Fast, hard, clean set.** ****THE WINDOW**** |
| **C** | **Held well past | ****Dead.** Will not set at all — confirms the d3223 control |

| ****The read that makes this repeatable**** | |
|---|---|
| ****It BOILS while it calcines.**** | **The powder in the pan **churns and bubbles like a pot** as the water leaves it. It looks exactly like boiling, and it **is** — that is the water coming out |
| ****When the boiling stops, it is done.**** | ****Count a hundred, pull the pan.** No thermometer required, and I will never need one for this** |
| **Heat** | **Modest. **A good bread-oven fire, nowhere near a lime burn** |

### And a storage rule I nearly got wrong

> ****Calcined plaster does not keep.** It takes water back out of the damp air and goes stale and lumpy. ****STORE THE STONE, BURN SMALL BATCHES AS NEEDED.**** I calcined **~3 kg** today, not twenty-four.

## PLASTER-TESTS-1

| Test | Result |
|------|--------|
| ****Gauging**** | ****Plaster INTO water, not water into plaster.** Sifted it in until it **islanded** at the surface, let it slake a minute, *then* stirred. Water-into-powder gives lumps every time |
| **Working time** | ****~12 minutes** neat, from stir to unworkable |
| ****It gets WARM**** | **The bowl **heated in my hands** as it went off. Not warm from the fire — **warm from setting**. Whatever the reaction is, **it gives heat back** |
| ****It EXPANDS**** | **Poured into a snug wooden ring and it ****gripped so hard I had to split the ring to free it****. It does not shrink as it sets — **it swells slightly** |
| ****Why that matters enormously**** | ****A material that swells into a mould pushes itself into every corner and reproduces fine detail.** Clay shrinks and pulls away. ****This is why plaster will be the casting material**** |
| ****RE-TEMPERING KILLS IT**** | **Stirred one batch to loosen it once it began to stiffen. It **loosened** — and then set **weak, chalky, crumbling**. ****Permanently ruined. Once it starts, you cannot go back.**** Mix only what I can place |
| **Accelerator** | **A pinch of **old set plaster, ground** → noticeably faster |
| ****Retarder — WHEY**** | ****Gauged a batch with whey instead of water: working time out to ~25 minutes**, and the set is sound **—** **WHEY-RETARDER-WORKS HIT** |
| ****WHEY-USE-1 — partly answered**** | ****I have 6.7 L of whey I could not find a use for, and it turns out to be a plaster retarder.** Not a big draw, but it is **a real use for a waste stream** |

## PLASTER-WALL-SWATCH-1

### Attempt 1 · **failed, and the failure was the lesson**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Did** | **Scratch-keyed a **dry** interior wall in the craft wing, laid a thin coat |
| **Result** | ****Went chalky.** Brushed off under a thumb like flour. **Would not harden** |
| ****Diagnosis**** | ****The wall DRANK the water before the plaster could take it back.** ****Plaster must SET, not DRY.**** It hardens by **absorbing its water again**, not by losing it — **take the water away and there is no set at all, only powder** |

### Attempt 2 · **✓ WALL-SWATCH-SET HIT**

| Step | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Wall** | ****Dampened first**, until it stopped drinking |
| **Key** | **Scratch-scored |
| **Gauge** | ****Whey-gauged** for working time |
| **Screed** | ****×2 batten guides**, straightedge run between them |
| **Swatch** | ****~0.4 × 0.6 m** |
| **Result** | ****Flat. Hard. Smooth enough to burnish with a wet float.** Warm to the hand while it went off |
| **Restated** | ****Interior only. Rain will soften it back.** |
