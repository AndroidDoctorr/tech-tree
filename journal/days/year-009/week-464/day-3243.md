## THE ACTUAL PROBLEM · **a box is not a transfer**

**Two new boxes and two skeps full of bees who have no reason to move. **Building the hive was the easy half.

### Options I weighed

| Method | Read |
|--------|------|
| **Cut-out** — slice the combs out, tie them under bars | **Works, but it is violent.** Skep comb is **curved to the basket** and ties badly, brood gets crushed, and I would be doing it twice |
| **Wait for them to swarm into it** | **Free, but **I do not control it and it leaves the skep colony where it is |
| **Let them migrate over a season** | **Gentlest and slowest. **No** — I want the wax this year** |
| **DRIVING *(drumming)*** | **This is the one.** And it is **exactly what skep beekeepers did**, because it is the only way to get bees out of a basket without wrecking it |

### DRIVING · **how it actually goes**

| Step | |
|------|---|
| **1** | **Invert the skep** and set it in a tub so it sits steady |
| **2** | **Fit an empty container mouth-to-mouth on top**, cloth-tied round the joint so there is no gap |
| **3** | **Drum steadily on the sides of the LOWER basket with both hands.** Not hard — **rhythmic and continuous** |
| **4** | **They march UP.** It takes **fifteen or twenty minutes** and there is a moment where the roar changes and you can **hear** them going |
| **5** | **Watch the joint for the **queen passing |

**DRUM-DRIVE-SUCCESS HIT.** **They went up. **QUEEN-CAME-UP HIT — saw her cross.

### Then hived them on bars

**Shook the driven cluster into **TOP-BAR-HIVE-5**, follower board pulled in tight to their size, entrance reduced. **HIVE-1 is now a top-bar colony.
