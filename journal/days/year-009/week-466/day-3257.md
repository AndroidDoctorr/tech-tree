## ★★ WHY A COLONY SWARMS — and why I have been thinking about it backwards

I had been filing swarming as **a problem, a failure, something gone wrong that should be prevented.** It is none of those.

> ★★ **SWARMING IS REPRODUCTION AT THE LEVEL OF THE COLONY.** The colony — not the bee — is the organism. A worker is a cell of it; a swarm is how the organism has **offspring.** **It is not a disease and I cannot abolish it. I can only steer it.**

**And that changes the goal.** I am not trying to stop them reproducing. **I am trying to make sure that when they do it, it happens into a box I own rather than into a tree I will never find.**

### The two things that actually pull the trigger

| Trigger | What it is |
|---|---|
| **CONGESTION** | **No empty cells. The nest gets **backfilled with nectar, the queen runs out of places to lay, and a queen with nowhere to lay is a queen the colony no longer needs in one piece |
| **QUEEN-SUBSTANCE DILUTION** | **Her scent is passed bee to bee, mouth to mouth. In a crowded colony the ones at the edges **stop receiving enough of it** — and that shortfall *is* the signal that there is **too much colony per queen |

**Both are density. Both are answered with SPACE.** That is almost the entire art.

## The afternoon window — HIVE-3 open

It cleared about the eighth hour and went still and genuinely warm. **Smoker lit, but barely used — a strong calm colony does not need punishing.**

### 1 · The tie test — and my own date was right

The linen cradles went on **d3252** and are due out **~d3259**. I did not cut them. **I tested one.**

> ★ **TEST ONE, DO NOT CUT ALL.** Lift a bar slowly and see whether the comb hangs on its own. If it holds, the rest probably hold. If it sags, put it straight back down and come back.

**It sagged.** Five days is not enough — **the comb is tacked to the bar but not welded to it.** **Ties stay. d3259 stands.*** Two combs at the warm centre of the nest *had* welded and I took those ties out while I was in there.

> **The window is narrow at both ends: **too early and the comb tears off the bar; too late and they have propolised the linen into the comb and I am cutting glued cloth out of brood.

### 2 · Queen cells — and the distinction that would have cost me a colony

Along the bottom edges of three combs: **queen CUPS.** Little downward-facing acorn shells of wax.

**My first instinct was to scrape them off. That instinct was wrong twice over.**

> ★★ **AN EMPTY CUP MEANS NOTHING.** Colonies keep cups standing all season the way I keep a spare bar set at the craft wing. **A cup is not a decision.**
>
> **A cup with an EGG in it, or a larva floating in white jelly, IS a decision — and from that point I have about EIGHT OR NINE DAYS before they go.**

**I turned every comb to the light and checked every cup. All dry. All empty.** **HIVE-3 has not decided. I am AHEAD of it, which is the only useful place to be.**

### ★★ And the part I must never get wrong: read the COUNT and the POSITION before the knife

| | Where | How many | What it means | If I destroy them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SWARM cells** | **Along the BOTTOM edges** | **Many** | **The colony is **dividing** | **I have delayed them, badly |
| **SUPERSEDURE cells** | **On the FACE of the comb** | **One or two** | **The colony is **replacing a failing queen** it knows more about than I do | ☠ **I HAVE KILLED THE COLONY.** I removed its only plan** |
| **EMERGENCY cells** | **Mid-face, hanging oddly off a converted worker cell | **Several | **They have **already lost her** | **Same — fatal |

> ☠ **TWO CELLS ON THE FACE IS NOT A SWARM. Scraping those is how a beekeeper murders a hive while believing he is managing it.** Count them and look at *where they hang* before anything sharp goes near.

## ★★ GIVING SPACE — and space in the wrong place is not space

This was the day's actual work, and there is a trick to it.

1. **Follower board pulled back, three empty bars added.** Room to grow into.
2. ★ **THE BARS WENT AT THE EDGE OF THE BROOD NEST — NOT AT THE FAR END OF THE BOX.*** *Bees will not cross a wall of sealed honey to go find a building site.* **Empty room behind the stores reads to them as no room at all.** An open box can swarm from congestion.
3. ★★ **ONE EMPTY BAR PUSHED IN BETWEEN TWO DRAWN BROOD COMBS.*** A hole in the middle of the nest reads as **unfinished work** — and the urge to fill it competes directly with the urge to leave. **They cannot both be the priority.**
   - ⚠ **Only in a STRONG colony and only in WARM weather.** In a weak one, or ahead of a cold snap, I have split the cluster in half and chilled the brood. **HIVE-3 is the only colony here strong enough for it and today was the only weather for it.**

> ★★ **THE ENTIRE VALUE OF THIS IS THAT IT WAS DONE TODAY AND NOT IN TEN DAYS.** **Space given BEFORE they want it prevents swarming. Space given AFTER the first egg goes in a cup does almost nothing** — the decision is already made and they will leave out of a half-empty box.

### How the split works — and why it is almost a trick

- **The NEW box goes on the OLD SITE.** The old hive gets shifted aside.
- Queen + a comb of stores + empty bars into the new box.
- ★★ **Flying bees navigate back to a PLACE, not to a box.** So the whole foraging force pours into the new hive and joins the queen — **which is precisely what a swarm IS: the old queen plus the flying workers.** They get the experience without the flight.
- **The old hive, moved aside, keeps **the brood, the nurse bees and the queen cells** and raises itself a new queen. **Reduce it to one good cell or it throws casts.
- Having lost its fliers, the parent has neither the numbers nor the appetite to swarm again.

> ★★ **AND I CANNOT DO ANY OF THIS FROM A BASKET.** A skep colony that decides to swarm simply swarms, and I stand in the yard and watch half of it leave. **Every single tool on this ladder exists because the combs come out. The conversion keeps paying.**

## Discoveries

1. ★★ **SWARMING IS REPRODUCTION, NOT FAILURE.** The colony is the organism; the swarm is its offspring. **Unstoppable — only steerable.** Goal is not prevention, it is **making them reproduce into a box I own.**
2. ★★ **DRONES ARE A CALENDAR, AND IT READS TWO WAYS AT ONCE.** Drones flying = swarm season live (**warning at a strong hive**) = mating possible (**hope at a virgin's hive**). Same sight, opposite meanings, one row of boxes.
3. ★★ **AN EMPTY QUEEN CUP IS NOT A DECISION. AN EGG IN ONE IS — and it starts an 8–9 day clock.** Scraping dry cups is worry, not management.
4. ☠ **COUNT AND POSITION BEFORE THE KNIFE. MANY along the BOTTOM = swarm. ONE OR TWO on the FACE = supersedure — and destroying those KILLS THE COLONY**, because I have removed its only plan for replacing a queen it already knows is failing.
5. ★★ **SPACE IN THE WRONG PLACE IS NOT SPACE.** Bees will not cross a honey barrier to find room. **Empty bars go at the EDGE OF THE NEST.** A half-empty box can swarm from congestion.
6. ★★ **AN EMPTY BAR INSIDE THE NEST READS AS UNFINISHED WORK** and competes with the urge to leave — **strong colonies and warm weather only, or I have chilled the brood.**
7. ★★ **TIMING IS THE WHOLE INTERVENTION. Space BEFORE = prevention. Space AFTER = nothing.** Same act, opposite value, ten days apart.
8. ★★ **CUTTING CELLS IS THE WORST RUNG ON THE LADDER** — delay dressed as a cure, with queenlessness as the failure mode. **The reliable answer is to SWARM THEM MYSELF.**
9. ★★ **FLYING BEES NAVIGATE TO A SITE, NOT TO A BOX.** That single fact is the whole mechanism of the artificial swarm — put the new box on the old stand and the foraging force delivers itself.
10. ★ **OPEN ON THE COLONY'S TERMS OR NOT AT ALL.** The doorstep hour cost nothing and carried most of today's information; the lid only confirmed it.
11. ★ **TEST ONE, DO NOT CUT ALL.** Comb was tacked but not welded at 5 days — **my d3259 estimate was right and I proved it without risking seven combs.**
12. ★★ **HIVE-7 LAID IN 4 DAYS; HIVE-5 TOOK A FORTNIGHT.** One variable — **a box furnished before they arrived.** The doctrine is now measured, not believed.
13. ⚠ **A METHOD WITHOUT THE FURNITURE IS NOT A CAPABILITY.** I know exactly how to split a swarming colony and own nothing to split it into.
