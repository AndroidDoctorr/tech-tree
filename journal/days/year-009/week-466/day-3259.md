## ★★ SLATE — purity, and what a purity claim actually costs

Sat down with the cathode plate and asked whether it is the purest thing I own. **The answer is interesting in a way I did not expect.**

### The cell is a purifier, not just a producer

> ★★ **Electrodeposition is SELECTIVE.** Every metal has its own voltage at which it will come out of solution. **Iron, zinc, nickel and lead all cost MORE than copper does** — so as long as I do not overdrive the cell, **they stay in the liquor and only copper lands on the plate.** The bath can be filthy and the deposit still clean.

**And the contamination that does get through is not chemical — it is mechanical.** Liquor and fine solids **trapped inside a deposit that grew too fast.**

> ★★ **WHICH MEANS THE SAME KNOB CONTROLS QUALITY AND PURITY. Low current density gives a coherent plate AND a clean one, for the same reason: a slow deposit has time to grow dense instead of swallowing the bath as it goes.** Sponge is dirty *because* it is sponge.

### Two completely different roads to a pure element

| | |
|---|---|
| **Selective deposition** | The cell. **Sorts by the voltage a metal costs** |
| **Selective vaporisation** | Distillation. **Sorts by boiling point** — and **mercury is the one metal that distils at a fire I can build**, which is why the mercury is probably purer than the copper |

**Honest count of what I could actually call an element in a vial today: copper, mercury, and sulfur if I sublime it. Three, not six.** Hydrogen, oxygen and chlorine are all within reach and all wait on the same thing — **current, and in one case voltage.**

### ★★ And the part that stopped me: I cannot currently PROVE any of it

> ★★ **"Over 99% pure" is a MEASUREMENT, not a description of a process.** I have been about to write a label that asserts something I have no way to check. **A careful process is evidence. It is not an assay.**

**Except — I built the assay a fortnight ago and did not notice.**

| Assay | Instrument | Sensitivity |
|---|---|---|
| **Density** | Balance + water displacement | Good. Catches alloy and porosity |
| ★★ **RESISTIVITY** | **SLIDE-WIRE-BRIDGE-1** | **Brutal. Copper's conductivity collapses with impurity far faster than its density moves** — a fraction of a percent of the wrong element is plainly visible |

> ★★ **I already established that resistivity is a fingerprint and that an alloy conducts worse than either parent. Turn that around and it is a PURITY METER.** The bridge was built to measure resistance. **It turns out to measure how good my metallurgy is.**

**So the museum label carries the number and the method, not the adjective.** *Cu · density x · resistivity y · electrowon at low current density, d3258.* **That is a claim. "Pure copper" is a boast.**

### ★★ THE PERIODIC TABLE IS NOT A DISPLAY CASE — IT IS DERIVABLE, AND THE ROAD RUNS THROUGH THE CELL

I filed the sample table as a prestige project for later. **Then I realised the cell hands me the hard part.**

> ★★ **Put two cells IN SERIES and EXACTLY THE SAME CHARGE passes through both.** So the two deposits are in a **fixed ratio that has nothing to do with me** — it is a property of the two substances. **Weigh both and I have measured one element against another.**
>
> ★★ **That ratio is the EQUIVALENT WEIGHT, and equivalent weights are the raw material of a table of atomic weights.** With nothing but **a balance, a clock and a river.**

**The display case was going to be the end of the project. It is the beginning of one.** *Collect the samples because measuring them is the experiment, not because they look well in a row.*

⚠ **Storage is its own craft** — gases leak, mercury is a vapour I must not breathe, several metals oxidise the moment they are made. **Sealed glass ampoules, and I can fuse glass now.** *The capability arrived before the project that needs it, for once.*

## ★★ THE ELECTROLYSIS CEILING — a number, not a feeling

Asked the obvious next question: **can GEN-WW-1 split water?** Worked it against the decomposition-voltage rule from yesterday.

| | |
|---|---|
| Copper out of liquor | **Cheap. Proved yesterday** |
| **Water into hydrogen and oxygen** | **Needs about 1.1 GB in pure theory — and more like 1.7–1.8 GB in practice, because prising oxygen off plain electrodes carries a heavy surcharge** |
| **GEN-WW-1 delivers** | **1.85 GB open · 1.40 GB LOADED** |

> ⚠ ★★ **WATER IS BLOCKED, and blocked by about a quarter.** I would get a flurry of bubbles as the load came on and then **nothing at all** as the wheel drooped under it. **Not a slow trickle of gas — a threshold I am simply on the wrong side of.**
>
> **And I would have called that a broken machine if I had not learned yesterday that a cell has a threshold. I would have gone looking for a fault in a generator that is working perfectly.**

**That converts "more field" from a vague ambition into a target: about 30% more volts under load.** More rods, shorter yoke, or an electromagnet field.

⚠ **And when it does run: DIVIDED CELL, outdoors.** *Hydrogen and oxygen must never share a space. Same doctrine as chlor-alkali.*

### ★★ Which is when the loop closed

**There is a second way to buy volts and I have just started manufacturing it.**

> **More turns on the armature raises the voltage at the same speed.** That was impossible last month because copper wire was the binding constraint of the entire year.
>
> ★★ **THE CELL MAKES COPPER. COPPER MAKES TURNS. TURNS MAKE VOLTS. VOLTS MAKE MORE COPPER — AND MORE VOLTS OPEN WATER, WHICH OPENS EVERYTHING BEHIND IT.**
>
> ★★ **This is the first thing I have built that improves itself. Every other machine here is exactly as good as the day I finished it. At ~40 g a day, the armature rewind is a FORTNIGHT of the river working alone.**

*And for once the fine-gauge trade is unambiguous: I am short of VOLTS, not amps, so the rewind goes finer and more numerous — not thicker.*

## Slate — the wagon cover

**Double layer confirmed, and white is not a coincidence.**

- **The gap is the insulator, not the cloth** — the cloth only holds the air still. **Battens to keep the layers apart; if they touch it is just a heavy single cover.**
- ⚠ **VENT THE GAP top and bottom, inner layer breathable, outer waterproof.** Sealed, it becomes a condensation trap and I sleep under a drip.
- **White for the outer** — it turns the sun away before the heat ever gets into the fabric. **Summer and winter both, which is why it wants to be a permanent second skin rather than a cold-weather extra.**
- *Counter-case worth knowing: a **loose-woven** tent goes **black** on purpose — it heats the top, which drives a draught up and out through the weave. **That is a ventilation engine and mine is a sealed cover, so white is right for me.***

**Queued behind the hemp harvest. ROPE and the new cover are the same cut of cloth and the same summer.**
