### Thyme and rosemary — seed is the wrong route

| Read | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Seed tray** | **Sown anyway, ~4 g thyme · ~6 g rosemary · **expect very little**. **Rosemary from seed is slow and mostly fails** even when everything is right **—** **ROSEMARY-SEED-GERM MISS** |
| **Cuttings** | **Took a dozen off the established plants. **Read poor** **—** **CUTTING-STRIKE MISS** |
| **Why cuttings failed** | ****Wrong season.** In early March the wood is **dormant and hard**. Cuttings want **soft or half-ripened wood**, which means **late spring into summer** — I took them three months early |
| ****The route that actually works**** | ****LAYERING.** Bend a low branch down, nick the underside, **peg it into the soil while it is still attached to the mother plant**, and leave it |
| **Why it is better** | ****It cannot wilt, because the parent is still feeding it.** A cutting has to root before it dies of thirst; a layer has no deadline. **It works in any season and it barely fails** |
| **Done** | ****×4 rosemary · ×4 thyme branches pegged and stoned down** · sever in autumn once rooted |
| **Filed** | ****Woody herbs = layer, not seed.** Retry cuttings in **June** when the wood is half-ripe |
