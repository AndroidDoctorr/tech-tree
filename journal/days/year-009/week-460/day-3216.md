### What I already know is going to happen

| Read | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Habituation** | ****It will stop working.** The rattle line took four days. A figure that never moves will take about as long **—** birds are not fooled by a shape, they are fooled by **uncertainty** |
| **Therefore** | ****Move it.** Every few days, to a different bed, facing differently · **change the tunic colour** occasionally · take it in and put it back out |
| **Layer it** | ****No single device works.** Scarecrow **plus** rattle line **plus** brush over fine seed **plus** the beds being walked by me at odd hours — the combination is what keeps a field uncertain |
| **Upgrade queued** | ****Hawk silhouette on a tall bending pole.** A dark raptor shape moving overhead reads as a real threat rather than a strange object, and it is a half-day of work |
| **Honest limit** | ****This matters for about three weeks.** Once the emmer is up and rooted, the birds lose interest — **the whole problem is the seed, not the crop** |
