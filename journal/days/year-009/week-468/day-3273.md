## ★★★ NICKEL — and I have been walking on it since d3264

**Sat down to plan a prospecting trip for pentlandite or millerite. Then thought about where I would be looking.**

> ☠ ★★ **PENTLANDITE IS THE WRONG TARGET FOR AN OPHIOLITE.** *Magmatic nickel sulfides want a melt that ran out of sulfur to hold its metals — ophiolite magmas generally do not.* **I would be hunting the rare form and walking past the common one.**

### ★★★ The common one is on the surface, and it is made by WEATHERING

> ★★★ **SERPENTINITE IS NICKEL-BEARING ROCK, AND A WARM WET CLIMATE CONCENTRATES IT.** *Rain leaches the magnesium out of a serpentinite hillside over a very long time and leaves the nickel behind in the soil.* ★★★ **THE ORE IS THE RED DIRT ON TOP OF THE ROCK.** **No shaft, no sulfide roast, no sulfur dioxide — a spade and a cart.**
>
> ★★ **And Kisecik is ophiolite. I wrote that word down on d3264 and hauled basalt off it, and the serpentinite was the ridge behind me.**

### ★★★ THE PLANTS ARE THE ORE INDICATOR

> ☠ ★★★ **SERPENTINE SOIL IS TOXIC TO MOST PLANTS** — *far too much magnesium, not enough calcium, and metals in it that most roots cannot tolerate.* ★★★ **SO IT CARRIES A SPARSE, STUNTED, DISTINCTIVE FLORA, AND IT LOOKS BALD FROM A DISTANCE.**
>
> ★★★ **A THIN, SCRUBBY, WRONG-LOOKING PATCH ON AN OTHERWISE GREEN HILLSIDE IS A SERPENTINE OUTCROP, VISIBLE FROM A KILOMETRE AWAY.** **I have been prospecting by breaking rocks. I can prospect by looking at vegetation — and vegetation is the thing I have spent nine years learning to read.**

| What to look for | |
|---|---|
| ★★★ **The bald patch** | **Stunted, sparse, species I do not see elsewhere. Survey it from across the valley, then walk to it** |
| ★★★ **GARNIERITE** | **Apple-green, waxy, soft — it fills fractures in weathered serpentinite.** *A colour that does not occur in anything else out there* |
| ★★ **Red-brown laterite soil over the serpentinite** | **The bulk ore. Dull, and it is the part that actually pays** |
| ★★★ **HYPERACCUMULATORS** | ☠ ★★★ **Some plants growing on serpentine take nickel INTO THEMSELVES to an absurd degree.** **Burn them and ASSAY THE ASH — the plant does the mining and the concentrating, and I only have to harvest and burn it.** *A living ore-sorter* |

### ★★ And I never needed pure nickel anyway

> ★★★ **I WANT CONSTANTAN AND INVAR. BOTH ARE ALLOYS. I DO NOT NEED NICKEL METAL — I NEED NICKEL IN COPPER OR NICKEL IN IRON.** ★★ **So reduce the ore IN CONTACT WITH the copper and let it go straight into the alloy.** *Going by way of pure nickel metal is a harder road to a place I do not need to visit.*

⚠ **Laterite is an oxide-silicate ore: no sulfur to burn off, so it wants high heat and hard reduction.** *Charcoal, a good blast, a long soak.* ★ **Slower than a sulfide, but nothing about it is poisonous, which is the opposite of the Dörtyol route.**

### ★★★ Which changes the May plan

> ★★★ **IF THE KISECIK LATERITE READS, DÖRTYOL IS NO LONGER A NICKEL TRIP. IT IS PURELY THE ACID TRIP.** *14 km on a road I built, against 117 km.* ★★ **Go to Kisecik FIRST and let the result decide how much Dörtyol has to carry.**
