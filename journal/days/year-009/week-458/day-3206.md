| Choice | Reasoning |
|--------|-----------|

### LIMELIGHT-1 · the accident

Held a chip of **quicklime** in the flame to see whether it would take it. It did not melt **—** it began to **glow a white so fierce I had to look away**, and threw a hard shadow of my own hand across the far wall of the porch **in daylight**.

| Read | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **What it is** | ****Lime does not burn.** It is simply **so refractory that it survives being heated white-hot**, and a white-hot solid throws far more light than any flame |
| **Why it matters** | ****This is a light source, not a heat source** — the brightest thing on the campus by a wide margin, and it runs on a pebble |
| **Cost** | **The **gas**, which is the problem *(below)* |
| **Filed** | ****LIMELIGHT-1** · lamp lane · needs a reflector and a lime holder to be worth anything |

### The real bottleneck
