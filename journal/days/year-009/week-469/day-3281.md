## ☠ ★★★ The nitre bed — 49 days, and it did not sit still

### ☠ ★★★ An unturned bed does not stall — it runs backward

> ★★★ **THE BUGS THAT MAKE NITRATE NEED AIR. THE ONES THAT EAT IT DO NOT.** ☠ ★★★ **So an unturned heap does not pause at whatever it had reached — it goes airless in the middle, hands the middle to the other side, and that side takes the nitrate APART again.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **I WAS NOT LOSING TIME. I WAS LOSING PRODUCT I HAD ALREADY MADE.**

★★ **Forked it right out and rebuilt it loose, core to the outside.** ⚠ *Damp to the mark, never sodden, never trodden — and the treading is what closed it in the first place.*

### ☠ ★★★ And the failure was in the one place I cannot look

> ☠ ★★★ **THE CORE WENT ANAEROBIC BECAUSE IT IS THE CORE. AIR CANNOT REACH IT — AND NEITHER CAN I.**
>
> ★★★ **THE FAILURE AND THE BLIND SPOT HAVE THE SAME CAUSE.** *Wherever air cannot get, I cannot get. Wherever water cannot drain, I cannot see. **The thing that makes a place fail is the thing that makes it unreadable**, so the part of any process I can inspect is systematically the part that is FINE.*

★ **And the diagnosis was free — one hand in, one sniff.** *The bed has been telling me in a language I can read the whole time; I just was not standing there.*
