## ☠ ★★★ First: the hole is bigger than I wrote down, and the reason is embarrassing

**Got in the water with the bar and read the hole properly for the first time. It runs further under the footing than the probe said, and it is deeper at the back than at the lip.**

> ☠ ★★★ **ON d3270 I PROBED FROM THE BANK, AND THE PROBE STOPPED WHERE MY ARM STOPPED.** *I did not find the back of the hole. I found the end of my reach, and I wrote that down as the back of the hole.*
>
> ☠ ★★★ **SO THE NUMBER WAS NEVER A MEASUREMENT OF THE HOLE — IT WAS A MEASUREMENT OF MY ACCESS, AND IT HAPPENED TO BE EXPRESSED IN THE SAME UNITS.** ★★ *Which is exactly why it looked like a reading.*

★★★ **The general form, and it is going straight into how I take readings: ANY MEASUREMENT TAKEN AT THE LIMIT OF ACCESS IS A MINIMUM, NOT A VALUE.** ★★ **Write it down as "at least," with the reason.** *"The hole is two spans deep" and "the hole is at least two spans deep, because that is as far as I could reach" are different facts, and only one of them is true.*

⚠ **And I have taken a great many readings at arm's length down a hole, up a flue and into a kiln.** *This is not a bridge lesson.*

## ★★★ Then the filter washed away in about a minute

**Went to lay the fine bedding course first, as designed. Tipped in the first bucket of sand.** ☠ **It was gone downstream before I straightened up.**

> ☠ ★★★ **A GRADED FILTER IS A STACK OF LOOSE LAYERS, AND A STACK OF LOOSE LAYERS IS AN OBJECT THAT ONLY EXISTS IN STILL WATER.** *I designed it yesterday standing on a bank, thinking about what the finished thing had to be, and never once about how it was going to get there.*
>
> ★★ **The design was right and the METHOD did not exist.** ★ *Cost: one bucket of sand and a minute. Cheap, and it would not have been cheap if it had half worked and I had built on top of it.*

### ★★★ So weave the filter instead of pouring it

**Cut brush off the T-2 face and the willow along the bank, bundled it, and lashed the bundles into a mat — a grid, roughly two spans by three, tied at every crossing.**

> ★★★ **A MATTRESS OF BRUSHWOOD DOES EVERY JOB THE SAND LAYER WAS GOING TO DO. It lets water through and holds the fine bed IN, which is the whole definition of a filter — and unlike sand it arrives in ONE PIECE.**
>
> ★★★ **AND THAT IS THE ACTUAL RULE: WHEN A MATERIAL WILL NOT STAY WHERE YOU PUT IT, STOP TRYING TO PLACE THE MATERIAL AND PLACE A STRUCTURE THAT HOLDS IT.**

★★ **Floated it out, worked it over the hole, and sank it by dropping the medium stone onto it.** ⚠ **It has to be woven loose enough to drape** — *the bed is a hole with a shape I cannot see, and only a flexible thing fits a shape you cannot see.* **A rigid panel would have bridged the hollow and left a void under the middle of it, which is a scour hole with a lid on.**
