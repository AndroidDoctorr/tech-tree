## ☠ ★★★ The re-listen — and the reading was measuring the wrong thing

**The job was to confirm a downgrade. `ASI-ORONTES-1` is filed at `BQ ~3.5`, which implies 2–3 mm of deflection under a loaded wagon, and the d2997 listen read ~4 mm — live-wood class on a concrete deck. It has sat unreconciled for 282 days.**

**Ran the team over it loaded, and went to take the reading the same way I took it last time.** ★★ **Then stopped, because of the scour hole.**

### ☠ ★★★ What was I measuring it AGAINST?

> ☠ ★★★ **I LAID THE STRAIGHTEDGE ON THE BRIDGE AND MEASURED THE BRIDGE'S MOVEMENT RELATIVE TO THE BRIDGE.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **A MEASUREMENT IS A COMPARISON. IT INHERITS EVERY FAULT OF THE THING IT IS COMPARED TO — and a reference sitting on the deck goes down with the deck, rocks with the bearings, and flexes with the very timber I am trying to read.**

★★ **So I drove a stake into the bank, well back from the abutment and out of anything the load could reach, and sighted from that instead.**

### ✓ And the bridge is fine

| | |
|---|---|
| **Deck, against a fixed datum** | ✓ **~2.2 mm loaded** |
| **`BQ ~3.5` implies** | **2–3 mm** |
| **Verdict** | ✓ **CONFIRMED. The register was right all along** |

> ☠ ★★★ **THE EXTRA ~1.8 mm WAS THE MEASURING RIG. Not the deck, not the abutments, not the ground.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **AND I CAME OUT HERE TO DOWNGRADE THIS CROSSING.** *I would have restricted loads on the main west spine, and at some point I would have rebuilt a sound bridge, all on the strength of a careful reading taken against a reference that could not support it.*

### ★★★ Two bad measurements in three days, one in each direction

> ★★★ **AT THE CAMPUS BRIDGE THE BAD MEASUREMENT MADE THE HOLE LOOK SMALLER THAN IT WAS. HERE IT MADE THE DEFLECTION LOOK BIGGER THAN IT IS.** ★★ **Neither was careless. Both were careful readings taken against something that could not hold still.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **A BAD MEASUREMENT DOES NOT MERELY FAIL TO INFORM — IT ACTIVELY MAKES YOU DO THE WRONG THING, AND IT DOES IT WITH CONFIDENCE.** *An unmeasured quantity leaves you cautious. A wrongly measured one sends you off to fix something that is not broken.*

### ☠ ★★★ And the old number is not wrong, it is UNRECOVERABLE

> ☠ ★★★ **I CANNOT CORRECT THE d2997 READING, BECAUSE I NO LONGER KNOW WHAT IT WAS MEASURED AGAINST.** *It is not a wrong number I can adjust. It is a number with no meaning attached to it.*
>
> ★★★ **RECORD THE DATUM WITH THE NUMBER, ALWAYS. A measurement without its reference cannot be re-used, compared, or repaired — it can only be thrown away and taken again.**

★★ **`BM-ASI-1` driven and cairned at the crossing** — *a benchmark is infrastructure, and it is the cheapest instrument I own: one stake, and every reading I ever take here afterwards is comparable to every other.* ★ **Same wants doing at the campus bridge and at every crossing I grade.**
