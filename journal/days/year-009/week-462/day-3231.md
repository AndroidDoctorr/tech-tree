| **Note** | **Copper is not steel.** Quenching it from red does **not** harden it — it just saves me waiting. **All the hardening in copper comes from the work, and all the softening comes from the heat** |

### 2 · The draw

| Step | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Bench** | **DRAW-BENCH-1 @ WW-2**, capstan under power · **DRAWPLATE-2** steel-insert lanes |
| **Lubricant** | **Beeswax and tallow, generously.** A dry draw **galls, scores the die, and ruins the lane** — and a scored die marks **every metre that follows it** |
| **Reduction per pass** | **Modest, and no cheating.** Take too much in one die and the wire necks and snaps — **and it snaps in the middle of a long run, which costs the whole length, not just the pass** |
| **Anneal between stages** | **Every few dies, back to dull red.** This is the whole rhythm of wire: **draw, stiffen, soften, draw again.** Skipping an anneal to save time costs a break |
| **Sequence** | **~6 mm → stepped down through the lanes → **~0.9 mm *(lane C, the tested one)* |

## The next problem is not copper · **INSULATION**

> **A coil of bare wire is not a coil. It is a lump of metal with the current taking the short way across.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Established practice** | **Linen thread wrap + linseed-oil varnish**, as on EM-COIL-3 |
| **The issue is not method, it is LABOUR** | **Wrapping **eighteen metres** for a bench coil is an evening. Wrapping **sixty-four is not |
| **Faster grammar to try** | **Varnish the wire on the run** — pull it through a wiper of hot linseed varnish as it comes off the capstan, and **dry it on the reel** · then **layer-wind with cloth or paper BETWEEN layers** |
| **Where that fails** | **Turn-to-turn within a layer.** Layer paper stops layer-to-layer shorts but **two neighbouring turns in the same layer still touch** — **the varnish has to carry that on its own**, so it must go on **clean and in more than one coat** |
| **Test before winding** | **Wind a short test length, then check it passes current end to end and NOT across the turns.** I have a needle that will tell me |
| **Queued** | **CU-WIRE-INSULATE-1** |
