## CAVE-3 — the works held, and the water left a written record

**Bone dry inside. Seed ark untouched, shelf dry, no damp on the wall behind it.** Five days of steady rain and not a drop reached the chamber.

**And the dry walk was better than I feared, because of something I had not thought about:**

> ★ **WATER WRITES DOWN WHERE IT WENT.** Silt lines, a fan of washed grit, scour marks where it ran fast, a dark tidemark where it stood. ★★ **A wet walk shows me the FLOW. A dry walk shows me the RECORD the flow left — and the record is quantitative in a way watching is not, because it marks the MAXIMUM, not the moment I happened to be standing there.**

**What the record says:** the diversion trench carried everything and **ran about two-thirds full at its fullest** — so it is adequate but not generous. The **drip lip did its whole job**, dry stone behind it. **The out-canted steps shed clean.** One silt fan at the trench mouth where it slows and drops its load.

**Actions:** clear the silt fan *(it will choke the trench in two more seasons)* · **deepen the trench a hand's width** while I know the number · ⚠ **and the trench needs clearing as ROUTINE, not as repair** — *a drain that silts up fails at exactly the moment it is needed most.*

## ★★ THE GYPSUM TRIAL — a real effect, and the design is why I believe it

Six alternating 2 m blocks down the lentil run, gypsum and control, laid out that way on purpose.

**The gypsum blocks are visibly different.** Where the bare control blocks dried into a hard sealed pan — the rain crust, the thing that stops seedlings breaking through and sheds the next rain instead of drinking it — **the treated blocks crumble under a thumb.** Open, granular, taking water.

> ★★ **AND THE ALTERNATING LAYOUT IS THE WHOLE REASON THIS IS EVIDENCE AND NOT AN IMPRESSION.** *Six stripes, treated and untreated side by side down one run.* **Whatever else varies along that bed — slope, shade, how heavy my hand was sowing — varies SMOOTHLY, and a smooth variation cannot produce an effect that alternates.**
>
> ★★ **The pattern in the ground has the same shape as the pattern in my treatment, and nothing else in the field has that shape.** *That is the argument. A single treated plot next to a single control would have proved nothing at all.*

★ **I had written that the effect might be invisible here** *(limestone ground already has plenty of calcium)* **and it is not.** **Which says the mechanism is not the calcium — it is the SULFATE and what it does to how the clay sits.** *Prediction written, prediction wrong, and the being-wrong is the information.*

**Remaining reads stand: flowering, and weigh by block at harvest.** ⚠ *The crust is the mechanism. The yield is the question.*
