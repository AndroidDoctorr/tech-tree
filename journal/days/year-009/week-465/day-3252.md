## ★★ HIVE-3 · **CUT-OUT — FIXED COMB TO TOP BARS**

### **Why this is not a drive**

> **HIVE-3 is a **box**, not a basket, and more to the point — **driving abandons the brood. That is the whole cost of it, and it is why HIVE-5 has spent two weeks with no workforce and no wax budget.
>
> **A CUT-OUT KEEPS THE BROOD.** Cut the combs out whole, tie them into the bars, and the colony carries on with its nursery intact. **More invasive on the day, far cheaper over the fortnight.**
>
> ***And brood is precisely the thing I am short of.***

### Method

1. **Warm still day** — cold comb is brittle and cracks
2. **Cut each comb out at the top, whole**
3. **Lay it FLAT on a board to work. Never hold a comb by its edge** — it tears under its own weight
4. **Trim to fit under the bar**
5. **Tie it in with soft linen strips** — cradle loops under and over the bar
6. **KEEP IT THE RIGHT WAY UP AND THE RIGHT WAY ROUND.** *Cells are built with a slight upward tilt; invert a comb and the cells are wrong and the honey runs out*
7. **Restore the nest order: BROOD in the middle, HONEY at the edges**
8. **Cut the ties out in about a week** — the bees will have propolised the combs fast, and left longer the linen gets built in

### ⚠ **Two combs tore**

> **Warm comb is floppy, and that is a different failure from brittle.** I had prepared for cold-and-cracking and got **warm-and-sagging**: two of the heavier brood combs folded under their own weight as I lifted them clear.
>
> **Correction: SUPPORT THE WHOLE FACE.** Slide a thin board under as it comes free — do not let any part of a comb hang unsupported, ever, at any temperature.
>
> **Salvage: torn comb went to the render pot. **+~90 g of comb to the wax queue — not nothing.

### ★ **Queen safe**

> **The one thing that could have wrecked this: rolling or crushing her against a comb I was cutting.** Worked slowly, watched every face before the knife went in, found her on the third comb and set that one aside first.
>
> **This is the risk of the operation. Not the comb, not the bees. HER.**

**HIVE-3 IS ON BARS. Seven combs tied in, brood centred, ties dated.**
