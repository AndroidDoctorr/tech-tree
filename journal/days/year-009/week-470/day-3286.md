## ☠ ★★★ The plants told me before the rock did

**I came to look for a nickel laterite and the first thing I actually read was the vegetation.**

**There is a patch out there — I know it well — where the ground goes thin and ochre and the growth is sparse, wrong-looking, a short scrubby flora that does not match a metre of the country on any side of it.** ★ *For nine years I have filed that as poor soil and walked around it.*

> ☠ ★★★ **IT IS NOT POOR SOIL. IT IS SERPENTINE.** *The rock underneath is ultramafic, the soil it weathers into is loaded with magnesium and chromium and short of calcium, and almost nothing will grow on it. **Only the specialists can live there, so the barren IS the outcrop.***

> ★★★ **THE BOTANY WAS THE PROSPECTING TOOL AND I HAD IT THE WHOLE TIME.** ★★ *I can name most of what grows in this valley and where. A flora that refuses to match its neighbours is a map of the bedrock, drawn on the surface, visible from a hundred metres away, in colour, all year.*

☠ **And it reads as the least interesting thing in the landscape.** *Bare ground looks like nothing happening. It is the opposite — it is the ground announcing its chemistry loudly enough to kill everything that disagrees.*

★ **Serpentine bedrock soaps under a wet thumb.** *That is the field test, and it passed everywhere I tried it.*

## ☠ ★★★ And then the read was right and my conclusion was wrong

**I had the indicator correct and I walked straight past what it was indicating.**

> ☠ ★★★ **I WAS LOOKING FOR GREEN STAINING IN THE FRACTURES AND THERE IS NONE. THERE IS NO NICKEL HERE AT ALL.** *The whole Kisecik hypothesis — filed d3273, carried for thirteen days, and the reason I walked out here this morning — was simply wrong.*

★★ **The ultramafic association I should have expected is not garnierite. It is CHROMITE** — *heavy, black, metallic, sitting in pods in the serpentine, and it is everywhere once I knew to look down instead of into the cracks.*

> ★★ **AN INDICATOR TELLS YOU WHAT THE ROCK IS. IT DOES NOT TELL YOU WHAT YOU HOPED THE ROCK WOULD BE** — ☠ *and I brought the hope with me, which is the exact failure the cheat-sheet doctrine warns about. **I knew what answer I wanted before I arrived, so I only looked in the one place that could have given it to me.***

> ★★★ **ONE WALK CROSSES THE WHOLE STACK, WHICH IS WHY ONE WALK CROSSES NINE METALS.**
