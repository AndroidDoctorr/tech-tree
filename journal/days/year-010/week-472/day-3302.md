## ☠ ★★★ And then the thing that actually stopped me

**A work fire that draws badly is a nuisance. I stand in smoke, I cough, I open a door.**

☠ **A fire that draws badly in a tight room where somebody is asleep is not making smoke. It is making the other thing — the one that comes off a starved fire, that has no colour and no smell at all.**

**I sat down on the hub step and worked out how long that hearth has been lit at night in a room I have been steadily sealing. It is years.**

> ☠ ★★★ **A MORTAL WOULD HAVE HAD A HEADACHE. Nine years ago, on the first bad night, and again the next — and he would have slept badly, blamed the fire, and moved the bed or opened the shutter.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **I GET NO HEADACHE. I HAVE NEVER HAD ONE.** ★★★ **So the one instrument every other human is issued at birth is simply missing from my kit, and I have spent nine years calling that an advantage.**

★★ **It is an advantage against the harm. It is a catastrophe against the DETECTION**, *and I had never once separated those two things.*

> ☠ ★★★ **EVERY HAZARD I CANNOT BE HURT BY IS A HAZARD I CANNOT FEEL — AND THEREFORE ONE I WILL BUILD INTO EVERY STRUCTURE I PUT UP, FOREVER, WITHOUT NOTICING.** *I do not get the warning because I do not get the injury, and the warning was never a separate system from the injury.*
>
> ★★★ **SO ANYTHING I AM IMMUNE TO, I HAVE TO BUILD AN INSTRUMENT FOR.** ☠ **It is the only class of problem where I am strictly worse off than an ordinary person, and it is invisible to me by construction.**

★★ **Lead was the same lesson wearing a different coat and I did not generalise it.** ☠ *I wrote "lead gives no warning of any kind" three days ago and filed it as a fact about lead.* ★★★ **It is a fact about me.**

## ★★★ The instrument already exists and I have been destroying it weekly

**Sat there trying to think what could possibly detect a gas with no colour and no smell, with what I have.**

**Then looked up at the throat of the hub hearth, which is black.**

> ★★★ **SOOT AND THAT GAS ARE THE SAME FAILURE IN DIFFERENT CLOTHES. Both of them are carbon that did not finish burning.** *A fire with enough air makes neither. A starved fire makes both, together, in proportion.*
>
> ☠ ★★★ **ONE OF THEM I CANNOT SMELL AND CANNOT BE HARMED BY. THE OTHER IS SMEARED ALL OVER THE INSIDE OF MY CHIMNEY, WHERE I HAVE BEEN SCRAPING IT OFF FOR NINE YEARS AND CALLING IT HOUSEKEEPING.**

☠ ★★★ **I have been cleaning the evidence.** *Every sweep threw away the reading and reset the experiment, and I never once wrote down how much came out or how fast it came back.*

### ✓ Two instruments, built this afternoon

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚑ ★★★ **`READ-BEFORE-SWEEP`** | **No flue is ever swept again until it is read: depth, texture, colour, and WHERE along the run.** ★★ **And the reading that matters is the RATE — how much came back since the last sweep — so the sweep date goes on the flue in chalk** |
| ✓ ★★★ **The smoke-spot plate** | **A clean cool tile held in the gas for a slow count.** *Barely marked on a good fire. Black in seconds on a bad one.* ★★ **Thirty seconds, repeatable, and it turns "the fire seems fine" into a comparison against last week's tile** |

★ **Kept the tiles. Dated. A row of them on the hub shelf is a record of a thing I could not otherwise perceive.**

> ★★ **A comparison again** — *the d3279 datum rule, and the three-plate method, and the desiccant weighed in and out.* ★★★ **I cannot read an absolute here any more than anywhere else, but I can read THIS TILE AGAINST LAST MONTH'S, and that is enough to see a fire going wrong before it is wrong.**
