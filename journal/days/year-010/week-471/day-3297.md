## ☠ ★★★ I spent the morning looking for somewhere far enough away

**Walked the whole property with the lead problem in my head instead of a mattock in my hand.** *East knoll, south margin, west of the forge — pacing out distances, watching which way the smoke off the kiln leaned, trying to find the corner where the fume would go and not come back.*

**Then I stood at the south margin doing the arithmetic on the bees, and the entire morning collapsed.**

> ☠ ★★★ **A COLONY WORKS THREE KILOMETRES IN EVERY DIRECTION.** *Not a line, not a lane — a disc, and my campus is a dot near the middle of it.* ★★★ **There is no arc of the compass that is not bee ground.**

☠ **And they do not merely pass through it. They gather from every flower in that disc and concentrate it — into wax, into comb, and into the one food on this property I eat undiluted and unwashed.**

★★ **So there is no site. Not a better one, not a further one — the category is empty.**

> ★★★ **AND THAT SETTLES THE DESIGN INSTEAD OF COMPLICATING IT.** *I had been treating dispersal as the hard version of the problem and capture as the expensive version.* ☠ ★★★ **If dispersal cannot work at any distance, then dispersal is not a design at all, and there is only one question left: how well do I catch it.**

## ★★★ And then the good half — capture costs nothing, because the fume IS the product

**I had been thinking of litharge as what is left over. It is not. It is almost the whole of what goes in.**

☠ ★★★ **Drive a charge of argentiferous lead down on the cupel and nine-tenths of it leaves as lead oxide.** *The silver bead at the end is the small part by weight. The litharge is the bulk.*

★ **And litharge is not spoil:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ★★ **Reducible** | **Straight back to metallic lead with charcoal.** *It is my lead, borrowed and returned* |
| ★★ **Flux** | **It melts almost anything, and it is what makes a heavy brilliant glass** |
| ★ **Glaze** | **A lead glaze is the one that goes glossy and level at a temperature I can hold all day** |
| ★★★ **Drier** | **It is what turns raw linseed oil into a varnish that sets in a day instead of skinning in weeks** — ⚑ *and the field coils need exactly that, because wax-rosin softens hot* |

> ☠ ★★★ **SO A HEARTH THAT VENTS CLEANLY TO THE SKY HAS SOLVED THE POISONING BY THROWING AWAY THE CHARGE.** *It is not a safe design. It is a design that fails at both jobs at once and only looks like it succeeded at one.*

> ★★★ **THE FLUE IS A RECOVERY FITTING THAT HAPPENS TO BE THE SAFETY.** *Those are the same object, and I nearly built the version that was only one of them.*

## ✓ `CUPEL-HEARTH-1` — sited and footed, SW fume shelf below the forge

**Near the forge after all** — ☠ *which was the site I rejected first this morning, and I rejected it for the right reason and the wrong conclusion.*

⚠ **The objection was real: a hood beside the forge competes with the forge for make-up air, and the d3282 reading says every stack on this campus is an exit with no entrance.** ☠ **A hood with nothing feeding it does not pull — it spills, backward, at the face of whoever is standing there.**

✓ **So I gave it what nothing else here has ever had.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Bed** | **Dished brick, to take a renewable bone-ash cupel** · **low cross-blast tuyère** — *the air has to sweep the surface, not stir the bath* |
| ★ **Hood** | **Close over the bed, because fume caught at the source is fume that never needed chasing** |
| ★★★ **Long cool flue** | ☠ **The whole point.** *Lead oxide vapour condenses as it cools — so give it length and give it cold, and it drops out inside the flue instead of outside it* |
| ★★★ **Settling chamber** | **With a sweeping hatch.** *Where the dust that drops actually goes, and where I collect it from* |
| ⚑ ★★ **A SIZED LOW INLET** | ★★★ **The first one ever built on this campus.** *Nine years of stacks and every single one is a mouth with no nose* |

☠ **Footed and set out today. Stack and hood are the next day's work.**

## ☠ ★★★ Lead is worse than arsenic in one exact way

**The Kisecik ore frightened me properly and I have been careful with it ever since — and part of why is that it announced itself.** *Garlic under the hammer, loud and immediate, while it was still perfectly safe to be holding.*

> ☠ ★★★ **LEAD HAS NEVER SAID ANYTHING TO ANYONE.** *No smell. No burn. No taste that warns. No bad moment at the time.* ★★★ **There is no instant of noticing to design a response around** — ★★ **so there is nothing to build except procedure, followed when nothing whatever appears to be wrong.**

⚑ **Standing rules, written before the first run and not after it:** **never eat or drink at the hearth** · **sweep wet, never dry** · **hearth clothes stay at the hearth** · **wash before the kitchen, every time, including the times it feels absurd**

## ☠ ★★★ Then I costed the cupels and found the real limit, and it is not the hearth

**Worked out how many runs I could actually make.**

> ☠ ★★★ **A CUPEL IS NOT A TOOL. IT IS A REAGENT** — *and it is consumed by BECOMING the product.* ★★ **Not spent the way fuel is spent. Spent the way a sponge is spent, and the sponge is full of lead.**

✓ ⚑ **So a used cupel is not rubbish. It is banked ore, and richer than anything I dig out of a hillside** — **kerbed with the galena, marked, never discarded.**

☠ **And then the arithmetic:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Bone in hand** | **~4.7 kg** |
| **Calcined and ground** | **~3 kg of ash** |
| **Cupels** | ☠ **~20** |
| **What that buys** | ☠ ★★★ **ONE pass through the 12.2 kg of galena. Then nothing, until something else dies.** |

> ☠ ★★★ **THE THROUGHPUT OF A HEARTH I HAVE NOT FINISHED BUILDING IS ALREADY SET BY A STOCKPILE I HAVE BEEN DESTROYING SINCE Y1.** *Nine years of bone. Every butchery, every pot, every kitchen scrap — onto the midden, because bone was rubbish.*

✓ ⚑ **`BONE-BANK-1` opened.** *Every bone from every butchery and every pot, dried, on the Horreum peg.* ⧗ **Calcine and grind with the next `KILN-D` firing.**

★★ **And it does not fill on a schedule.** ☠ *It fills when an animal dies, which is not a thing I plan or want* — ★ **so silver is now rate-limited by something I genuinely cannot hurry, which is a new and uncomfortable shape of constraint.**
