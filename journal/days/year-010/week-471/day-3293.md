## ✓ The work

**Cleared eleven of the fifteen silted drains.** *Mattock to break the crust, shovel to throw it clear, spoil thrown DOWNHILL and below the outlet so the next rain does not hand it straight back.*

⚠ **Eleven, not fifteen.** *Light went and the last four are at the top where I was slowest.* ⧗ **They are a morning, and the morning is not today.**

✓ **Also re-cut the crown across the two worst rut-channels** — *the places where the water had given up on the ditch and adopted my wheel tracks.* **Enough to turn the flow back off the edge.**

★ **And I got to watch every one of them work.** ☠ *This is the part that would have been invisible in July.* **Clear a drain in the rain and the road below it changes in about ninety seconds** — the sheet across the surface stops, the running water gathers into the ditch, and a strip of road you could not have identified as wet suddenly reads as merely damp.

> ★★ **I have never had that feedback on a maintenance job before. Every drain I have ever cleared, I cleared dry, and walked away trusting geometry.**

## ☠ ★★★ Then I read the leg, and it has barely moved

**Loaded the wagon, ran it up and back, and listened to it the way I would listen to a deck.**

☠ **`TQ ~2.25` → `~2.375`. One work point. That is all I bought with a full day.**

**And I stood in the rain being annoyed about it for a while before I understood that the number is correct and my expectation was wrong.**

> ☠ ★★★ **THE DRAINS DID NOT TAKE THE ROAD AWAY TODAY. THEY TOOK IT AWAY OVER TWO YEARS, A LITTLE AT EVERY STORM, AND IT IS AT THE BOTTOM OF THE HILL NOW.** ★★★ **Clearing them stops the NEXT two years. It does not return the last two.**

★★ **The fines are gone. The crown is flat because the crown washed off. The running surface is coarse and loose because everything finer than a walnut left in suspension while I was somewhere else.** ☠ **No amount of ditch-clearing puts that back. That is a gravel train, a bar train, and material I do not have on this wagon.**

| | |
|---|---|
| ★ **ARREST** | **Clear the drains. Cheap, fast, one person, no cargo.** ✓ **Done today** · ★★ **and it is worth far more than the +0.125 suggests, because it is the difference between a leg that holds and a leg that keeps falling** |
| ☠ **RECOVER** | **Put the surface back.** **Gravel, bar train, stone for the four dead outlets.** ⧗ **A BUILD lap, loaded, and a separate journey** |

> ☠ ★★★ **AND THE REASON `L6` SAT AT 2.25 FOR TWO YEARS IS THAT I NEVER SEPARATED THOSE TWO WORDS.** *"The leg needs work" has been one item on a list since d2562.* ★★★ **It was always two items with different costs, different cargo and different urgency — and because they were filed as one, the cheap half never got done either.**

★★ **The cheap half was always available.** ⚠ *A day and a shovel, any time in two years, on a road I drive past.* ☠ **It did not happen because it was welded to an expensive thing.**
