## ☠ The wheel cannot make a retort, and it took me an hour to admit it

**I have thrown for six years. `POT-WHEEL-2` will give me an amphora without my thinking about it.** *So I went in assuming the forming was the easy part and the firing was the risk.*

☠ **A wheel makes bodies of revolution. It makes one axis, and it makes it beautifully.**

> ☠ ★★★ **A RETORT IS TWO BODIES OF REVOLUTION ON DIFFERENT AXES, AND THE ENTIRE DIFFICULTY IS THE JOINT BETWEEN THEM.** *The bulb throws in four minutes. The neck throws in three. The thing that makes it a retort rather than two pots is the place where they meet, at an angle, and that is the one feature the wheel has no opinion about.*

★★ **And the joint is not merely the weak place — it is the place the fire will work hardest on.** *The bulb sits in the heat and the neck runs out of it to the receiver.* ☠ **So the steepest temperature difference in the whole vessel lands precisely on its only handmade seam.**

⚑ **Threw bulbs and necks separately, joined at leather-hard, slurry and scored, with a fillet worked in from both sides.** *Every retort I have read about is made this way and now I know why they all mention the joint.*

---

## ☠ ★★★ Then I halved the wall thickness

**I had rolled coils for a wall near twenty millimetres. Fierce duty, hot acid, a hard dry heat — make it strong, make it thick.** *That is what I have done for nine years with anything that has to survive something.*

**Stood there with the calipers and worked out that I had it exactly backwards.**

> ☠ ★★★ **STRENGTH AND SURVIVAL ARE NOT THE SAME PROPERTY IN A VESSEL THAT IS HEATED.** *A thick wall is stronger against a blow and far weaker against a fire.* ★★★ **What cracks a heated pot is not the temperature — it is the DIFFERENCE in temperature across the wall, and a thick wall guarantees a difference by definition.** *The inside is hot and the outside is not yet, and the two halves of the same wall pull against each other until one loses.*

★★ **A thin wall cannot hold a gradient. It arrives at one temperature because there is nowhere for a difference to live.**

✓ **Took them down to ~7 mm, even, checked with a pin gauge at six places on each.** ★ **And the same decision pays three more times over:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⧗ **Drying** | ★★★ **Weeks becomes days.** *Thin ware dries all the way through; thick ware dries a skin and traps water under it* |
| ☠ **Firing** | **Trapped water flashes to steam and takes the vessel apart.** ★ **Thin ware has no trapped water to flash** |
| ★ **Heat to the charge** | **The fire is outside and the liquor is inside.** *Every millimetre of wall is a millimetre the heat has to cross* |
| ⚠ **Handling** | ☠ **Genuinely more fragile cold.** *Accepted — I am carrying it twice and heating it fifty times* |

> ★★★ **THE GRADIENT IS THE ENEMY, NOT THE MAGNITUDE — THIS IS THE THIRD TIME THIS MONTH.** *A gradient across a lathe bed is a bend. A gradient across a drying wall is a crack. A gradient across a fired wall is a shatter.* ☠ ★★★ **In every one of them it is the DIFFERENCE that destroys, and in every one of them I first reached for "more" when the answer was "even."**

## ★★★ Four vessels, and I made them all different

**I came in to make four of the same thing so that three could fail.**

☠ **Then I counted the calendar. Form today, dry, bisque, high-fire — that is one cycle, and there is time for exactly one more before the liquor.**

> ★★★ **FOUR IDENTICAL VESSELS TEST THE KILN. FOUR DIFFERENT ONES TEST THE DESIGN.** *If four copies all crack I have learned one thing and spent my only spare cycle learning it.* ★★★ **If four variants crack differently, the survivor tells me which feature saved it — and I only get one round of learning, so the round has to carry the variables.**

## ⚠ And the fine weather is the wrong weather

**Warm, dry, north wind. Perfect for hay and completely wrong for what is on my bench.**

☠ **A thin neck in moving air dries hours ahead of a thick bulb** — *and differential drying pulls the seam apart before it has ever seen a fire.* ★★★ **Which is the gradient again, a fourth time, in the mildest possible medium: plain air.**

✓ **Damp-cloth wrapped, sleeved in a crate, cloth over the crate, in the coolest corner of the storage wing out of the draft.** ⚑ **Necks wrapped heavier than bulbs, deliberately, to hold them back to the bulb's pace.**

⧗ **Unwrap a little each day. Leather-hard ~d3300, bone-dry ~d3308 if I do not rush it. Bisque then, high-fire after.** ★ **Ready well inside harvest, and that leaves ~50 days of slack for a second round.**

> ★★★ **AND THAT SLACK IS THE ENTIRE REASON TODAY WAS TODAY.** *The vessel was never a day's work — it was a day's work standing in front of a month of waiting, and the month could not start until the day was done.*

## Discoveries

1. ☠ ★★★ **A RETORT IS TWO BODIES OF REVOLUTION ON DIFFERENT AXES AND THE WHOLE DIFFICULTY IS THE JOINT.** *Six years at the wheel transfers to the bulb and the neck and not at all to the thing that makes it a retort.* ☠ **And the fire puts its steepest gradient exactly on the only handmade seam.**
2. ☠ ★★★ **STRENGTH AND SURVIVAL ARE NOT THE SAME PROPERTY IN A HEATED VESSEL.** *A thick wall is stronger against a blow and weaker against a fire, because what cracks a pot is the DIFFERENCE across the wall — and a thick wall guarantees one.* ★★★ **A thin wall cannot hold a gradient; there is nowhere for the difference to live.**
3. ★★★ **THE GRADIENT IS THE ENEMY, NOT THE MAGNITUDE — FOUR TIMES THIS MONTH.** *Across a lathe bed it is a bend; across a drying wall a crack; across a fired wall a shatter; across a green seam in a north wind, a joint that opens before it ever meets fire.* ☠ ★★ **Every time, my first reach was "more" and the answer was "even."**
4. ★★★ **FOUR IDENTICAL VESSELS TEST THE KILN. FOUR DIFFERENT ONES TEST THE DESIGN.** *With time for only one spare cycle, the spare cycle cannot be spent confirming a single guess* — ★★ **the variants have to ride in the first firing.**
5. ★ ✓ **Thin walls pay four times and cost once** — *faster drying, no trapped water to flash, better heat to the charge, and only cold handling gets worse.*
6. ★★★ **The day's work was never the vessel. It was starting the month.**
