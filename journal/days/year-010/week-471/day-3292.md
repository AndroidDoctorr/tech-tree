## ★★★ The rain is a surveyor and I have never once hired it

**I went out to read `L6`. What I got was every leg between here and there, for nothing.**

☠ **Because I have been grading this road by how it feels under a loaded wheel** — *deviation, rut, stumble, the pull on the team.* **All of that is real and all of it is a summary.** ★★★ **Water is not a summary. Water finds the actual low point of every metre of that road and shows you where it is, in the open, while you walk past.**

> ★★★ **A ROAD IN THE DRY SHOWS YOU WHAT IT IS LIKE TO USE. A ROAD IN THE RAIN SHOWS YOU WHAT IT IS GOING TO BECOME.** *Those are different documents and I have only ever collected the first one.*

## ☠ The first look at `L6`

**Came up the last of `L5` in failing light and the sound changed.** ★ *Running water, and it was not off to the side where it belongs. It was in the road.*

☠ **The leg is draining along itself instead of across itself.** *Seven kilometres of climb with the water using my wheel ruts as its channel, which is the one thing every cross-drain I cut up here was supposed to prevent.*

**Walked the first four hundred metres with a lamp before I made camp.** ⚠ **Three cross-drains in that stretch. Two are silted level with the road and doing nothing at all. The third is still open and the ground below it is the only firm ground I stood on.**

> ☠ ★★★ **THE DRAINS DID NOT FAIL GRADUALLY. THEY FAILED COMPLETELY, ONE AT A TIME, AND EACH ONE THAT FILLED HANDED ITS WATER TO THE NEXT ONE DOWN.** *So the bottom drain is carrying the load of all three and will be the next to go, and after that there is no drain on the leg at all.*

★★ **That is not decay. That is a queue.** ⚠ **And I do not know how far down the queue has already got, because I have seen four hundred metres of seven thousand.**

**Camped at the split. Wet, and pleased to be here rather than reading about it.**

2. ★★★ **A ROAD IN THE DRY SHOWS YOU WHAT IT IS LIKE TO USE; A ROAD IN THE RAIN SHOWS YOU WHAT IT IS GOING TO BECOME.** *Two different documents, and I have only ever collected the first.*

4. ☠ ★★★ **THE CROSS-DRAINS ON `L6` DID NOT DECAY, THEY QUEUED.** *Each one that silted handed its water to the next one down, so the survivors are carrying load they were never sized for.* ★★ **Failure is not spread evenly along the leg — it is marching down it in order**, and the bottom drain is always the next to go.
5. ⚠ **And the leg is now draining ALONG itself rather than across itself** — *water in the wheel ruts, using my own road as its channel, which is the exact thing every drain up here was cut to prevent.*
