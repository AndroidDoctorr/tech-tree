## Discoveries

| ID | Notes |
|---|---|
| ★★★ **A STANDARD CHARGE STARTS BEFORE THE SCALE** | Three exact masses taken from three different ore streaks are three precise unknowns. **Homogenize first, then divide.** The cone-and-quarter is what makes the balance mean what it says |
| ★★ **THE REFERENCE SAMPLE IS PART OF THE HEAT** | If a later assay says the feed was richer or poorer than believed, the sealed fourth quarter lets every result be re-read. Without it, the charge record preserves numbers and loses the material those numbers described |
| ★ **GEOMETRY IS A REAGENT** | Equal collector mass with unequal surface changes how quickly metal finds and wets the copper. Collector diameter and thickness belong in the recipe beside grams |
| ⚠ **ONE COMMON FIRE REMOVES TIME AND INTRODUCES POSITION** | A/B/C share the heat curve but inherit three furnace locations. The first run records position; a repeat rotates it. Standardization names the remaining confound instead of pretending it vanished |
