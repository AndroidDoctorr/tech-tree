## Discoveries

| ID | Notes |
|---|---|
| ★★ **THE BINDER IS NOT THE BRICK** | The graded grog carries the load before the kaolin does anything. **Particle sizes do most of the binding mechanically; clay only locks the last gaps.** That is why 5% can stand at all, and why adding clay past the needed amount may buy green strength by selling the hot ceiling |
| ⚠ **GREEN STRENGTH AND REFRACTORINESS PULL OPPOSITE WAYS** | `F15` feels best in the hand today, which makes it the easiest formula to choose for the wrong reason. The best green brick may be the first one to soften because it contains the most silica-bearing binder. **Today's handling rank is recorded and explicitly not the verdict** |
| ☠ **THE RAW CONTROL LOOKS PERFECT** | `R10` lifted as cleanly as `F10`. ★★★ **That is exactly why the control exists:** a material carrying destructive bound water gives no warning while green. If it opens or spalls in the fire while `F10` holds, the failed brick proves the dead-burn step more clearly than the successful one can |
