## Discoveries

| ID | Notes |
|---|---|
| ★★★ **THE CRUCIBLE IS THE ATMOSPHERE MACHINE** | Trying to make the whole furnace reducing would spend fuel reducing the walls, exhaust and every leak. **Only the charge needs the atmosphere.** A lidded crucible buys full furnace temperature and local chemistry with one wall instead of two |
| ★★ **HOT BLAST IS HEAT RECOVERY, NOT MORE BLOWER** | The blower sets mass flow. Passing that air through the exhaust returns stack heat before the tuyère |
| ☠ **A VALVE IS NOT A SAFETY IF IT CAN CLOSE THE ONLY EXIT** | Every inlet may be selected or shut. The muffle exhaust cannot. The safe failure is loss of atmosphere control, not pressure |
| ★ **DIMENSIONS TURN WISHES INTO INTERFACES** | Chamber, crucible, tuyère, blast, ports and exhaust now have separate measurable boundaries |
