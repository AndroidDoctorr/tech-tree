## ☠ ★★★ First `ROLL-TEST`, and the instrument was wrong before the road was

**Ramp out, ballast on, cart released on the `L0` strip. Sixty-one paces.**

**Walked up to `L1` and did the same. Seventy-four.**

**Stood there pleased with myself for about a minute, and then looked back down the way I had come, at the slope I had just walked up.**

> ☠ ★★★ **I RELEASED A CART DOWNHILL AND WROTE DOWN A NUMBER ABOUT THE ROAD SURFACE.** ★★★ **`L0` and `L1` are described in my own register as `Rolling`. Of course the cart runs further on some strips than others — I have built an instrument that measures the HILL and calls it the ROAD.**

☠ **One day old. I invented it yesterday, specifically to get my own opinion out of the measurement, and put a different unmeasured thing straight back in.**

### ★★★ And the correction costs nothing but walking back

> ★★★ **ROLL IT BOTH WAYS AND AVERAGE.** ★★★ **Downhill the slope helps and uphill it hinders, by exactly the same amount** — *so the average has the hill removed from it entirely, and what remains is the road.*

★★ **I do not have to know the gradient. I do not have to measure it, level it, or correct for it.** ★★★ **I only have to make it appear twice with opposite signs.**

> ☠ ★★★ **AND THAT IS A DIFFERENT AND STRONGER THING THAN WHAT I HAVE BEEN DOING ALL MONTH.**
>
> | Method | What it does | ✓ **Seen at** |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Comparison** | ★ **Makes the error VISIBLE by putting two like things side by side** | The tiles · the deficit table · this against last month |
> | **Differential** | ★★ **Makes the error READABLE by subtracting the big equal part away** | ★ **The ark jars — 400 g cancels and the beam reads the gram** |
> | ★★★ **REVERSAL** | ★★★ **CANCELS the error outright by making it appear twice with opposite signs** | ★★★ **This. Roll both ways** |
>
> ★★★ **REVERSAL IS THE STRONGEST OF THE THREE, BECAUSE THE OTHER TWO SHOW ME THE FAULT AND THIS ONE REMOVES IT.**

☠ ★★★ **And I have just learned it on a dirt road with a cart, which is not where it matters.** ★★★ **It matters on the lathe.** *A straightedge checked against itself reversed · a spindle read in both positions · every self-proving measurement in a shop that has no master to check against* — ★★ **and I have no master to check against, which is the whole of my problem.**

⚑ **This goes in the metrology notes, not the trail notes.**
