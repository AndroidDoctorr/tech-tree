## ☠ ★★★ What exactly is a 3.2?

**I wrote 3.2 five times today. I wrote 2.85 yesterday. I have been writing these numbers for years, ranking legs against each other with them, building a deficit table out of them, and drawing a diagram off the table.**

☠ **And I have never once been able to say what the difference between a 3.0 and a 3.2 actually is.**

> ☠ ★★★ **`TQ` IS MY OPINION OF MY OWN WORK, WRITTEN DOWN AS A NUMBER SO THAT IT WOULD LOOK LIKE A MEASUREMENT.** *I grade the road. I also built the road. I grade it immediately after building it, while pleased with myself.*

★★ **This is the d3279 datum rule and I walked straight past it.** *A measurement inherits every fault of whatever it was compared against* — ☠ ★★★ **and `TQ` is compared against nothing at all.**

★ **Which also means yesterday's beautiful deficit gradient, and the whole argument that it maps where my laps died, was drawn in units I invented and never checked.** ⚠ *I still think it is true. I no longer think I proved it.*

---

## ✓ ★★★ So make it an observable — and the cart is already the instrument

**A road's quality, for me, is not a feeling about its surface. It is how hard it is to pull a load along it. That is the entire content of the word.**

> ★★★ **SO MEASURE THAT. RELEASE A LOADED CART FROM REST AT THE TOP OF A KNOWN SLOPE AND SEE HOW FAR IT ROLLS.**
>
> ★★★ **A GOOD ROAD LETS IT RUN AND A BAD ONE STOPS IT, AND THE DISTANCE IS A NUMBER THE ROAD PRODUCES RATHER THAN A NUMBER I AWARD IT.**

✓ **`ROLL-TEST`, and every part of it already exists:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The ramp** | ✓ **A fixed short timber ramp carried on the cart. Same ramp, same height, every time** — *so the launch energy is identical and cancels* |
| **The load** | ✓ **A measured stone ballast. Same stones, always** |
| **The reading** | ✓ **Paces to a stop, on a marked strip** |
| ☠ **The rule** | ⚠ **Same cart, same wheels, same greasing, same day if possible** — *because I am comparing legs to each other, not measuring an absolute* |

> ★★ **Comparison again. Sixth time this month, and by now I should be starting there rather than arriving there.**

★ **It does not tell me what a leg IS. It tells me, honestly and without my opinion in it, whether `L2` is better than `L4` and whether either is better than it was last year.** ★★★ **That is the whole job.**

⚑ **Marked a 40-pace test strip on each of the five legs before I left it, cairned at both ends.** ⧗ **First full `ROLL-TEST` set on the next pass — and every `TQ` in the register is provisional until then.**
