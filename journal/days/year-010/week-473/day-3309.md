## ☠ The midden does not give it back

**Twelve days of banked bone since d3297 is a bucket. So I went to the midden with a fork, expecting to make up the difference from nine years of it.**

☠ **Nine years of bone is not on the midden. Some of it is, in pieces.**

*Weathered soft and chalky at the surface. Gnawed and carried off. Trodden into the ash layers. The small bones gone entirely, the long bones split and scattered, and a good deal of it simply dissolved into the heap and become the heap.*

> ☠ ★★★ **I HAVE BEEN MOURNING NINE YEARS OF BONE AS IF IT WERE IN STORAGE SOMEWHERE, WAITING.** *It is not lost the way a mislaid tool is lost.* ★★★ **It is GONE, and no amount of forking recovers a thing that has already gone back into the ground.**

★★ **Which is the other half of the d3303 lesson and I had only had the first half.** *I could not feel the nine years going past* — ☠ ★★★ **and now I cannot buy them back either, because the world was running its clocks the whole time I was not reading them.**

## ☠ ★★★ The bone nearly went in wrong, and the reason is the kiln's whole personality

**Loaded the bone trays in beside the retorts and was about to close up when I stopped to think about what bone ash actually has to be.**

★★ **It has to be WHITE. White means the organic matter has burnt out completely.** ☠ **Grey or black means carbon is still in it** — *and I have just written down why carbon in a cupel is the one thing I cannot have.*

☠ **And then: a stoked kiln is REDUCING. That is not a fault, it is what a fire does when you feed it faster than it can breathe** — **and I stoke hard through the whole climb.**

> ☠ ★★★ **I WAS ABOUT TO CALCINE BONE IN THE MOST REDUCING FEW HOURS AVAILABLE ANYWHERE ON THIS PROPERTY, IN ORDER TO MAKE THE ONE MATERIAL THAT MUST HAVE NO CARBON IN IT.**

### ★★★ And the answer was the hours I have always thrown away

**Stood at the door working out where in a kiln I could possibly get heat and air at the same time, and the answer was: not during the firing.**

> ★★★ **AFTER IT. THE COOLING IS NOT THE END OF THE FIRING — IT IS THE PART OF THE FIRING THAT HAS AIR IN IT.**
>
> ★★ **Stop stoking, open the damper wide, and a kiln at eight hundred degrees full of moving air is an oxidising furnace that costs nothing whatever to run** — ☠ ★★★ **and I have opened the damper and walked away from those hours after every single firing for nine years.**

✓ **So: retorts fired on the normal schedule · stoking stopped · damper wide · bone trays shallow, open, high in the chamber where the air moves · left to soak down through the oxidising cool.**

✓ **Out this evening: white. Chalk-white and friable, and it crushes between finger and thumb.** ★★ **The midden bone came out white too, which answers the charcoal question for me** — ★★★ *the soak that makes the ash is the same soak that burns the contamination out of it.*

> ★★ **Third time now that the waste phase of a process has turned out to be a product phase.** *Kiln rejects became the pozzolan. Flue fume became the litharge.* ★★★ **And now the cooldown becomes the calciner.** ⚑ **I should start looking for this on purpose rather than tripping over it.**

✓ **~6.9 kg of bone in · ~4.4 kg of white ash out** — ★ **which is about 29 cupels, or one and a half passes through `GALENA-1`.**
