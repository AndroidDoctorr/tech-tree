## ☠ ★★★ The seal test, week one — and I cannot read my own experiment

**Four jars off the shelf, one at a time onto the beam.**

☠ **Four numbers that are, as far as my balance is concerned, the same number.**

★★ **The jars weigh a little over four hundred grams each. The charge inside is a hundred and twenty. What I am hunting is a gain of a gram or two in a week** — ☠ **and I am trying to see it as a difference between two four-hundred-gram readings taken seven days apart.**

> ☠ ★★★ **THE TEST IS SOUND AND THE INSTRUMENT IS NOT.** ★★★ **I have spent months calling precision a problem about lathes and boring mills, and it has just stopped a seed jar.**

### ★★★ So stop weighing the jar

> ★★★ **PUT ONE JAR AGAINST ANOTHER AND WEIGH ONLY THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THEM.** *Four hundred grams on each pan cancel exactly, and the beam reads nothing whatever except the discrepancy.*
>
> ★★★ **A BEAM THAT CANNOT TELL ME 402 FROM 403 CAN ABSOLUTELY TELL ME WHICH OF TWO NEARLY-EQUAL PANS IS HEAVIER.** ★★ **The big number was never the measurement. It was the thing hiding the measurement.**

☠ **And I already knew this.** *It is the three-plate method — you do not need a flat reference to find flatness, you compare three against each other and the error has nowhere to hide.* ★★★ **Four jars is the same trick with a different noun.**

| Reading | ✓ **Means** |
|---|---|
| **All four track together** | ✓ **No outlier. None of them is leaking differently from the others** |
| ☠ **One diverges** | ☠ **That jar is the leaker, and I do not need to know by how much** |
| ☠ **All four track the OPEN control** | ☠ ★★★ **They are ALL leaking** — *the tie-breaker, and the reason the open dish exists* |

> ★★★ **THE COMPARISON CATCHES THE ODD ONE OUT AND THE CONTROL CATCHES THE COMMON FAULT.** *Between them there is no way for a bad jar to pass* — ★★ **and not one part of it needs an accurate absolute weight.**

★ **Fifth time this month the answer has been *compare, do not measure*.** *The datum, the plates, the desiccant, the tiles, and now this.* ⚑ **I should stop being surprised by it.**
