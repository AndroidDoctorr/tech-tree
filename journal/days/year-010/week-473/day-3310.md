## ✓ Winding — and the hard part was not the winding

**Two coils, one per pole, from the ~600 m drawn while I was away arguing with a ford.**

★ **Tension is the whole craft of it.** ☠ *Slack and the layers shift and chafe each other. Tight and the wire thins and the crossovers bite through their own coating.* ✓ **Found the feel of it in the first fifty turns and then it was two hours of not thinking.**

✓ **Linen strip laid between layers.** ☠ **Not potted, not filled** — *the wax-rosin softens hot, and a shunt field is the one winding that never rests.* ★ **The strip keeps the layers apart and leaves air paths between them, which is what I want a field coil to have.**

---

## ★★★ But the two coils have to MATCH, and I cannot count to fifteen hundred twice

☠ **Two pole coils with different turn counts make a machine with two different poles** — *lopsided field, and everything downstream of it slightly wrong forever.*

☠ **And counting fifteen hundred turns by hand, twice, without a single error, is not a thing a person does.**

> ★★★ **I DO NOT NEED TO KNOW THE NUMBER OF TURNS. I NEED THE TWO COILS TO BE THE SAME, AND THAT IS A DIFFERENT QUESTION WITH A FAR EASIER ANSWER.**
>
> ★★★ **SAME WIRE, SAME GAUGE — SO EQUAL WEIGHT IS EQUAL LENGTH IS EQUAL TURNS.** ✓ **Put the two coils on opposite pans and wind the light one until the beam sits level.**

☠ **Three days ago I worked that trick out for a seed jar, because my balance could not see a gram inside four hundred.** ★★ **I did not expect to need it again the same week, on a dynamo.**

★ **And it is better than counting even if I could count**, *because it catches a turn that slipped its layer, a splice I forgot, and any stretch in the wire — none of which a tally mark would ever have noticed.*

✓ **Wound within a few grams. Beam level.**

## ☠ The fault I would never have found

**Sat back pleased and then thought about what could be wrong that I cannot see.**

☠ ★★★ **A short between two neighbouring turns is not a broken coil. It is a closed loop of one turn, with almost no resistance in it, sitting in a strong changing field** — *which is a short-circuited secondary, and it will cook itself and everything around it.*

> ☠ ★★★ **AND IT READS PERFECTLY SOUND END TO END.** *Current goes in, current comes out, the coil conducts exactly as it should.* ★★★ **The test I would naturally reach for cannot see the fault at all.**

★★ **Which is the immunity problem in a different coat** — *a failure with no symptom until it is a fire.*

✓ **So test them against each other.** ⚑ **Same current through each in turn from `DANIELL-CELL-1`, and compare how hard each one pulls a needle.** ✓ **Matched. Neither is the odd one out.**

★★★ **Comparison, differential, reversal — and today all three of them were the same tool used three different ways, on the same object, inside two hours.** ⚑ *I should stop treating them as separate discoveries.*

**Wheel came to speed. Needle sat dead.**

**Two seconds. Four. Six.**

☠ **I had my hand on the gate, and the thing I was about to do was shut it down and go and look for my mistake.**

**Eight, and it moved. Then it ran away upward and settled hard.**

★★ **The wire, the yarn, the seasoning stone, the vitriol heap.** ☠ ★★★ **Everything I own that compounds looks like nothing is happening right up until it obviously is, and I have now nearly killed one of them with my hand on the gate.**
