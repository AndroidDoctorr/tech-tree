## ✓ Gold

**Panned the gravels below the weathered ground, working up until the colour stopped.**

✓ **Real colour, modest.** ★ *Fine flakes, a few heavier grains, nothing like a nugget.* **Enough to be certain, not enough to be rich.**

★★ **And that is the honest read: this is a place where gold occurs, not a place where gold is gathered.** *A day's panning is a day's panning. **I have never in nine years found anything where the getting was the easy part**, and it would be strange if the one exception were the metal every story is about.*

> ☠ ★★★ **AND AFTER YESTERDAY, THE PAN IS NOT THE SMALL SHARE OF THE GOLD. IT IS ALL OF IT.** *The gold in the rock sits inside the arsenopyrite, and every way of getting it out of arsenopyrite begins with a roast I have just forbidden myself.* ★★★ **So a yield I would have called modest against the lode is not modest against the lode — it is the entire accessible resource, and it grades itself differently the moment the alternative is removed.**

★★ *There is something in that worth keeping past today.* ⚠ **A number only means anything next to what it is being compared against** — *the d3279 datum rule, arriving again from a completely different direction.*

> ★ **Its use here is not wealth.** *It does not corrode, it takes leaf, it joins to glass, and it conducts.* ⚠ **It is a materials problem, not a treasure, and I should stop reading it as the second thing.**

☠ **Worked upstream to where the colour died, and marked it.** ★★ *That boundary is worth more than the gold in the pan — **a gold trail that stops tells you which side of the line the source is on**, and that is the only cheap direction-finding I own.*

## ☠ ★★★ And then the galena at `ZN-PB-MARK-1A`, which I marked in d1651 and never smelted

**Broke and smelted a little as a test, then drove the lead down on a scrap of bone ash — and there was a bead left on the dish.**

✓ **Small. Genuinely small.** ★ *The register already says silver is sparse at Kisecik and the register is right — this is not an ore I would work for silver.*

> ☠ ★★★ **BUT THE BEAD IS NOT THE FIND. THE FACT THAT THE METHOD WORKED IS THE FIND.**

★★ **I have hessite and electrum on this hillside — the minerals with silver in the name — and they are fine, scattered and hard to find.** ☠ **And they are not where silver comes from.**

> ☠ ★★★ **SILVER COMES OUT OF LEAD ORE, AND IT HAS BEEN IN EVERY LEAD ORE I HAVE EVER WALKED PAST.** *`M-16` at Bolkardağ is listed as "galena / electrum class" and I have been reading that as a silver deposit when it is a **lead** deposit that I extract silver from — which is a completely different problem, with a completely different method, and the method is the part I did not have.*

### ★★★ Which turns the far lap from a gamble into an errand

☠ **Bolkardağ is a long way behind the Seyhan crossing and I have deferred it for years, partly because I did not actually know what I would do when I got there.**

> ★★★ **NOW I DO. And I proved it on a poor ore, at sixteen kilometres, with nothing at stake.** ★★ **A rehearsal on bad material is worth more than a first attempt on good material a hundred and seventy kilometres from home.**

★★★ **Note the shape, because it is the exact inverse of three days ago.** *At Dörtyol the near site made the far trip **unnecessary**. Here the near site makes the far trip **worth making**, because it removes the unknown from it.* ☠ **Same move — go and look at what is close — and it can point either way. The mistake was never going.**

### ★★★ And I can already do every step

| Step | Status |
|---|---|
| **Smelt galena to lead** | ✓ **Easy. It is one of the lowest-temperature smelts there is and my furnaces are far past it** |
| **Cupellation** | ★★★ **Melt the silver-bearing lead on a porous bone-ash hearth, blow air across it.** *The lead oxidises and soaks into the ash; the silver does not oxidise and will not soak, so it simply stays — a bright bead left behind on a grey dish* |
| **Bone ash** | ✓ **I have bone and I have kilns** |
| **Air blast** | ✓ **I have bellows, and now a wheel** |

> ★★★ **THERE IS NO NEW TECHNOLOGY ANYWHERE IN THAT CHAIN.** *Silver has sat on the long-term list for years as a thing I could not reach, and the obstacle was never a furnace or a skill — **it was that I was looking for it under its own name.***

⚠ **Lead is a poison and it is a quiet one** — *no smell, no burn, no warning, and it accumulates.* ☠ **Cupellation makes lead fume, which is the worst form of it.** ⚑ **Outdoors, upwind, downwind of nothing I eat from, and the litharge is a product to be collected, not a waste to be scattered.**
