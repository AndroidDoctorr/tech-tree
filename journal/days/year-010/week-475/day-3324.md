## ☠ Test the terminal before adding bricks

**Started with smoke at the cold throat, plenum disconnected. Hood on: a lazy inward drift. Hood lifted clear: the thread snapped upward.**

**Measured the four side openings under the old weather cap. Their total free area was only about seven tenths of the flue bore.**

> ☠ ★★★ **THE FLUE MOUTH WAS VISIBLY OPEN AND HYDRAULICALLY CLOSED.**

☠ **The light north wind on d3314 did not prove that the stack was healthy. It supplied suction across the restricted cap and masked the restriction.** The still day on d3311 exposed it.

> ★★★ **A CHIMNEY IS A BUOYANCY PUMP. IT DOES NOT REQUIRE WIND.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **IF AMBIENT WIND IS REQUIRED FOR DRAW, THE TERMINAL IS USING WEATHER AS A PART IT DOES NOT OWN — AND AN OPPOSITE WIND CAN TAKE THE SAME HELP BACK.**

⚑ **So the job changed before the first brick was laid:** fix the terminal area first, add only enough height for margin, and give the kiln a damper to prevent that margin becoming overdraw.

## ☠ The old crown was not level

**With the hood removed, the stack below still read plumb, but the old cap ring had settled low on one quarter by about half a finger.**

☠ **Extending directly from it would have turned a small cap error into a visible lean.** Height multiplies angular error; it does not average it away.

✓ **Cut back the loose bed, laid a true levelling ring, and checked every new course by plumb line in two directions.**

> ★★ **A TALLER STACK DOES NOT CORRECT THE STACK UNDER IT. IT MAGNIFIES IT.**

## ✓ `KILN-D-STACK-2`

**The build:**

- **One full tied course plus levelling ring:** stack ~3.8 m → **~4.15 m**
- **Same internal bore throughout:** no narrow throat and no broad cooling pocket
- **Inner joints:** thin fire-clay slip
- **Outer bed:** lime-sand mortar
- **Terminal:** masonry supports and reused fired-tile hood, raised until total side free area is **~1.6 × flue bore**
- **Wind symmetry:** equal opening on all four sides; no privileged windward face

★ **The height gain is modest on purpose.** Most of today's recovery came from removing the terminal choke. Another metre of masonry would add weight, earthquake leverage and heat-losing surface before proving it was needed.

---

## ✓ ★★★ The kiln has two pumps

**`KILN-D` already has the `WW-2` plenum pushing through twin tuyeres. The stack pulls from the other side.**

> ★★★ **THE PLENUM AND THE STACK ARE TWO PUMPS IN SERIES, AND THE CHAMBER SITS BETWEEN THEM.**

☠ **If the plenum pushes harder than the stack can exhaust, the chamber goes positive:** hot gas leaks from the door and every joint, heat bypasses the ware, and more blast makes the kiln worse.

☠ **If the stack pulls too hard, it drags heat out faster than the load can take it and admits more oxygen than the atmosphere schedule wanted.**

> ★★★ **HEIGHT BUYS PRESSURE MARGIN. IT DOES NOT DECIDE HOW MUCH MARGIN TO SPEND.**

✓ **Added `KILN-D-STACK-DAMPER-1` in the cool upper course:** a fired-tile slide with stops at full, working-mid and minimum-safe opening.

☠ **There is no CLOSED position.** The minimum stop leaves roughly a quarter of the bore open, so a forgotten damper cannot turn a live kiln into a pressure vessel or a CO box.

★ **Operating target:** enough plenum for the climb, enough stack for the chamber to remain slightly negative, and no more exhaust than the cones require.

1. ☠ ★★★ **THE FLUE MOUTH WAS VISIBLY OPEN AND HYDRAULICALLY CLOSED.** *The old hood's total side area was only ~0.7 of the bore.*
2. ★★★ **A CHIMNEY IS A BUOYANCY PUMP; IT DOES NOT REQUIRE WIND.** ☠ **The d3314 north wind was masking a restricted terminal, not proving a healthy stack.**

5. ★★★ **THE PLENUM AND THE STACK ARE TWO PUMPS IN SERIES, WITH THE CHAMBER BETWEEN THEM.** *Too much push makes it positive; too much pull exports heat.*

7. ☠ **A kiln damper gets no closed position.** *A hard minimum stop makes forgetting incapable of sealing a live fire.*
8. ★ **Most of the gain came from terminal area, so the extension stayed modest.** *Do not buy earthquake leverage and heat loss before the existing restriction is removed and measured.*
