## ☠ It was not loose because it had not been pressed

**Scarified the failed wheel tracks and the diagnosis was visible in a shovel cut.** Large gravel touching large gravel, with open voids between it and almost nothing in the middle sizes.

> ☠ ★★★ **THE FILL WAS GAP-GRADED. COMPACTION COULD NOT LOCK IT BECAUSE THE PARTICLES HAD NOTHING TO LOCK WITH.**

**A wheel pressed the coarse stones downward; the next wheel rolled them sideways into the empty space. More passes on the same mixture would only repeat the rearrangement.**

★★ **“Compact it harder” was incomplete advice.** Compaction can only move particles into available voids. If the grading leaves the voids empty, pressure does not create the missing sizes.

---

## ✓ The repair

**First lap:** local `M-09` gravel brought home through the screens and hammer-broken where the rounded pieces were too large. Loaded as a continuous mix, coarse skeleton through stone dust.

**At `L0-W`:**

1. Scarified the old coarse surface rather than burying it
2. Mixed the new grades through the loose depth
3. Added water in small passes — damp, never slurry
4. Compacted empty, then half-loaded, then fully loaded
5. Stopped when the wheel no longer shoved a ridge ahead of itself

> ★★★ **WATER DID NOT BIND THE ROAD. IT LET THE PARTICLES FIND A DENSER ARRANGEMENT.**

☠ **Too little water and friction stops the grains before they seat. Too much and the fines float, pump and leave the road as mud.** The useful state is damp enough to rearrange and dry enough to carry load.

✓ **At the end, a loaded wheel printed a shallow face and no shoulder wave.**
