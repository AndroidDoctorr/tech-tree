## ☠ ★★★ The wild grain — and the missed marking turned out to be the wrong thing to regret

☠ **The calendar has said for years: mark the stands in March while they are green, because in June they are indistinguishable from everything around them. I did not do it this spring.**

✓ **Walked the plain anyway. ★ The Y9 parent-patch ghost at `T-2` was still standing and it held** — *found `P-04` and `P-06` from it inside the hour.* ★ **So the stands were never actually lost.**

**And then I stood in front of them and saw what late really costs, and what it buys.**

☠ **Most of the seed is already on the ground.** ★★★ **Wild grain shatters — that is what wild MEANS. The head goes brittle at the rachis and throws its seed the moment it is ripe, because a grass that holds onto its seed has no children.**

☠ **So a June gather is a poor basket. I have perhaps half what a timed gather gives.**

> ★★★ **BUT LOOK AT WHAT IS STILL STANDING THERE HOLDING ITS SEED.**
>
> ★★★ **EVERY HEAD STILL FULL IN LATE JUNE IS FULL BECAUSE IT CANNOT LET GO.** *It has a tough rachis. It is the plant that failed at being a wild grass.*
>
> ★★★ **AND A HEAD THAT WILL NOT SHATTER IS THE ENTIRE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A WILD GRASS AND A CROP.** ☠ **It is the first thing anyone ever bred into wheat, and it is the only thing that matters, and the late stand hands it to me sorted.**

☠ ★★★ **I have been gathering wild grain at the right time for FOOD and calling it seed collection.**

> ★★★ **THE LATE GATHER IS THE WRONG WAY TO FILL A BASKET AND THE RIGHT WAY TO FILL A SEED JAR.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **THEY ARE TWO DIFFERENT HARVESTS. THEY WANT TWO DIFFERENT DATES, THEY SELECT FOR OPPOSITE THINGS, AND I HAVE BEEN RUNNING THEM AS ONE JOB FOR NINE YEARS.**

✓ **So I split it on the spot.**

| | When | Take | Selects for |
|---|---|---|---|
| ✓ **FOOD gather** | **Early, at first dry** | **Volume, any head** | ★ *nothing — and that is fine, it is food* |
| ✓ ★★★ **SEED gather** | ★★★ **LATE. Deliberately late** | ★★ **Only heads still holding, stripped by hand** | ★★★ **TOUGH RACHIS — the domestication trait, free** |

✓ **Today's take went to the seed jars, not the food bin.** ★★ **~40 g of `P-04` and `P-06` off plants that refused to shatter, and I want every one of their children.**

⚑ **And the March marking changes purpose entirely.** ☠ **I do not mark in March to find the stand for seed — I have a ghost for that.** ★★★ **I mark in March so the FOOD gather can be timed, and the food gather is the one I have been missing, not the seed one.**

> ★★ **The thing I forgot cost me a basket of grain and taught me what nine correct-looking years had been quietly getting wrong.**
