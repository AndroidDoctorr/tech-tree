## ☠ ★★★ The strength was decided before the interesting part began

☠ **Copperas is not iron and sulfur and nothing else. ★★★ It carries seven parts of water to one of salt, locked into the crystal itself, and it does not look wet.**

> ★★★ **EVERY ONE OF THOSE SEVEN THAT I FAIL TO DRIVE OFF QUIETLY, BEFOREHAND, ARRIVES IN MY RECEIVER LATER AND WATERS DOWN THE THING I SPENT THE DAY MAKING.**

✓ **So: hours at low heat, well under anything dramatic, until the green crystal had gone to a dull white powder and stopped losing weight.** ★ **Watched it on the balance rather than by eye, and stopped when two readings agreed.**

> ☠ ★★★ **THE STRENGTH OF THE ACID WAS DECIDED IN THE DULL HOUR, NOT THE INTERESTING ONE.** ★★★ *The fire afterwards is just the part that happens.* ★★ **I have known this about smelting and about clay and I did not expect chemistry to say it back to me quite so plainly.**

## ✓ The fire, and cutting the run

✓ **Then the real heat. ✓ Sand seal held. ✓ Receiver cool and vented, the rig behaving exactly as designed on paper three days ago, which is not something I say often.**

★★ **First runnings went aside** — *thin, pale, and mostly the water I did not quite catch.* ✓ **I know this from the turpentine at d3237: the first of anything off a still is not the thing you came for.** ★★★ **Cut the run, keep the middle, and the discipline is the product.**

**And then the middle came over.**

✓ ★★★ **Oily. ★★★ It moves like nothing else I own** — *slow, thick, and it clings to the glass and runs back down in sheets.* **The old name is not a metaphor. It genuinely looks like an oil.**

## ✓ ★★★ Proving it, rather than believing it

★ **I wanted a number, not an impression.**

✓ **Filled a small measure with water, weighed it. ✓ Emptied, dried, filled it with the acid, weighed it again.**

> ★★★ **IT IS VERY NEARLY TWICE THE WEIGHT OF THE SAME VOLUME OF WATER.**
>
> ★★★ **That single comparison is the whole proof.** *Nothing I have ever made is that heavy for its size, and a weak acid could not be* — ☠ **because a weak acid is mostly water, and water weighs what water weighs.**

★★ **Seventh time this month the answer has been two things on a balance.** ⚑ *I have stopped being surprised by that and started reaching for it first.*

✓ **Charred a splint black on contact without a flame anywhere near it** — ★ *which is the other thing it does, and it does it to skin, and I did not need reminding.*

✓ **Into `ACID-BOTTLE-1`, grooved stopper, and the groove earned itself immediately** — ★ *came out under a finger rather than a fight.*
