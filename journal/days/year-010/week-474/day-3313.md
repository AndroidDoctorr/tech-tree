## ☠ ★★★ And then the joint turned out to be the interesting problem

**Sat down to design a seal and could not find a material.**

☠ **Hot sulfur fume takes everything organic I own** — *wax, rosin, linen, leather, flour paste, the clay-and-dung lute.* ★ **Charred, softened or eaten, all of them.**

**Chased that for an hour before asking the better question, which was what the seal is actually FOR.**

> ☠ ★★★ **A STILL IS NOT A SEALED VESSEL. IT IS A VESSEL I AM DELIBERATELY HEATING UNTIL IT MAKES GAS.**
>
> ☠ ★★★ **A TIGHT JOINT ON A DISTILLATION IS NOT A GOOD SEAL. IT IS A BOMB.** *Green vitriol does not gently give up acid — it breaks down and throws off a great deal more gas volume than the vessel holds.* ★★★ **If that cannot get out, then what I have built is a pressure vessel with a fire under it and my face at the working height.**

★★ **Every instinct I have says a joint should be tight, and every one of those instincts came from roofs and boats and barrels** — ☠ *things that are trying to keep the world out, not to let a reaction breathe.*
