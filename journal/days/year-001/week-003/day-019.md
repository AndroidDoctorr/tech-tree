### Assistant — grout, high-temp kiln, quicklime

You ask while poking inlet embers.

**You:** Grout for bricks — what do I actually need?

**Assistant:** Three tiers:

| Tier | Mix | Temp / use |
|------|-----|------------|
| **Now** | Clay + sand grog (your brick mix) | Sun-dried or low fire; **works for dry stack, weak if soaked** |
| **Better** | Same + **crushed fired brick** (grog from B7/B8 shards) | Survives moderate heat; **kiln lining candidate** |
| **Best (later)** | **Lime mortar** — quicklime + sand + water | Water-resistant; binds stone/brick for hypocaust & walls |

**You:** Quicklime — how near is "near"?

**Assistant:** **Feasible in weeks to a few months**, not tomorrow. Requirements:

1. **Limestone** — you have pile 7; need **clean calcite lumps**, not mudstone
2. **Temperature** — calcination at **~900°C+** sustained; your pottery kiln **might** hit it at core with charcoal blast — **unverified**; need **thermometer you don't have** or tile melt tests
3. **Dedicated lime burn** — small stack of limestone in hot charcoal pit or kiln vent path; not mixed with pots
4. **Slaking pit** — water + quicklime = **violent heat**; dig away from hut; eye hazard
5. **Time** — build slaking setup, prove temperature, repeat burns

**Near-term path:** Fire **B9–16 tomorrow** → crush one failed brick → mix **high-grog mortar** → patch kiln interior → test higher fire. **Then** limestone trial stack in charcoal pit when store C > **40 kg**.

**You:** Higher-temp kiln design?

**Assistant:** Your egg kiln tops out around **800–950°C** estimate — enough for pottery, **marginal for lime and copper**. Upgrades:

- **Inner lining** of fired grog bricks (the ones you're making now)
- **Thicker dome** + smaller chamber
- **Draft control** — stoke hole + vent plug precision
- **Hypocaust tie-in (v2)** — hot flue from oven **could** pre-dry greenware or warm limestone — **engineering for later**

**You:** Hypocaust brick spec different?

**Assistant:** **Vitrified face** on floor tiles — longer fire hold. Same grog mix, **slower dry**, **higher soak**. You're making the right crude objects; patience is the spec.

You feel dangerously informed. Good.
