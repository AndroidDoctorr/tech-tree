### Shelter planning — hut, not lean-to

You walk the **workshop shelf** (80 m downslope) with thirteen poles laid out like bones.

**Roof span math:** Longest poles ~**3.2 m**. Reasonable ridge hut width ~**2.2 m** interior; length ~**2.8 m**. Rectangle. Not huge — one immortal and piles of stuff.

**Build order** (you argued with yourself; assistant picked):

1. **Layout** — corner stakes and string (reed line)
2. **Post holes** — corners first, then mid-wall; depth **40 cm**
3. **Ridge frame** — tallest poles; know the roof before you go deep on pit walls
4. **Floor pit** — sunken footprint **20 cm** with **step shelf** at east end (3 treads, 40 cm wide)
5. **Walls** — stone + clay mortar, raised over time
6. **Kiln/oven niche** — south wall, **deeper dig +30 cm**; hypocaust floor space **reserved** for v2 (spring; you're not cold yet)
