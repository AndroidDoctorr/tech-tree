## Events

You wake thinking about one thing: **what's under the mound.**

### Dawn — open batch #1

Weir first (habit): **seven fish** — best day yet. You string them on a reed and leave them in B-1 cool pit. Then the kiln.

The earth cap is cool. No steam. You pull clay and dirt by hand, then lever the stone plug from the air inlet. Smell hits: **clean charcoal**, not ash-soup.

You reach into the pit.

| Outcome | Mass | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Good charcoal** | **14.2 kg** | Ring when struck; breaks clean |
| Half-burnt / junk wood | 2.8 kg | Outer edge + one corner of stack; re-stackable as fuel |
| Fine ash | ~4 kg | Scooped to pile 7 (potash future) |
| **Wood → charcoal yield** | **~31%** | 46 kg loaded; acceptable for pit #1 |

You whoop. Quietly. There's no one to hear it except the assistant, which does not congratulate you — it **records the number**.

**Pit inspection:**

| Wall zone | Condition |
|-----------|-----------|
| Red clay base patch | **Intact** — no spalling |
| Grey walls lower half | Good |
| Grey collar upper third | **Hairline cracks** — usable with patch |
| Liner slabs | Fine |

You mix **2 kg** reserve clay with ash water and smear cracks inside. Pit is **ready for batch #2** after more wood and a dried cap. Phase-2 outer ring still waits on clay.

**Charcoal on shelf:** 2.8 kg (test) + 14.2 kg (batch #1) = **17.0 kg total**.

### Evening — sort and stack

Piles look like a hoarder's nest. That's fine. You re-grade chert at the discard ring, stack oak charcoal wood in a **dedicated wedge** beside the kiln (34 kg + 4 kg leftover + 2.8 kg reclaimable half-burnt ≈ **41 kg** staged for batch #2 when you want it). General firewood back to ~75 sticks.

You cook four fish and pick pistachios. Sit on the terrace. The kiln is patched. The shelf is **heavy with charcoal**. You are absurdly rich in one very narrow resource.
