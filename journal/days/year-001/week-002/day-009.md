## Events

Recovery day — on purpose. You are not lazy; you are **tired**, and the assistant agrees that roofing tired is how you fall through the thatch.

### Easy morning — river, weir, nothing heroic

**Weir:** Six fish. Trap intact — heron did not party. You reset one stake without thinking.

**River:** You wade to mid-shin in the eddy below camp. Sit on a submerged flat rock. Water cold enough to matter. Clothes stay on; boots still don't exist; feet appreciate it. Two minutes becomes **twenty**. The assistant stays quiet. You watch minnows and decide not to spear any out of spite.

| Task | Result |

### Afternoon/evening — roof

Thatching north slope from **~1600** until light fails:

- Oak leaf + grass bundles — same shingle overlap as south
- **North slope ~90%** covered; **ridge gap** narrowed to **~40 cm** strip at apex
- **Overall roof coverage ~85%** (south done Day 8 + north today)
- C-1 reed cord: **~4 m** used in tie-downs
- C-5 flax: ridge cap starter strip — experimental

You cannot stand on rafters. Work from ground + **unsafe ladder** for lower north edge only. Upper ridge waits for a real ladder or a very stupid immortal.

**Hut overall: ~75%** (up from 65% — roof gain; walls barely moved)

### Evening

Four fish, snails, sunset from hut step shelf. Structure looks like a **house** from the south. From the north you can still see sky through the ridge. Good enough for a tired day.

Respawn: **still bed pit.** Assistant: "One dry night under **85%** roof doesn't count. Finish ridge + door, then reconsider."
