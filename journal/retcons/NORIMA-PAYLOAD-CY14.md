**Filed:** **d5078** · **Cal-Y14 D94**  
**Cause:** **`HAUL-GRAMMAR-Y14`** lifted sand/gravel/lime off **cart lap** counts but still capped heroes at **~20–70 kg net** while **`WAGON-V2-CHASSIS-1` (Norima)** runs **×4 iron tyres · `ST-SPR` leaf springs · bulk dry bed · iron pintle** on **TQ ~1.9–2.2** corridors. Koruhöyük returns logged **~115 kg column / ~50 kg pozz** — **wagon-limited in name only.**

**Player directive:** **Forward from d5079** — state **net cargo** and enough **days** to **fill** the bed for that material.

## Fill rate — material class

| Class | Examples | Net / trip | Mining days to **full** bed |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **A · loose** | Sand · gravel bar · wet clay | **~140–200 kg** | **0** extra *(same hero)* |
| **B · quarry** | Pozz/tuff · field stone · lime knap | **~160–220 kg** | **+1–2** at face |
| **C · ore** | Galena · malachite · cassiterite — dress · gangue out | **~120–180 kg** | **+2–3** at face |
| **D · timber** | Fell + buck | **~100–180 kg**/trip | Fell day ≠ load day |

★ **Sand:** shovel + sieve can fill the bed in **one campus day** — tow is the cap, not mining.

★ **Ore:** a **full** remote bed is **several face days**; do not credit **~200 kg** after one pick session.
