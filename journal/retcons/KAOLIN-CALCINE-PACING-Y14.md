## What was wrong

Day logs quoted **`TC-PROBE-1` working-mid ~45 min** without stating that this is **soak at temperature only**, not batch duration. A single tray is **~2.5–3.5 h** door-to-sieve (dry-down · soak · grind). **Overlap heroes** (char retort watch · hood commission · nitre leach) leave enough slack for **×2 serial batches** when the muffle is empty between charges — not “one batch = full idle day.”

| Context | Typical cadence |
|---|---|
| **Calcine-only hero** | **×2–3 serial** per day if peg is thin; **×1** if grind/mask fatigue or other PM hero |
| **Char retort interleave** | **×1 AM + ×1 PM** on twin-half grammar *(d4682 / d5214 class)* |
| **Hood / bench overlap** | **×2 serial** · tend bank between holds *(d5275 patch)* |

**Do not** cap at one batch per calendar day on overlap days unless the player defers or a hotter hero owns the muffle all day.
