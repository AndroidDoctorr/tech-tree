# HOME site lock — tributary south bank (Day 1920)

**Authority:** Player sketch + sat-map pin (Habib-i Neccar / Demir Kapı class gorge · future Antakya) · live geography is [map.md](../../map.md).  
**Cause:** Early ASCII and a few plan lines put the **Orontes** at the campus foot (often **south**) and called the workshop lane **downslope south**. The pin in mind the whole campaign is **south bank of TRIB-1**, gorge **east**, Orontes **~1 km west**.

**Closed day files are not rewritten.** Read “river / Orontes at camp,” “terrace falls south,” and “Pine N” in old logs as this lock unless a day is clearly on the **Asi trunk** (ore-trip) or the **coast**.
