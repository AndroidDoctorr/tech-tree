# Cal-Y advance @ 1 Jan — not @ Yule *(player directive · d4621)*

**Filed:** Day 4621 · post review of week 659–660 headers

## Error

**`Cal-Yn` was advanced at Yule (~d4610)** via **`SUN-CAL-Y14-CLOSE-4609` → “Cal-Y15 opens d4610”**, while **prose dates still said Dec Y14** on some lines. That **double-counted the year** (solstice feast ≠ civil year roll).

## Correction

| Rule | Canon |
|---|---|
| **`Cal-Yn` D1** | **1 Jan** absolute · **`CAL-Y(n−1)-CLOSE`** on **31 Dec** |
| **Yule (~21 Dec)** | **`SUN-YULE` / feast / hunt** — **does not** roll **`Cal-Y`** |
| **`SUN-CAL-*`** | Solstice observance only — **not** civil **`Cal-Y`** close |
