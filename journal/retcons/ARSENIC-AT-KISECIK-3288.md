# ARSENIC-AT-KISECIK-3288

**Filed:** Day 3290 review · **Cal-Y10 D145** · **Type:** addition, not reversal

**Cause:** Days d3286–d3290 logged the Kisecik district from the published mineral suite, and **arsenopyrite was omitted.** It is not a trace phase there — it is one of the main ore minerals of the Kızıltepe vein group, and **the native gold and electrum occur as inclusions inside it**. Its weathering product, a pale green-grey arsenate crust, sits on the same outcrop faces already described as gossan.

**Nothing logged was wrong. Something load-bearing was missing**, and it changes both the acid route and the gold route.

## Why it was retconned rather than discovered later

★★ **The Player broke fresh sulfide by hand across two days.** *Struck arsenopyrite gives an immediate, unmistakable garlic smell, and he is a skill-4 prospector with a hammer in his hand.* ☠ **He would not have missed it. Deferring the find to a later day would have required him to be less competent than he is.**

## Doctrine gained

> ☠ ★★★ **`SMELL-IS-NOT-THE-HAZARD` — the warning and the danger are different substances and do not arrive together.** *Fresh arsenopyrite shouts under a hammer. Its roast product has no smell at all.* ★★★ **The ore warns clearly while it is safe and goes silent at the moment it becomes lethal** — the exact inverse of nine years of trained instinct.

**Source:** MTA *Kisecik (Hatay) Hydrothermal Gold Veins* and the 2003 GSA account of the deposit both list arsenopyrite among the principal ore minerals, with native gold and electrum as inclusions in it, plus scorodite among the secondary minerals.
