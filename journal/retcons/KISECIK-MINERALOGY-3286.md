# RETCON — Kisecik mineralogy, nickel location, and the Dörtyol trip (Days 3286–3290)

**Filed:** Day 3290 review · **Cal-Y10 D145**
**Cause:** **`KISECIK-LATERITE-1`** was logged d3286 as a top-grade **nickel** laterite with garnierite-class fracture fillings, which then re-scoped Dörtyol to an acid-only expedition and sent the wagon 105 km west over d3287–d3290. **Kisecik carries no nickel.** The standing d3273 hypothesis that it might was wrong, and d3286 confirmed the wrong thing instead of correcting it.

**Player catch:** *Kisecik should have chalcopyrite, gold, sphalerite, galena, malachite, hessite/electrum, chromite, cinnabar, magnetite, hematite, limonite, siderite, pyrite and lots of serpentinite/olivine. Nickel is further north in the Amanos, near Islahiye.*
