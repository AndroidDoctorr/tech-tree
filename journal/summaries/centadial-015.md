You **are** on **tin geography that is no longer a rumor** — starter wash, not a mine, not a button — the first century where **the metal gap that survived every prior rollup is sitting in the wagon**.

## On playing one day at a time

Centadial 15 is the century of **not pretending a chip is a civilization**: you did not smelt on the slope, and you did not call Kozan a mine. Day 1475 (**first cassiterite in the hand**) and Day 1492 (**turn back from Ceyhan to reload**) are the two character beats — **the find**, then **the refusal to rush the sack**. Day 1500 is quieter and heavier: **two and a half kilos of wash**, **headroom left**, **a kiln you refused to invent today**. The fun at Day 1500 is that **fourteen centuries of “no tin” ended on a collection day**, not a speech. **Day 1501 is more crumbs, or the turn south — both legal, both after the truth.**
