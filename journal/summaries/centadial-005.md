## On playing one day at a time

Centadials are rollups; the game is **days**. This century worked because you kept **one honest primary** per day — cart rim lap, joist row, pitch course, flax break session, kitchen sill — and filed the rest as maintenance. The fun at Day 500 is not nostalgia for Day 1; it is **compound interest**: every rung still feels small, but the campus now has barn, cart, crane, wheel, dyes, and a kitchen wing rising off the same respawn bed. **Day 501 opens Centadial 6.**
