## On playing one day at a time

Centadial 10 is the century of **finishing the machine Centadial 9 only started**: you did not leave **~2.24 kg** sitting as a souvenir bloom, and you did not pretend a **bond beam** is a **roof**. Day 968 (**first clang · Cal-Y4**) and Day 995 (**tower weathertight**) are the two character beats — **hit metal with a machine**, then **walk store to lab under tile**. Day 1000 is quieter: **lane green**, **seventy-seven unfired brick**, **wood pile thin**. The fun at Day 1000 is that the milladial ends on a **factory combo day**, not a feast — the campus is live enough that tomorrow is just **fire the kiln**. **Day 1001 opens the brick-and-wing runway of Week 143's close.**
