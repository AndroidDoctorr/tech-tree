You **are** on a **live west bedroom**, a **live south kitchen**, a **dead first house**, and an **east bath that is a void with three honest courses** — the first century where **the villa is home, and the hut is salvage**.

## On playing one day at a time

Centadial 12 is the century of **moving house in the right order**: you did not demo **H-v2** until the west cell passed drip and the bed was in it, and you did not load **wythe course 5** on a cheek that had not cured. Day 1114 (**first night in the villa**) and Day 1164 (**last look at the hut**) are the two character beats — **a new respawn**, then **permission to take the old one apart**. Day 1200 is quieter: **thirty-six pilae brick**, **cross-draft clean**, **suspensura still a slate**. The fun at Day 1200 is that **home is no longer the milladial hut** and the next honest job is still **one more course in a hole**. **Day 1201 is pilae four, then the floor that hangs on them.**
