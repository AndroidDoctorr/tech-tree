## One paragraph

You woke alone on a terrace with soft hands and a talking assistant. Three thousand days later you have a **courtyard Domus you sleep in**, a **brick science campus with hub stage, storage wing, and KILN-D at fourteen hundred degrees**, **porcelain at the sink and the flush**, **a palisade ring at stage-2 composite (~85/97 m)**, **Route A as a reinforced road string with longevity doctrine to Erzin and Aleppo**, **gunpowder and blast caps in the cart**, **a pendulum clock on the records desk**, **BOOT-5 on your feet**, and **VOLTAIC-8-CELL-1 holding ~7.2 V on the chem bench** — with **mercury arc opened, capillary thermometers on the slate, and fig leather in the larder**. You are **not** on a dynamo, a commutator motor, or cloning. You are **not** in space. You **are** an early industrial campus with **Roman-scale plumbing and sanitaryware**, **Cilician-scale trail logistics**, **machine tools that thread**, and **electricity as a tray you maintain** — the third milladial of a speedrun that still has the rest of the human tech tree in front of it.

## On playing one day at a time

A third milladial is **another thousand honest primaries** stacked on two: hub bond beams, tin laps that acid-washed before smelt, bridge kits between gravel bands, char banks before pane grids, kiln cones that finally said fourteen hundred, palisade stone that stayed in front of wood, plate sprints that NO-GO'd when malachite ran thin, and a Day 3000 assembly that fit in one afternoon because the tray grammar was already filed. Future knowledge picked the queue (hub before hero mine, porcelain after kiln proof, caps before trail blast, plates before 8-cell). It did **not** skip the heat. Day 2100's storage c3 still matters because Day 3000's chem-lab is the same campus with a battery on the bench. Day 2556's cone read is why the toilet is porcelain. Day 2941's escapement is why exped food gets packed on time. Day 3000 ends on **a polarising tray**, not a speech: the proof that the campus runs is that the milladial close looks like **farm scare and fig flag ~2 d**.

The fun at Day 3000 is the same fun as Day 1, scaled: **you still choose tomorrow's one job**. The tree is not done. **Day 3001 opens the milladial hangover — mercury in a retort.**
