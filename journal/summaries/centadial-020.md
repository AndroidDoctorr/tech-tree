## On playing one day at a time

Centadial 20 is the century of **infrastructure you can walk on and rules you can feel**: the bridge deck closes mid-century, and suddenly the north road is not a ford argument. Day 1936 and Day 1953 are the two glass beats — **west wall, then east**, each twenty panes that took char you had to bank first. Day 1973 is the machine character beat: **the belt chooses lathe or drill without apologizing to the gate**. Day 2000 is deliberately small — **clay on a wheel**, **salt trays waiting for November**, **a green amphora that is not fire-ready**. The fun at Day 2000 is that **two thousand days landed on a belly coil while a magnet still only twitches a needle**. **Day 2001 is shoulder/neck, or hands off the rack until leather-hard — both legal.**
