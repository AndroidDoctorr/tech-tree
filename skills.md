**What the Player can actually do.** Cite the relevant skill and its **Now** tier in the day file on any hero craft, smelt or field test — [rules.md](rules.md#skills-in-daily-play).

> ☠ ⚠ **STALE — tiers below are baselined at d1094 and the changelog stopped at d1436. The current day is d3275.**
>
> That is roughly **1,800 days of unlogged progression**, across an era that included the whole west road, the bridges, electrolysis, the generator, porcelain trials and the precision chain. **Treat these numbers as a floor, not a reading**, and re-baseline before leaning on any single row.

## Scale

| Rank | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | none | No basis |
| **1** | fumbling | Theory, one-offs, high failure rate |
| **2** | novice | Repeatable with focus, slow |
| **3** | competent | Good by default, occasional rework |
| **4** | practiced | Reliable, diagnoses own mistakes |
| **5** | master | Fast, instinctive, teachable |

**Start (IRL)** is what the Player brought on day one — real experience, theory, and partial transfer from related work, including a physics degree, costume and armour craft, Dagorhir, and sewing. ★ **He is not a blank slate, and Start is authoritative for day one.**

**Now** is Start plus in-world practice.

**Update rule:** bump **Now** when a day logs a first reliable PASS at a **new tier** of task. Reps that reinforce the current tier are not a bump and do not need logging — ★ *the day file already records them, and a ledger of non-changes is noise.* **Start** changes only if the User says their background changed. **No retcon of past days.**

**Cognition:** ADHD — parallel sprints are a strength, maintenance loops need structure *(precedent: the farm neglect arc, d353–365)*.
