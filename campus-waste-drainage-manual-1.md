## Intent

**Never contaminate farm beds, seed rows, spring capture, or aqueduct intake.**  
Grey water gets **traps + settlement** before ditch. Blackwater stays **latrine / soil-pit only**. Chem and forge waste get **named pads, berms, and barrels** — not the kitchen grease line and not the field.

**WD-1 is procedure + placement.** Infrastructure heroes (sand traps, screens, grates) live on the drainage slate; this manual is the **cadence and rules** that keep them working.

## Chem & lab waste

### Placement

- **Bench rinse:** dilute · wipe first · **no concentrated acid** down slop sink.
- **Heavy dregs / failed crucibles:** **chem porch berm** or **labeled crock** · cool before move.
- **Voltaic / copper / nickel tail:** **separate crock** · not compost · not grey.
- **Glass cullet / broken labware:** **pile 6 discard** or **grog reclaim** if clean refractory class.

### Procedures

| Cadence | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **After each acid session** | Neutralize rinse tray · wipe bench · check porch drain screen |
| **Weekly** | Leaf/debris clear @ chem porch · smell test @ E drain |
| **Monthly** | Berm scrape · consolidate crocks · **fire-burn** organics only |
| **Seasonal audit** | Walk **chem E → D-1** pour test · confirm **no backflow toward aqueduct branch** |

### Rules of thumb

- **Acid + metal + heat = named waste, not sink.**
- **When in doubt, dry it on the berm before you water it down a line.**
- **Never “flush through” to the farm ditch to dilute — you only move the problem.**

## Forge · slag · metal scale

### Placement

- **Slag pad** @ FORGE-D / incudinarium — **Tier 3 grog** reclaim OK · loose slag stays on pad.
- **Chill tray / curl / sprue:** cool **fully** → sort **iron reclaim vs pile 6**.
- **Scale and grind dust:** **dry sweep** → pile 6 · **not** grey rinse.
- **Quench water:** **hub S drain pad** · not slag pad · not rett pool.

### Procedures

| Cadence | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **After each smelt / heavy forge day** | Tap slag to pad · sweep scale before rain |
| **Weekly** | Pad perimeter check — **no runoff lip toward ditch W** |
| **Monthly** | Consolidate slag · reclaim grog class to chem berm stock |
| **Seasonal** | Audit **hub S oil film** · install **oil drip grate** when stone stock allows |

### Rules of thumb

- **Slag is hot, sharp, and chemically annoying — treat like chem waste for routing, not like gravel.**
- **Never spread slag or forge sweep on farm paths or compost.**
