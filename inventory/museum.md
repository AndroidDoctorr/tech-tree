# Museum

**Masses and working stock live in [inventory.md](../inventory/index.md).** A museum entry is a **reservation**, not a second copy of the ledger — if a thing is listed here, **it is not available to be consumed.**

---

## THE RULE

> ### PULL THE SPECIMEN BEFORE THE BATCH GOES IN.

Ore gets smelted. Glass gets remelted. A first gets superseded and thrown on the scrap pile. **Once it is processed it cannot be recovered.**

- **Select at the face, at the bench, at the moment.** Never in hindsight.
- **Best example, not first-to-hand.** One good piece beats five ordinary ones.
- **A museum piece is spent stock.** Log the draw honestly — the cost of the museum is real.
- **When a first is superseded, the original stays.** That is the point of it.

**Trigger it on:** a new mineral · a first successful process · a thing made only to prove it could be · the best example of something already held.
