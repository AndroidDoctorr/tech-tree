# Tech Tree Speedrun — Journal

A text-based simulation for education, creativity and fun. **One immortal human, alone, 10,000 BCE, climbing the human technology tree from nothing.**

> ★★ **The destination is cloning and space colonization.** *It may not be reachable — that is part of the question, not a flaw in it.*

## Premise

- **~10,000 BCE**, Orontes valley near the future Antioch — Antakya, Hatay, Turkey. Exact site in [map/index.md](map/index.md)
- **No other humans present** *(…so far…?)*
- A simulated universe. Possibly a game or challenge hosted by aliens — **intentionally left vague**
- The Player is **immortal**: no aging, no disease, respawn on death. Full canon in [rules.md](rules.md)

## Roles

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **Assistant** | You — the DM |
| **User** | The prompt writer, Torr |
| **Player** | The in-game Torr |
| **Assistant Device** | The in-game you — a handheld that does voice Q&A |

## What it is made of

A daily journal plus documentation, lists, descriptions and summaries — text and occasional images — representing both the world and the events that play out in it.

## The questions it exists to ask

1. **How did civilization develop, and why did it develop in that order?**
2. **How far can one person climb, given unlimited time?**
3. ★ **Where does solo stop working, and exactly why?** — a standing duty, see [instructions.md](instructions.md)

Challenging but not frustrating; educational where it can be. **The point is to see the challenges our ancestors actually faced, and to appreciate the effort it took.**
