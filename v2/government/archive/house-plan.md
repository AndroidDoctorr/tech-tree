## Summary

**Domus H-V3** is a **single-storey courtyard villa** wrapped around **C-0** (~12 m S of H-v2). Ranges face the **atrium** (open court · impluvium drain); **H-v2** becomes the **horreum** (north store range) until obsolete, then **demolish → plaza hardscape**.

**Not in the domus:** **officina** · **telarium** (loom) · **incudinarium** (forge) — **Atelier** (old W-1 pad) · **MILLHOUSE-2 / FORGE-D** @ Fabrica ([map.md](map.md)).

**Structural grammar:** **stone socle** + **single-wythe brick mures** (or rubble-lime core in bath wing) · **stone colonnade** on courtyard faces · **zoned hypocaust** (bath block only) · **slab + tile** elsewhere · **shed tile roofs** per range.

**Build doctrine:** **four staged ranges** · **full footing outline @ Stage 0** · **impluvium drain first** · temp **thatch closure** only on range ends until colonnade completes.

### Partial domus *(live while building)*

**Yes** — you do **not** need the full compound before any range is useful.

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **One range = one livable wing** | Roof tile + **mures** closed on that range only · **PANEL-THATCH** on open court faces |
| **H-v2 until cubicula** | **culina vetus** + bed stay @ H-v2 until **west range** passes drip test — then respawn moves |
| **Half compound** | e.g. **south culina live** while **west/east** are footing-only · **atrium** drain can work before all ranges |
| **Parallel clocks** | **Brick factory** · **stone dress** · **domus stages** — interleave; factory never blocks on full hoard |
| **Factory ramp** | **2nd brick mold** · Kiln A/B **stagger** · pit char — raises press rate without waiting on domus |

**Not:** half a roof on one range · hypocaust east before **thermae** shell closed · moving respawn before **tile drip PASS**.

## Walls — **drop double-layer cavity mures**

### Verdict

**Double brick wythes (MURE-2) are dropped** for this plan.

| Why double wall made sense (old T-plan) | Why it does not fit courtyard villa |
|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Cavity for **future wire / warm air** | Services run in **parietes** + **colonade service wall** — courtyard face is **open** |
| Two-storey **tie** across cavity | **Single storey** — bond beam at eaves is enough |
| Modern insulation gap | Mediterranean mass wall + **lime plaster** — Roman **thermodynamic mass**, not cavity R-value |

### Replacement wall types

| Type | ID | Use | Build |
|------|-----|-----|-------|
| **Exterior mure** | **MURE-R1** | **Closed** faces (north onto H-v2 · outer compound walls) | **Stone socle 80–120 cm** · **single B-series wythe ~½ brick (≈11 cm)** · **lime plaster** ext + int · oak **lintels** only |
| **Bath mass wall** | **MURE-R2** | East range · heat-facing | **MURE-R1** + optional **rubble-lime core** (grog + lime fill between stone skins) when stock allows — **opus caementicium** class |
| **Courtyard face** | **COLONNADE-1** | **No mure** — open to court | **Stone pillar** · oak **beam** · tile lean roof · rear **PAR-SERVICE** partition |
| **Interior paries** | **PAR-1** | Room divides | **Half brick** or **wattle + lime daub** · **lime plaster** · **chase pockets** for pipes · **OUTLET-BRICK** plugs |
| **Service spine** | **PAR-SERVICE** | Behind colonnade | **Single thick partition** (~½ m) · aqueduct · grey drains · future wire — **inspectable** from court side |
| **Temp closure** | **PANEL-THATCH-1** | Range end until next stage | Removable · **≤2 winters** then swap **lime-daub panel** |

**Electric / wire horizon:** vertical chases in **PAR-SERVICE** and bath paries — **not** in exterior mure.
