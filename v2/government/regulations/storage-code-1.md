# Storage code — SC-1

*Filed Day 3273.*

**[building-code-1.md](building-code-1.md) governs the structure. This governs what is INSIDE it.**

> ☠ ★★★ **THE STRUCTURE SURVIVES AND THE CONTENTS DO NOT.** *A shake that leaves every wall standing can still empty every shelf onto the floor.* ★★ **Nothing else in the codes owns contents, which is why this file exists.**

Every rule below names **what a miss costs** — a rule that costs nothing on a miss is a preference, not a rule.

---

## SC-1.1 — Shelves and stacks

| Rule | Cost of a miss |
|---|---|
| ★★★ **A LIP ON EVERY SHELF** | **Everything on it, in one shake.** *The cheapest single fix on the campus* |
| ★★ **Heavy LOW, light high** | **A falling mass, and what it lands on** |
| ★★ **Crates sized to STACK square, lined with straw or linen** | **A toppled stack and broken contents** |
| ★★★ **PUT IT AWAY WHEN DONE** | ★★ **A thing left on a bench is a thing on the floor.** *Costs nothing, catches everything* |
| ★ **Nothing heavy stored above a workspace or a bed** | **Injury, not loss** |

## SC-1.2 — Irreplaceable

**Seed ark · museum · records · reference standards · instruments.**

| Rule | Cost of a miss |
|---|---|
| ☠ ★★★ **DISTRIBUTE — never all of one thing in one building** | **The whole category, permanently** |
| ★★★ **Duplicate the RECORDS off-campus** | **Nine years of knowing why** |
| ★★ **Off the floor, sealed, and dry** | **Flood, damp, vermin — the slow losses, which are the likely ones** |
| ★★ **Instruments in fitted, padded boxes, not on open benches** | **The standards chain. A dropped reference is not a broken tool, it is a LIE that keeps working** |

### ★★★ CAVE STORAGE — certified by twelve thousand years

> ★★★ **The Assistant knows which caves in this region are still standing in the modern day. That is a structural record I cannot build to, cannot test for, and could never obtain by experiment.** ★★ **Use it for the seed ark duplicate and the records duplicate.**

⚠ **STANDING is not DRY.** *Certify the structure from future knowledge; certify the humidity, the seeps, the roof spall and the animals by going and looking, in more than one season.* **Sealed jars regardless.**

## SC-1.3 — Volatile and reactive

**Bitumen · turpentine · alcohol · oils · acids · anything that burns or eats.**

| Rule | Cost of a miss |
|---|---|
| ☠ ★★★ **LOW, and APART FROM EACH OTHER, and APART FROM FIRE** | ★★ **The campus.** *Fire after a shake does more than the shake* |
| ★★ **Acids in glass, in a secondary vessel, never above waist height** | **A burn, and the glassware** |
| ★★ **Labelled on the vessel, not on the shelf** | ☠ **The label stays with the shelf and the jar becomes anonymous** |
| ★ **A sand bucket within reach of every store** | **Minutes, which is all a fire allows** |

## SC-1.4 — Reviewing

- ★★ **Walk the stores once a season and look UP.** *Ask of each thing: where does this land, and what is under it?*
- ☠ ★★ **Anything stored buried or underwater is the hardest thing to repair.** *Budget for that before burying it, not after.*
