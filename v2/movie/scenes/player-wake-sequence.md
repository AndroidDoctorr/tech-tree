# Player wake sequence

**Circle step:** GO  
**POV:** Ensemble montage  
**Purpose for audience:** Disorientation; no two drops alike; assistant boot is only constant.

---

## Beat

1. **Player A** — eyes open; river sound; flat terrace; **no scorch mark, no pod.** Hand finds device already in pocket or palm.
2. **Player B** — reed marsh; insects; different sky color (same sun).
3. **Player C** — rocky slope; bruised; immediate panic run → cliff? → **hold for later death**.
4. **Assistant (variant lines):**
   - *"Good morning. You are alive."*
   - *"Local year approximately ten thousand before your calendar's reference zero."*
   - *"Ask me questions. I cannot carry water for you."*
5. **Montage rhythm:** hunger → thirst → first night → first fire attempt (some fail).
6. **End beat:** Wide shot — **multiple smokes** on horizon, unknown to each other. Title card?

---

## Lines / scraps

- *"Where am I?"* / *"Geolocation unavailable. Describe what you see."*
- *"Am I dead?"* / *"You are awake. Those are different problems."*
- *"Call someone."* / *"There is no someone on this network."*

---

## Notes

- **No respawn yet** — audience thinks death is real.
- Geography-literate player: one muttered recognition beat (*"…Levant?"*) — optional.
- Stagger drops in **story time** or **same dawn** — decide later (same dawn = tighter; stagger = lonelier).
