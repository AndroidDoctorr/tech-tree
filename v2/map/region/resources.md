### ★ The `CAVE-3` floor was not nitre

The chamber floor earth looked like a nitre deposit and was worked as one. It is **gypsum plus an epsom-class bitter salt** *(d3222–d3223)*.

**No quarry was cut, because the whole chamber is the ark** — and following the mistake to its source found `M-21`, which is worth far more than the nitre would have been.

### ☠ `NICKEL-TARGET-1` — the ore is not what you think it is

> **Do not look for pentlandite.** It is the wrong target for an ophiolite.

The ore is the **red-brown dirt sitting on top of weathered serpentinite**, and it is found by **vegetation, not by rock**: a sparse, stunted, bald patch on an otherwise green hillside. **Garnierite** is apple-green and waxy, in the fractures.

**Method:** burn hyperaccumulator plants and assay the ash.

★ **Reduce it in contact with copper and you get constantan directly.** Nickel metal as such was never the point.

**Where:** the **Kisecik laterite**, reframed d3273 — the same ophiolite that carries `M-14` sulfur and the `M-15` sulfides, ~14 km NW. ⚠ **Scout queued, never executed.** No field assay has been run, so the target is doctrine and not yet a site.
