# Trails and bridges

The road network beyond campus. **Legs carry `TQ`; crossings carry `BQ`; fords carry `FQ`.** Those are three different scales and they measure three different things.

Local campus trails `T-1`–`T-5` are in [campus/index.md](../campus/index.md). Waystations and caches are [off-campus-structures.md](off-campus-structures.md).

## `TRAIL-Q` — road quality, per leg

Think path grading. **Each tier removes friction** — deviation, slope, brush, rut, approach stumble.

| `TQ` | Label | Loaded pace *(~53–81 kg column)* | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **String** | ~2.5 km/h | Scout tape · bind · sidestep · no crown |
| **1** | **Marked** | ~2.8 km/h | Stakes and char visible, still rough, constant adjustment |
| **2** | **Crown** | ~3.2 km/h | Crowned, one-line wagon track · bridge build grammar |
| **3** | **Pass** | ~3.5 km/h | Reliable loaded · approaches dressed · minor ruts only |
| **4** | **Haul** | ~3.8 km/h | Cuts and fills · felled lane · repeat-trip class |
| **5** | **Road** | ~4.2 km/h | Haul road · minimal deviation · the ore-return target |

**Theoretical max** on perfect flat marked crown is **~5 km/h** with a motivated empty wagon. ⚠ **Route A is not there and will not be** — slope and crossings cap honest days.

**Hop time** between nodes ≈ `km ÷ loaded pace` **+ crossing minutes** (ford ~15 min · bridge ~25 min).

**How `TQ` moves:** each maintenance block on a leg files **+1 work point**, and **+4 WP = +0.5 `TQ`**, capped at 5. ★ **Gains are slow by design** — this is why mature legs sit near 3 and not near 5.

## `BRIDGE-Q` — crossing friction

`BQ` grades **loaded crossing friction** — deck stiffness, scour, approach apron, listen in millimetres. ★ **It is not march speed**, and it does not belong on a leg.

| `BQ` | Label | Loaded listen | Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Scout** | Not crossed loaded | Ghost site · reserve queued |
| **1** | **Ford GO** | N/A · boot or knee wade | Summer and low water · **re-read every spring** |
| **2** | **Live wood** | ~4–5 mm | Early oak deck · deployed, not reinforced |
| **3** | **Mature wood** | ~3 mm | Repeat lap · apron dressed |
| **3.5** | **Pass** | ~2–3 mm | Reinforced, or concrete abutment |
| **4** | **Haul** | ≤~2 mm | Post-reinforce |
| **5** | **Road** | ≤~1 mm | Multi-reinforce · ideal apron. Rare, and a target rather than a hero |

**Fords have no `BQ`.** They carry **`FQ`** — **GO · AMBER · RED**, nothing finer. Where a ford has concrete shore work, the shore takes a `BQ` and the ford lip still takes an `FQ`.

**Upgrade ladder:** `LISTEN` → `TOUCH` → `REINFORCE` → `DEPLOY` → `REBUILD`. Escalate one tier only when the tier below fails audit. The full doctrine is [trail-longevity.md](../../government/procedures/trail-longevity.md).

## Return timing

★★ **There is no teleport.** A return is counted in nights since leaving home, not in days of travel remaining.

| Returning from | Legs | Total |
|---|---|---|
| **Kırıkhan split** ~53 km | Split → post-Afrin ~25 km · post-Afrin → HOME ~28 km | **2 days** · 1 only if no trail work |
| **İskenderun terrace** ~82 km | Isk → post-Afrin ~54 km · post-Afrin → HOME ~28 km | **2 days minimum** |
| **Erzin / The Castle** ~132–142 km | Erzin → post-Afrin ~104 km · post-Afrin → HOME ~28 km | **2 days** |
| **Ceyhan split** ~170 km | Split → Erzin rejoin · Erzin → post-Afrin ~104 km · post-Afrin → HOME ~28 km | **3 days** at clear-move · 2 is aggressive |

> ⚠ **A full İskenderun → HOME return with trail work and loaded crossings cannot close in one calendar day.** 82 km at ~3.25 km/h is **~25 hours of marching before any crossing** — and there are six bridges and two fords in it. File leg 1 as a camp at post-Afrin and leg 2 as a home arrival mid-afternoon.

**Fast-string pace cap:** ~92 km/day listening only on mature `L0`–`L9` · **~40 km/day** through the Issus squeeze at `L10`–`L12`.
