## The cave ark — ~250 m N, across the tributary

The only off-campus structure close enough to visit without planning a trip, and the most important one.

### `CAVE-3` — `SEED-ARK-1`
Above the seep band, behind a rock shoulder, through a **low crawl entry**. Cairned d3220.

A small stooping chamber. **Powder-dry floor, no bats, air PASS** — the only dry space found on the whole cave line.

**Fitted out d3221:** stepped path · diversion trench · fixed line · dry-stone shelf. A quarry split was **cancelled d3222** — ★ **the whole chamber is the ark.**

✓ **Rain-verified d3263: bone dry** after weather, which is the test that mattered.

> ☠ **The ark is fitted, proven and empty. The seed has not been moved in.** `CAVE-3-DOOR-1` is still queued at d3275, and until there is a door there is no ark — only a dry hole with a shelf in it.

**Doctrine filed d3273:** duplicate the seed *and the records* into the certified cave. Not move — **duplicate**. What is banked at home: [seed-vault.md](../../inventory/seed-vault.md).

### `CAVE-1` — chamber and spring
Cairned d3213. Walk-in, good moving air, **live seep and pool at the rear**. Cool store and shelter class, a genuine fallback site.

⚠ **Too damp for seed.** ☠ **The water is untested — boil it.**

### `CAVE-2`
Cairned d3220. One room, air PASS, but damp wall and floor on the same seep horizon. **No use** for seed or nitre.

⚠ **The Castle hill is a mark, not a stop.** It is a ~10 km foot detour NNW off the road. Walking to it costs a day and buys a bearing.

> ★ **A crossing waystation carries both the camp backup and the staged bridge or cart cache** — it is the closest relief if a cart or a bridge fails mid-lap. That double duty is why these four sites and not others.

### ☠ Food caches — RED at both ends

The Kozan waystation food cache read **RED at d2664** and the Ceyhan and Kozan stores were weevilled at **d2666** — do not draw. At **d3096**, more than four hundred days later, **Belen and Kozan food both still read RED**, with a refill only queued for the March prep band.

⚠ **No restock hero is logged anywhere through d3275.** Corral, field kiln, cart kits and bridge cache are all live and reliable; **the food is not.** Carry rations for the whole lap and treat the cache food as absent.

★ **The hard kit held for a decade and the soft kit failed in two years.** Rope, wedges and planks sit in a sealed box; food is a clock, and nothing on these pads was reading it.
