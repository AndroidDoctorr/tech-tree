# Domus

Living quarters. **~92 m from camp**, inland on the plateau — off the water and off the fume cluster by design.

A courtyard compound: four ranges around the open court at `C-0`. The **west cubiculum is the respawn anchor**. Design grammar and the column grid are in [house-plan.md](../../government/archive/house-plan.md); this file is the spaces as they stand.

```
        [ horreum — H-v2 body · store · ante ]
                        |
        (north passage · open strip · SR lean cover)
                        |
    ┌───────────────────┬───────────────────┐
    │  W · cubicula     │   E · thermae     │
    │  dormio · vestis  │   caldarium ·     │
    │  RESPAWN          │   latrina · lav   │
    ├───────────────────┴───────────────────┤
    │      C-0 · atrium · impluvium         │
    │      compluvium · cisterna            │
    ├───────────────────────────────────────┤
    │  S · culina · penus · porticus        │
    └───────────────────────────────────────┘
                        ↓ to D-1, then TRIB-1
```

⚠ **Everything drains outward to `D-1` and then TRIB-1 — never into the horreum or the hypocaust void.** The plateau falls east-upslope to the gorge, west-downslope to the Orontes, and north to TRIB-1, so the court and all four ranges are graded to the same outfall.

★ **The court is a hardscape pad and is kept smoke-friendly** — kiln smoke, char dust and wagon turns all pass through it, so it takes no soft goods and no food storage.
