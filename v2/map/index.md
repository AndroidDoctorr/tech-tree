# Map

Where things are. **The map holds places; it does not hold stock or condition.**

| Ask | Goes to |
|---|---|
| *Where is it?* | here |
| *How much is there?* | [inventory](../inventory/index.md) |
| *What condition is it in?* | [infrastructure.md](../inventory/infrastructure.md) |
| *When do I go?* | [calendar.md](../checklists/calendar.md) |

A map entry describes the **space** — extent, aspect, dry or damp, what it connects to. Those don't change when a sack moves. Contents are found by grepping the location ID across the inventory, which is always right because it is never stored twice.

## ★★ One place, one row

> ☠ **The campus bridge sat in the register twice under two names, with two different grades, for seven years.** It was `TRIB-FT-01` at BQ ~3.0 and `BRIDGE-1 @ km 3 creek` built d720 and ungraded. One crossing. A fossil of the early campus-layout muddle, sorted on the ground and never in the records — merged d3270.
>
> ★★ **A fact stored twice diverges, and then both copies look authoritative. The tell is always that the two copies disagree about something small.**

If a place appears under two names, **merge it before reading either**.

## Site

**Orontes valley, near the future Antioch.** Campus sits on a **plateau, south bank of TRIB-1**. The stream leaves the **east gorge** and runs **west** to the Orontes.

| Axis | What is there |
|---|---|
| **N** | TRIB-1 · Fabrica and the T-1 fork · `CAMPUS-BRIDGE` · road continues north |
| **E** | Upslope · gorge · pines on the climb · goat trap `A-03` |
| **S** | Inland plateau, then the mountain rise |
| **W** | Downslope · farm and olive · Amuq · **Orontes ~1 km** · coast ~20 km |

⚠ **The Orontes is not at the foot of camp.** TRIB-1 is the home stream — the weir, the clay bank and both water wheels are on it. Camp sits **+4 m above TRIB-1 high water**; the Orontes floodplain is the separate drop to the west.

★ **Two clocks, and they do not merge.** **Domus** is inland on the plateau and is where living happens. **Fabrica** is on the water and is where production happens. **Atelier** is clean craft, mid-campus, deliberately off the fume cluster and not on the Fabrica plus.

Site lock: [HOME-SITE-LOCK-1920](../journal/retcons/HOME-SITE-LOCK-1920.md).

## `STAKE-CODE-1` — reading a stake without the slate

Filed d917. Every stake carries its own meaning in the field.

| Prefix | Head band | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SR` | Madder red | Structure — walls · terraces · buildings | `SR-S1-03` |
| `PW` | Yellow ochre | Paths · crowns · cart lines | `PW-T5-01` |
| `DR` | Alum white | Drain · water · swale | `DR-D1-02` |
| `WF` | Char black | Waste · slag · midden | `WF-SB-01` |
| `AG` | Herb green | Farm · pen · garden · apiary | `AG-AP-01` |
| `SC` | Woad blue | Scout · ghost · horizon · reserve | `SC-GO-01` |

**On each stake:** band colour at the head · **notch count** = segment number on the line · **lime tick on the north face** = camp vector · **limestone chip at the foot** scratched `PREFIX-LINE-POINT`. The kitchen slate is the master duplicate.

## Route marker colours

Filed d128 as `RS-3`. Distinct from stake bands — these mark **routes**, not sites.

| Stripe | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **White (lime)** | Home · critical turns |
| **Red (PM-R1)** | `M-G1` corridor |
| **Yellow (PM-Y1)** | Scout deviation · new probes |
| **Black** | Water · hazard |

Trail grade colours on the player overlay are a third scheme again, and they live with the `TRAIL-Q` scale.
