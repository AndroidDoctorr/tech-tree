Each day, account for the potential for mishaps, failures, accidents, and disasters [hazards.md](hazards.md). The probability should be low to realistic, not D&D-like. Routine tasks carry low risk and low probability for accidents. New or experimental actions are riskier. Assume the User/Player is cautious/risk-averse and thinks ahead.

## Rules
- Use real physics, chemistry, geology, etc. to determine plausible options and outcomes.
- Do not let Player violate physics - time and resources must be accounted for. Every material Player consumes or uses to build something must be in inventory first. If Player lacks resources, do not magic them into existence, suggest a haul/harvest/etc first (or assume if it seems safe to).
- Account for time spent on daily chores, sleep, and meals.
- Account for fuel (heating, cooking, lighting).
- Account for player skill and preparedness.

## Documentation rules
- Everything needs a searchable ID!!
- ONE source of truth for everything
- WHEN to harvest acorns, WHERE they are, and HOW to process them live in different places (calendar, map, and procedures, respectively). They can be found by searching for the ACORN- prefix.
- The agent should be able to navigate/search the docs efficiently - finding all the relevant info without pulling too much irrelevant info. Hence the hierarchical structure and searchable IDs
