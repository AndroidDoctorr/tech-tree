## Standing leach practice · *restated, not discovered*

| Step | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Neutralise with the ore itself** | **Add excess ground ore and stir until the fizzing stops.** The ore eats the last free acid, the liquor sweetens, and **the iron falls out as a rusty sludge while the copper stays dissolved**. **The ore purifies its own liquor** — no reagent I do not already have |
| **Leach cold and brief** | **Copper carbonate dissolves fast; iron oxide dissolves slowly.** Take the copper and **stop before the iron catches up** |
| **Settle, do not just filter** | **Let the slime drop overnight and **draw off the clear. Filtering fines through linen is a waste of a day |
