### RENDER-IN-WATER *(the method that matters)*

| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| **Melt the comb dry over the fire** | **Float it on water in the pot** |
| **Wax scorches, darkens, smells, and the batch is degraded** | **The water **holds the pot below scorching — wax melts far under boiling |
| **Dross stays mixed through it** | **Dross, cocoon, and bee parts sink or strain out; clean wax floats and sets as a cake on top** |

## THE READ · **the hive design is the bottleneck, not the bees**

**This is the real finding of the day and I should have seen it years ago.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The problem** | **A skep is FIXED COMB.** The bees build straight onto the inside of the basket. **There is no way to take honey or wax out without cutting the comb apart** — and the traditional answer to that is to **kill the colony over a sulfur pit** and take everything |
| **What I have been doing instead** | **Scavenging.** Cappings, burr comb, scraps. **Which is why three hives over three years have given me a few hundred grams** |
| **The unlock** | **MOVABLE COMB.** Give the bees **individual bars across the top** of the box instead of a solid lid. **They build one comb hanging down from each bar.** Then I **lift a bar out, cut the comb off it, and put the bare bar back** — colony intact, comb rebuilt, **harvest every year without destroying anything** |

### BEE SPACE · **the measurement the whole thing turns on**

> **Bees leave a gap of about 8 mm as a walkway and will not build in it.** Anything WIDER they fill with comb. Anything NARROWER they glue shut with propolis.

| Get it right | Get it wrong |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Bar width ~32–35 mm** — natural worker-comb spacing, centre to centre | **Too wide → they build **cross-comb** between bars and the whole thing welds into one mass again** |
| **A **guide** on the underside of each bar — a bevelled ridge or a strip of wax — so they start the comb **on the bar, straight** | **No guide → comb wanders off the bar diagonally and cannot be lifted |

**Retrofit target:** **HIVE-3 is already a wooden box.** Bars are a far easier conversion there than trying to do anything with a skep.** **Skeps stay as they are — they work, they are just not harvestable.
