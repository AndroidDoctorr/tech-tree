## ★★★ BEFORE TOUCHING IT — I MEASURED THE MACHINE'S LINE

**I was about to rewind on the strength of a rule of thumb. Instead I spent the morning loading the machine through known resistances and writing down what it did.**

**Voltmeter across the terminals, ammeter in series, and R-STD-1 plus the R-BIG stack swapped through as the load. Volts and current at each step.**

### The result is a STRAIGHT LINE

| Load | Volts | Current |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | **2.28 GB** | 0 |
| Heavy | falling | rising |
| **Dead short** *(extrapolated)* | 0 | **~0.95 I** |

> ★★★ **"HOW MANY VOLTS DOES IT MAKE" IS NOT A QUESTION WITH AN ANSWER.** *It makes 2.28 with nothing attached and zero into a dead short and everything in between depending entirely on what I hang on it.* ★★ **The machine is not a number. It is a LINE between those two points, and the load picks the spot.**
>
> ★★ **And the SLOPE of that line IS the internal resistance** — measured from outside, without opening anything. **2.4 R**, which agrees with what the winding should be. ★ *Two independent routes to the same number is the first time this machine has confirmed itself.*

## ★★★ THEN THE ARITHMETIC STOPPED ME REWINDING THE WAY I MEANT TO

**Most power comes out when the load matches the internal resistance** — *too light a load and the volts are high but nothing flows; too heavy and the current is high but the volts have collapsed inside the machine.* **The peak sits exactly in the middle.**

**Peak available: about 0.54 GB·I.** ⚠ **That is the whole machine.**

### ★★ And then I asked what a rewind does to it — and got a result I did not expect

**Finer wire in the same window means MORE TURNS. So:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Volts rise with the turns** | **E ∝ N** |
| ⚠ **But resistance rises with the SQUARE of the turns** | *more turns AND each one thinner* — **r ∝ N²** |
| ★★★ **So peak power ∝ E²/r ∝ N²/N² = UNCHANGED** | |

> ★★★ **FOR A FIXED WINDING WINDOW, THE MAXIMUM POWER IS INDEPENDENT OF THE NUMBER OF TURNS.** ★★ **A rewind does not lift the line. It ROTATES it about a fixed power** — *steeper and taller for volts, flatter and longer for current.*
>
> ★★★ **A REWIND IS A TRADE, NOT A GAIN. I cannot wind my way to a bigger machine.**

★★ **I had this as a rule of thumb — "gauge barely changes efficiency" — and now I have the reason, and the reason is much more useful than the rule, because it also says where power DOES come from:**

> ★★★ **E ∝ N·Φ·ω, and r depends only on the window — so peak power ∝ (Φ·ω)².** ★★ **POWER GOES AS THE SQUARE OF THE FIELD AND THE SQUARE OF THE SPEED, AND NOT AT ALL WITH TURNS.**
>
> ★ **That ranks the whole improvement list in one line, and it explains every diminishing return I have measured.** *More magnets gave +23% volts, which is +51% power. More water is worth the same again. And the rewind is worth nothing at all in power — it is worth everything in getting the power out in a SHAPE I can use.*

## ★★ THE REWIND — and the copper it needed was already wound

**Target: 0.9 mm → 0.65 mm, same window, 76 → ~145 turns per coil. About 180 m. Volts up ~1.9×, resistance up ~3.6×.**

⚠ **Then I weighed the requirement: ~180 m of 0.65 mm is roughly 535 g of copper. The cell has been running nine days at about 17 g. The bank is nowhere near it.**

**I nearly filed the rewind as blocked for a month.**

> ★★★ **THEN: THE OLD ARMATURE IS THE COPPER. 95 m of 0.9 mm is the same 535 g, sitting on the core, doing the job I want to replace.**
>
> ★★★ **AND I DO NOT HAVE TO MELT IT — DRAWING CONSERVES VOLUME.** *95 m at 0.9 mm drawn down to 0.65 mm is **182 m**, which is the rewind to within a metre.* **The old winding contains EXACTLY the new winding, because the new winding is the same window filled with finer wire. It could not have come out otherwise.**

| | |
|---|---|
| ★★★ **NO MELT, NO MELT TAX** | **Drawing is cold work. The metal never goes liquid, so it never drinks oxygen** — *the 1.3% I fought for yesterday is simply not paid here* |
| ★ **And the anneal IMPROVES it** | **Cold work raises resistance; annealing between passes takes it back out.** *The wire comes off the bench better than it went on* |
| ★★ **The rewind is SELF-FINANCING** | *I was waiting on a month of river for copper I was already standing in front of* |
