## One paragraph

The sixteenth hundred days **turn crumbs into a button, then the button into bronze**: you **walked the four-day return**, **smelted SN-INGOT-TRIAL-1 (~102 g)**, **locked fork A**, **ran crumbs laps 2–4**, **stood CART-PACK-RIG**, and **on Day 1526 poured the first alloy** (**~10% Sn · trip-hammer bushes · ~1.65 kg stock**). After that the century is **not a mine rush** — it is **woad leaf**, **TR fire**, **corridor garnish**, **Erzin · Pinarus · Dortyol reinforce**, **wild flax 5–7**, a **Kozan field kiln and corral** you **did not smelt in**, **saltpeter in the jar**, **Kisecik sulfur marked**, then **open-sand bronze** on the cart hubs and a shopping list. Day 1600 is **not a feast**. It is **TIN-SMELT-TRIAL-4 (~96 g)**, **Sn bank ~193 g**, **char RED**, **wood thin**. You are **not** on a tin mine. You **are** on **workable bronze that already lives in the trip and the wagon** — the first century where **the metal gap that survived every prior rollup is closed**, and the honest leftover is **fuel**.

## On playing one day at a time

Centadial 16 is the century of **not pretending meh is a lode**: you smelted at home four times and got the same answer. Day 1505 (**first tin metal**) and Day 1526 (**first bronze**) are the two character beats — **the button**, then **the alloy**. Day 1600 is quieter and more like a Tuesday: **ninety-six grams**, **one hundred ninety-three in the tray**, **six kilos of char in the lane**. The fun at Day 1600 is that **bronze arrived in the middle of the hundred and you spent the rest of it making the road and the shopping list honest**. **Day 1601 is wood, or a pit, or a restock — not a speech about the Gates.**
