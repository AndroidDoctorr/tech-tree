## On playing one day at a time

Centadial 21 is the century of **the larder you can eat from and the year you can close**: November is not one day — it is **salt scrape, olive pick, press, harvest, and amphora fire** stacked across five weeks, each with its own char gate. Day 2061 is the emotional peak — **ten courses, a SUN glyph, Cal-Y6 actually ending** — and then Day 2062 is deliberately ordinary: **campus day, paint eve, glass batch 1 of five**. The hemp scout (Days 2048–2054) is the surprise hero — three legs out, one bag home, a feast present that did not exist on the Centadial 20 horizon. Day 2100 is brick again: **mortar slaked, c3 closed, ~71% shell, ice still ten days out**. The fun at Day 2100 is that **two thousand one hundred days landed on a lintel seat ghost while electricity is still not the story**. **Day 2101 is c4, or green-press refill, or ice kit staging — all legal.**
