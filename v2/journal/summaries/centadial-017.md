## One paragraph

The seventeenth hundred days **rebuild the fuel, then spend the summer becoming a chemist and a year**: you **hauled wood and sulfur**, **mixed gunpowder and proved a cracker**, **cut the Kisecik road**, **finished the Kozan kiln and poured tin in it** (**SN-INGOT-FIELD-1 ~95 g**), **validated a 12-day lap**, **shot a firework**, **reinforced PAYAS**, then **stockpiled crumbs and ran a 2-charge field batch**. Kisecik paid a second metal suite — **Zn/Pb scout · lead · brass button · cinnabar marked, not roasted**. You **cut EMMER-Y5**, **striped the awnings**, **synced Yule to the solstice**, and **closed Calendar Year 5**. Day 1700 is **not a smelt**. It is **P-RETT-8 on the rack** and **PAINT-CANVAS-1 on EASEL-1** — charcoal of the court in north light. You are **not** on mercury. You **are** on a **fifth harvest in the horreum**, a **field kiln that finally poured**, **lead and brass you can weigh**, and a **studio that opened the day after the feast** — the first century where **the year closed on a calendar you admitted was 21 December, and Day 1700 is paint**.
