### Surface doctrine — not tile everywhere *(filed Day 710)*

| Zone | Best surface | Why |
|------|--------------|-----|
| **Floor** | **Fired LF tile** + lime-sand grout bed | Spill containment · wipe · acid splash class |
| **Counters / benches** | **Pompeii-class grouted tile or dressed stone slab** | Heat + chem · **not** wood tops |
| **Walls** | **Lime plaster skim + whitewash** | **Bright · smooth · light bounce** — cheaper than full tile |
| **Lower wall belt** *(optional later)* | **Tile wainscot ~1 m** | Splash zone only · not whole room |
| **Fire table** | **Tile or stone top** | Peripheral · charcoal meter |
| **Wood** | **Frames only** ✓ | Shelves · cab carcases — oiled · not limed |
| **Ceiling / roof** | **Fired roof tile** ✓ | Already closed Stage 4 |

**Rule:** **Tile where spills, heat, and scrubbing live.** **Lime skim where light and flat matter.** **Wood only where nothing corrosive sits.**
