# Atelier

Clean craft. **~80 m from camp**, mid-campus, on the old `W-1` pad. ★ **A separate building** — not part of the Fabrica compound *(confirmed d1921)*.

A four-way complex around a central tower hub, built out d2072–d2264. Three of the four wings are complete; the north work wing is still a ghost.

```
                [ N · work wing — GHOST ]
                     (temp stoop)
                            |
 [ W · STORAGE-WING-1 ] — [ TURRIS ] — [ E · CRAFT-WING-1 ]
        5×6 m           hub 5×5 m           5×6 m
                     island hearth
                            |
              [ S · portico · 3 × 5 m ]
```

★ **The whole point of this building is that it is somewhere else.** Pigment, canvas, oils and linen cannot share air with the forge or the fume hood, so the Atelier sits off the Fabrica plus and runs on its own clock. Original wing plan: [craft-wing-plan.md](../../government/archive/craft-wing-plan.md).

### The island hearth

`CAMPUS-HUB-ISLAND-HEARTH-1`. Dug ~10 cm into the centre and levelled to the inner ring, then **×6 fired stretchers with a fire-clay pack and a raised lip at the apron**. Four oak posts on the column pads (d2217) carry a **scarf beam hood** (d2219). Live smoke d2220.

★ **It is a masonry heater, not a fireplace.** The long flue path takes smoke through brick and tile mass, and **the mass radiates for hours after the fire is out** — kachelofen class. The hood mass reclaims on the same principle.

⚠ **The ridge vent and the flue are deliberately separate paths.** Warm air leaves at the tower top through the ridge slot; smoke goes up the stack. Do not merge them — the vent is not a flue cap.

✓ **`CAMPUS-HUB-VENT-1` CLOSED d3282** — island hood lip in fired tile bedded in lime, hung off the armature from outside the hot zone; **low cross-draft louvers** on the two faces that do not look at the island. Smoke test PASS, plume held against an opened windward door.

> ☠ ★★ **The louvers are make-up air, not comfort.** *The ridge slot and the stack are two ways out of a tight building competing for one way in, and if the flue loses that competition it reverses and the hearth smokes into the room.* **Separating the two paths at the top does not separate them at the bottom.** ⚠ **Tested on a still day — the honest test is the first hard wind.**

**Outstanding after stage close:** **hub exterior limewash** · `HUB-RUG-N-1` · `FAN-PROVISION` ghost. Deferred, none blocking.

## What was here before

`W-1` was a **~3 × 4 m wattle-and-daub workshop**, struck d2945 as `W-1-LEAN-TO-STRIKE-2945`. Frame down, site clear.

`ROOF-W1` was the **first tile roof on campus** (d364) and its grammar was absorbed into the craft-wing eave class — the building is gone but the detail it taught is still in use everywhere.

`TEMP-PORTICO-1` was the demount-ready temporary porch at the craft wing east door — ~1.6 × 2.2 m of oak lean and reed, 100% d1752. The hub was staked on its pad at d2072 ("no demount yet"), and the substantial south portico superseded it. ⚠ **No demount was ever logged.** Weed passes still name "`TEMP-PORTICO` footings" as late as d2267, so something is physically left there.

## ⚠ "M2" does not mean Millhouse-2 here

Day files working this complex say things like *"@ M2 hub interior"* and *"`CAMPUS-HUB-CENTER-STAKE-2072` @ M2 east pad."* **`M2` is campus-belt shorthand for the Atelier compound pad**, not `MILLHOUSE-2` at the Fabrica.

★ **Two different hubs exist and they are not the same building.** `CAMPUS-HUB-M2-1` / **Turris** is this one, ~5 × 5 m with the island hearth. `WW-HUB-TOWER-1` is the Fabrica connector between the store and the chem lab. Entries like *"`HUB-S-OIL-GRATE-1` @ M2 forge + hub S pad"* (d2413) mean stock was drawn from the Fabrica forge — **not that the building moved.**
