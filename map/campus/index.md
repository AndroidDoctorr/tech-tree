## Buildings

Permanent structures with interior spaces.

### [Domus](domus.md) — ~92 m, plateau
Living quarters. Bed and **respawn anchor**, kitchen, horreum, baths, courtyard at `C-0`.

### [Fabrica](fabrica.md) — ~140 m, T-1 fork on TRIB-1
Production. Forge, chem lab, machine tools, water wheels, related storage.

### [Atelier](atelier.md) — ~80 m, mid-campus on the old W-1 pad
Clean craft. A four-way complex around the **Turris** tower hub and its island hearth: craft wing east, storage wing west, substantial portico south, work wing north still a ghost. ★ **Deliberately not on the Fabrica plus** — it is kept off the fume and smoke cluster.

The first bridge out of campus, on the `T-2` farm road. Concrete abutments, upgraded more than once. **BQ ~1.5**, downgraded from a filed ~3.0 at the d3270 merge.

All timber and all concrete faces **probed sound** d3270. But:

> ☠ ★★★ **The bed is scoured out under the downstream footing — a hole deeper than the footing is set.** The bridge is in excellent condition and standing on ground that is leaving.
>
> ★★ **Every upgrade ever made addressed the rot clock. None touched the scour clock.**

⚠ A riprap apron was started d3270 and is **not finished**. **Lighten or avoid loaded wagon until it is in.**
