# Fabrica

Production. **~140 m from camp**, at the T-1 fork on the **north edge of TRIB-1** — on the water, because everything here is driven by it.

Also called the **WW-YARD**. One compound in a plus shape around a central hub, with the wheels on the stream bank behind it and the dirty work in the open court in front.

```
              [ STORE-4 · 5×6 · toward TRIB-1 ]
                            |
 [ MILLHOUSE-2 · 5×6 ] — [ WW-HUB-TOWER ] — [ CHEM-LAB · 5×6 ]
          W                     +                     E
                            |
        [ south court: belt · FORGE-D at the millhouse cheek ]

 [ north pad at TRIB-1: WW-1 · WW-2 · raceway intake ]
```

## ★★ Orientation — the wheels are on the north bank

> ⚠ **Canon, filed d1989.** `WW-1` and `WW-2` are on the **north (TRIB-1) bank**, on the north pad. The **south court** is belt clearance, `FORGE-D`, and the smoke and working zone.

⚠ **The Atelier is not on this plus.** It is a separate building, mid-campus, and clean-craft work does not come here.

## Yard markers

Stone markers set at d141, still the naming grammar for the yard: **WHEEL · HOUSE · BELT · FORGE · CHAR · ORE · TOOL**. `CHAR`, `ORE` and `TOOL` flank the built legs and hold fuel, feedstock and racks rather than structures.

## Hub — `WW-HUB-TOWER-1`

Centre of the plus and the connector between all four legs. Oak floor, tile, vented.

★ **The hub is where power is distributed, not where it is made.** `GS-1`, the flywheel, **stays on the hub** when loads are swapped — quick-release collars let the blower, spindle and mill come on and off without touching the wheel.

**Power path:** water → hub collars → belt → blower (mid) → forge.

## West leg — the workshop

`FORGE-D` sits on this cheek, facing the south court.

⚠ `MILLHOUSE-1` was the **~3 × 4 m Phase 1 open workshop** at the `HOUSE` marker. It is gone — superseded, not a second building. Bench and machine tools carried over: `WORKBENCH-1`, `MILLHOUSE-SHELF-1`.
