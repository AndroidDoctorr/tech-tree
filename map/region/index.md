## Corridors

Three roads leave the campus, and they diverge early.

| Corridor | Furthest live | State |
|---|---|---|
| **Route A west** *(via the Kırıkhan split)* | **~220 km** at the Kozan north gate | **Tin verified.** Waystations deployed the whole way. Composite `TQ ~2.9` |
| **Aleppo Road east** *(after the Asi crossing)* | **~32 km east** at `AM-EAST-05` and `AM-ALEPPO-SPUR-1` | ★ **Best road on the map — `TQ ~3.47`.** Kaolin and bitumen both come off it |
| **Coast west** | **~20 km** at `COAST-SITE-1` | Complete d202. A **day trip**, not an expedition |

★ **The scale difference is the thing to hold onto.** `COAST-SITE-1` at ~20 km west is a day out and back. The Cilician plain is **100+ km** of graded string. The Orontes mouth at 60–80 km is a separate boat problem entirely and is **not required** for the land ore push.
