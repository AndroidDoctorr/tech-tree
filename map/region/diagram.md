# Route diagram — the itinerarium

**A schematic, not a map.** ★★ *Shape, bearing and terrain are deliberately thrown away. What survives is what you actually need on the road: **what connects to what, how far, what you cross, and what is there when you arrive.***

Built after the **Tabula Peutingeriana** — a Roman road map drawn as a long strip, useless for navigating by and excellent for planning a journey. **This one runs vertically.** Distances are **cumulative km from HOME** down the left margin.

Canonical numbers live in [trails-and-bridges.md](trails-and-bridges.md) and [resources.md](resources.md). ☠ **This file draws them; it does not own them.**

### ★ Why it happened, and it is not carelessness

★★ **The inner legs are the OLDEST.** *`L0` through `L4` were cut in the first years, by someone with no blasting caps, no concrete, no gravel train and no wagon to justify the grade.* **The outer legs were built later by a far more capable person, to a better standard, with better tools** — and then the inner legs were never revisited, because **nobody stops two kilometres from the ford to do roadworks when the errand is a hundred kilometres away.**

> ☠ ★★★ **THE ROAD IS OLDEST WHERE IT IS BUSIEST, AND THE BUSY PART IS THE PART YOU DRIVE THROUGH ON THE WAY TO THE WORK.** *Every outbound lap begins by burning the first twenty-eight kilometres in a hurry, with a full day ahead and a loaded wagon — which is exactly the wrong state of mind and the wrong load for grading.*

⚑ **Correction: the inner band gets its own lap.** ★★ *Not a leg of an expedition — a day out and back from home, tools only, treating `L0`–`L4` as campus infrastructure rather than as the first stretch of somewhere else.* **Twenty-eight kilometres is a comfortable day with an empty wagon.**

### ★ And the multiplier argument

★★★ **A leg inside 30 km is crossed on every journey in every direction — west, east, north, and the coast.** *`L0` carries traffic that `L13` will never see.*

> ★★ **Grade improvement is worth `TQ gain × laps per year`, and the inner legs win that product by an order of magnitude even before the higher standard is applied.** ☠ *The standard is not sentiment about the home stretch. It is the only band where the arithmetic is obvious.*
