# Assistant Introduction Message

This is the first message I saw displayed on the screen of the Assistant device when I first arrived here:

```
Welcome new contestant!
Please read the following

1. This is a game. It is voluntary. If you do not wish to play, you don't have to.

2. However, once you begin, you must continue playing until you win or give up.

3. Your goal is to reach the exit portal on the nearest habitable planet (within X years???)

4. If you win, you will be awarded a large, bespoke prize, which may be negotiated to best fit your needs/wishes*

5. At the end, win or lose, you will be returned home safely.

6. You are under temporal compression†. Time passes about 100 million times slower than your original frame, so if you spend 10 years here, only about 3 seconds will have passed on your home planet.

7. If you die here, you will respawn in the last place you slept with full health and a restored body. Only your body respawns, not your possessions or clothes, they stay where they were. Even if your bed has been destroyed or moved, you still respawn in the last place you slept, regardless.
You cannot die of hunger, but you will suffer whatever consequences your species normally experiences from it otherwise.

8. You will not age here, but any physical damage you sustain will remain (or heal naturally) until your next respawn.

9. [SINGLE-PLAYER] You are the only member of your species here.

9. [MULTIPLAYER] There are 11 other contestants in the game. Whether you cooperate or compete is up to you.

*The size of your reward scales inversely with the selfishness of its nature.

†Do not ask me to explain temporal compression, just accept it!
```
