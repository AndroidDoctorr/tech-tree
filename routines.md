# Routines

**Created d3263.** The standing checks — daily, periodic, and triggered. **Seasonal windows live in [player-calendar.md](player-calendar.md); this is everything that fires more often than once a year.**

> ☠ **Why this file exists.** Until now there was no checklist. Every recurring check lived inside whichever project document happened to own it — drainage in the cave notes, a crust read in the trial notes, a tan step in the leather notes. **A project note is read when I think about the project.** In week 466 it rained five days running and **two standing reads fired and neither was walked**, because I was thinking about copper.

---

## ★★ The design rules

| | |
|---|---|
| ★★ **A trigger lives where its CONDITION is checked — not where its PROJECT lives** | The rain does not care what I am working on |
| ★★ **Prefer a STATE trigger to a CALENDAR trigger wherever the state is readable** | *"Every ten days"* drifts and gets missed. *"When the weight falls"* **cannot fire early or late, because it fires on the thing itself** |
| ★★ **A good routine is a CHEAP MEASUREMENT plus a THRESHOLD** | That is the whole reason the hive scale matters: it converts *"how are the bees?"* — which costs a smoker and an hour — into a number that costs nothing |
| **Every entry names what a MISS costs** | If a miss costs nothing, it is not a routine, it is a preference |

## ONCE A YEAR — ★★ SPRING, RIGHT AFTER THE HIGH WATER

- ☠ ★★★ **PLUMB EVERY FOOTING.** *Two minutes a crossing.* **ROT AND SCOUR ARE DIFFERENT CLOCKS — age predicts rot, WATER predicts scour — and scour is the one that drops a SOUND bridge into a river with no warning,** *because the hole is under water and the structure above it looks perfect right up until it does not*
- ★ **PROBE EVERY BURIED BEAM END** *(spike: sound wood resists, rot takes the point)*. **Ignore the deck — a timber bridge rots where the beam ends sit in the bank, dark and damp with no airflow**
- ⚠ **And re-read the REGISTER ITSELF.** ☠ ★★ *The campus bridge was in it TWICE under two names with two different grades for seven years.* ★★★ **A fact stored twice DIVERGES — and the tell is that the copies disagree about something SMALL. Hunt small disagreements; one row per crossing**

## ★★ WEATHER TRIGGERS — *run these against the sky, not against a plan*

| When | Do | ⚠ A miss costs |
|---|---|---|
| ☔ **Rain STARTS — while it is still running** | **Walk the CAVE-3 drainage.** *Trench, drip lip, steps, where the water actually goes* | ⚠ **Resolution. A dry walk reads the RECORD** *(silt lines, tidemarks — which give the maximum)* **but never the flow** |
| ☔ **Rain, and a cut face anywhere near the work** | ☠ ★★★ **READ THE SEEPAGE LINE on every terrace cut.** *A hillside carries water INSIDE it; a bench punches a hole in that plumbing and the water comes OUT OF THE WALL* | ☠ ★★ **The line shows about ONE DAY A FORTNIGHT and is invisible the rest of the time — and a drain cut without it goes to the wrong depth** |
| ☔ **Rain ENDS + 1–2 days** | **GYPSUM-STRIP-TRIAL crust read.** *A crust is a DRYING phenomenon — during the rain is too early* | The one reading that only exists in that window |
| 🌬 **Hard wind** | **Hive lids and roof weights · bird devices · drying racks** | A lid off in the rain is a dead colony |
| ❄ **Late frost warning** | **Vine · fig · any emerged bed** | A year of fruit |
| ☀ **Long dry spell** | **Rett pool level · tan pit level · beds** | A rett bundle stranded dry mid-process |
| ☀ **First properly fair day after a grey run** | ★ **HEFT THE HIVES.** *Grey days are days with no income* | ⚠ **A colony starves a few days BEFORE the flow, with everything about to be fine** |
