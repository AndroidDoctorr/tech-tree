## `G2-STRUCTURAL-CONCEPT-1` *(decisions d5072)*

### Crane vs building — **independent systems**

| System | Frame | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| **Crane runway** | **Dedicated column line** · **steel crane beam** phase 1 · timber struts optional bracing only | **Vertical + traverse load** · **not** wing wythe |
| **Building shell** | **BC-2 block stem** · grouted cores · **closed ring beam** · **timber roof diaphragm** | **Envelope + quake diaphragm** · **self-weight + snow** |

> **Do not hang the crane from the block ring beam.** Tie crane columns to footings and to a **separate runway beam**; **gap or slip joint** at the party wall so wing shrink/crack does not rack the runway.

### Timber?

| Element | Phase 1 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Crane columns** | **Steel tube or `REBAR`/bloom built-up posts** on **anchor bases** | Timber posts **NO** for 500 kg class upgrade path |
| **Crane beam** | **Steel I-class** *(forge + bolt)* · **timber pad pieces** only as bearing shims | Upgrade to 1–2 t = **swap beam + check pad** |
| **Roof** | **Timber truss / rafter** on ring beam · **strap tie-down** per BC-2 | Same grammar as Officina / north wing |
| **Temp aisle wall** | **Timber stud** · **not** load-bearing | Stripped when central bay closes |

### When crane footings go in

| Pour | Contents |
|---|---|
| **`G2-FOOTING-POUR-1`** *(first dig hero)* | **Continuous strip** @ full **18×10** perimeter + **party-wall strips** · **thickened pads @ `CRANE-COL-N1/N2`** · **anchor sleeve templates cast in** · **dowels for wing stem** |
| **Later** | **No second chance** on sleeve grid — **column bases bolt down** after stem to pad level |

**Pad thickening:** **~1.2 m × 1.2 m × **~600 mm** depth class** @ each crane column · **4× anchor sleeves** · **PVC or greased cardboard voids** for upgrade bolt pattern.

**Wing choice:** **East 4×10 machine shop** builds first *(player d5072 — either flank OK)* · **temp wall on west face** of east wing.
