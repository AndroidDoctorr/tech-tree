# Campus district master plan

**Filed:** d4733 · **Cal-Y13** *(living document)*  
**Authority:** [BC-3 site and layout](../government/regulations/building-code-2.md#bc-3--site-and-layout) · [map/campus/index.md](../map/campus/index.md) · stake grammar **`CAMPUS-MASTER-PLAN-1`** *(d883 strings)*  
**Related:** [ec-1-campus-expansion.md](ec-1-campus-expansion.md) · [next-house.md](next-house.md) · [construction-mats.md](construction-mats.md)

**Intent:** One spatial strategy for the home precinct — five districts, a paved circulation network with staging and parking, utilities sized for the *finished* campus, and build phases that never foreclose a later wing, road, or drain main.

---

## Principles

| Priority | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Drainage before decoration** | Crown roads and size **`DRAIN-D-*` mains for final paved area** — not today’s footpath. Permeable beds and gravel aprons are deliberate load relief. |
| **Clean downwind / upslope of dirty** | Smoke, dust, acid fumes, and kiln plenum exhaust stay in the **industrial** envelope; **research** and **residential** get separation by distance *and* by terrace step where the shelf allows. |
| **Manufacturing beside industrial** | Machine yard, garage, north work wing, and Fabrica power share **short belt runs** and **one heavy trunk class** — but **clean rooms do not share air** with forge or fume hood. |
| **Nothing alone in a field** | Every pad ties to **wall, terrace lip, or garden belt** within one build generation — interim stakes OK; permanent structures sit in **composed blocks**, not isolated boxes. |
| **Earthquake grammar** | Redundant routes · no irreplaceable co-located · nothing heavy above essentials · **crumple-zone** links between house and workshop per [next-house.md](next-house.md) · dry-lay garden edges, mortared stone for security walls. |
| **Walk it a season** | BC-3: stake envelopes, live in them, then commit stone and pavement. Desire lines become road width, not fights with walls. |

> **Manufacturing and industrial share a border** along the T-1 / Fabrica approach and a **paved heavy lane** from camp staging to WW-YARD. **Research stays south of that pair** on the mid shelf. **Residential sits highest** on the retained terrace — above bank scour and above routine flood splash class.

**Wind and plume (design check each new stack):** note dominant weather on the day of commission; **do not route residential air intakes or research open windows across the industrial plume path**. When in doubt, add **wall height or a deflector** before adding a new kiln or hood outlet.

## Circulation — paved roads, walks, parking, staging

### Surface hierarchy

| Surface | Use | District |
|---|---|---|
| **Asphalt or concrete** | Cart roads · machine yards · kiln aprons · wagon parking · material drop zones | Industrial · Manufacturing · staging hubs |
| **Stone or brick** | Pedestrian walks · courtyard rings · Domus passages | Residential · Research · Agricultural *paths* (not bed floors) |
| **Stone walls** | Retaining · security · terrace ties | All districts — **mortared** where animals or collapse risk |
| **Palisade / fence** | Agricultural perimeter · sleep-area wildlife barrier | Agricultural · Residential margins |

### Road network — target grammar

1. **Spine A — `ROAD-SPINE-1`:** camp (0 m) ↔ Domus terrace ↔ Officina portico — **resident and research loop**, stone walk + eventual parallel **light cart strip** where barrows repeat.
2. **Spine B — `ROAD-SPINE-2`:** camp ↔ T-1 fork ↔ Fabrica / WW-YARD — **heavy paved** · connects to existing **`PATH-CROWN`** crown work.
3. **Spine C — `ROAD-SPINE-3`:** camp ↔ `CAMPUS-BRIDGE` ↔ farm / barn — **agricultural service** · ties to T-2; **finish bridge scour apron before loaded staging depends on it**.
4. **Cross-link M–I — `ROAD-MI-LINK-1`:** Fabrica south court ↔ manufacturing yard ↔ kiln terrace service — **yellow/red shared staging** · no through traffic under unstable loads uphill of Domus.
5. **Parking bays — `PARK-*`:** named **pull-offs** (wagon · cart · future motor class) **off the spine**, not in courtyard centres:
   - `PARK-DOMUS-1` — guest / delivery · downhill of impluvium drain
   - `PARK-FABRICA-1` — ore and charcoal · quench-safe distance
   - `PARK-MFG-1` — machines and belt service
   - `PARK-FARM-1` — hay and harvest cart · mud season drain
   - `PARK-CAMP-1` — general visitor · ties to formalized pile courts

### Staging courts — material piles

Replace ad-hoc “middle of yard” with **named courts** on **graded pavement** draining to a **single sump / swale** each:

| Court | ID class | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| **Aggregate & sand** | `STAGE-AGG-1` | Concrete / road / filter projects |
| **Timber & charcoal** | `STAGE-WOOD-1` | Fuel and construction lumber |
| **Ore & metal** | `STAGE-ORE-1` | Industrial island — red posts |
| **Clean stock** | `STAGE-CLEAN-1` | Research — fibre, glass, instruments — **no forge dust** |

**Rule:** a pile may sit on dirt **once**; when it becomes permanent, it **moves to a court** or **gets a pad and drain** — otherwise the district bleeds mud into the road network.

## Water and power — plan for final draws

### Water tiers *(unchanged doctrine — extend, don’t mix)*

| Tier | Plan action |
|---|---|
| **Potable** | Weir pool remains primary drink · rain supplement at Domus only |
| **Domestic aqueduct** | **Header @ Fabrica** stays hub · **size branches for future house + greenhouse trickle** before closing new walls |
| **Power water** | WW-1 / WW-2 · **manufacturing loads** (crusher, gen, plenum) stay on race grammar — no new lift |
| **Irrigation** | Farm ditch and rett **downstream of drink reach** · agricultural district expansion **does not cross domestic trough without an air gap** |

**Drainage:** every new paved acre ties into **`DRAIN-D-1`** family or a **district sub-main** sized at stake time for **full district envelope**, not first building footprint.

### Electrical — align with [ec-1-campus-expansion.md](ec-1-campus-expansion.md)

| District | Trunk strategy |
|---|---|
| **Industrial** | Feeder-1 class · chem + motor taps · **fuse every branch** |
| **Manufacturing** | **New trunk** (Feeder-3 / garage class) · **never hang motors on lamp feeders** |
| **Research** | Feeder-2 + **`GRID-TAP-WING-N-1`** when north wing machines commission |
| **Residential** | Domus margin taps · lighting first · **heavy kitchen appliances gen-or-bank named** |
| **Agricultural** | Minimal early · **pen float and apiary scale** as low-draw branches when needed |

**Minimize losses:** wheelhouse → building entry is the expensive run — **place manufacturing and industrial on the short side of the map from `GEN-WW-2`**, research and residential on **separate trunks** so dim lights do not share wire with stall current.

## Integration — terraces, gardens, walls

| Element | Function |
|---|---|
| **Retaining terraces** | **`TERRACE-UP` grammar** — each bench **falls to a back-drain** at the cut foot · tie Domus, Officina, and farm steps into one readable cascade |
| **Garden belts** | **`GARDEN-ORCHARD`** and oak reserve — **green district fingers** into residential and research margins · permeable strip between paved zones |
| **Stone walls** | **`SR-W1` / `SR-S1` / `PALISADE-2` class** — agricultural and residential **security** · industrial **baffles** (dust, goats, cart edge) |
| **Walkway colonnades** | North passage ghost · portico links — **purple/blue** accents · earthquake: **light cover, heavy posts tied down** |

Nothing finished should read as a **single building in open grass** — minimum pairing: **wall segment + walk + one planted bed** or **shared terrace lip** with the next pad.

## Checklist before any new permanent pad

1. **Which district?** Color accent named on the stake card.
2. **Drain main sized for full district pavement?** If no → phase 0/1 first.
3. **Two routes still open after a shake?** If no → reroute spine, don’t build.
4. **Heavy above essential?** If yes → move pad or shorten wall.
5. **Chase / trunk stub in before wall close?** EC-1 + BC-3 water rule.
6. **Wildlife:** agricultural and residential margins **fenced** before trusting sleep.
7. **One season walk** on strings — BC-3 commit rule.
