## ★★★ The two-furnace note *(d3582)*

> ☠ ★★★ **YOU CANNOT HAVE MAXIMUM TEMPERATURE AND FULL ISOLATION IN THE SAME CHAMBER.** *A muffle wall is a thermal barrier by definition — the charge is always colder than the fire.* ★★★ **That is the d3311 gradient lesson wearing a third coat: the thing that protects the charge is the thing that starves it of heat.**

**So build two, and let each be good at one thing.**

| | **`FURNACE-2` — the melt** | **`MUFFLE-1` — the treatment** |
|---|---|---|
| **Job** | Crucible melts · reduction · master alloys | Anneal · carburise · controlled soak |
| **Target** | ★★ **As hot as the blast will carry it** | ~700–900 °C, held and read |
| **Isolation** | ★★★ **The CRUCIBLE is the muffle** — lidded and luted, it isolates perfectly **at full furnace temperature**, because that wall has to exist anyway | Sealed box, gas pipe, charge never sees flame |
| **Atmosphere** | Set by what is packed IN: charcoal to reduce · ★ **a glass cover to seal** *(d3262 — molten glass FLOWS where charcoal is lumps with air between)* | Pipe-fed · air · H₂ · flue-gas purge · carburising pack |

### Getting hotter — ranked

1. ★★★ **HOT BLAST.** Run the incoming air through a duct in the flue before the tuyère. **The heat going up the stack is the thing that makes the fire hotter** — historically a third off the fuel and a large jump in temperature, for ductwork and nothing else. ★★ **Sixth time the waste phase was the product phase**
2. **Forced blast off the water wheel** — precedent exists, collar #4 already drives the cellar fan *(d3018)*
3. **Refractory** — forsterite off the serpentinite reject · kaolin `M-26` for crucible bodies
4. **Hot face + insulating back-up** *(the d2583 insul step)* · **small chamber** — temperature is power over loss area

### The gas pipe

☠ **Not steel, and not metal in the hot zone** — a metal tube in a furnace scales away. ✓ **Stoneware tube through the hot zone, metal only on the cool run**, joined with a **packed dry-sand annulus** — *the d3314 seal, invented for exactly this hot-vessel-to-cold-vessel problem, and it cannot hold pressure, which is the reason to use it.*
