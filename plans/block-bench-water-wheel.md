# Block bench — water-wheel vibratory press

**Status:** **PLANNING** · filed **d4779**  
**Goal:** Raise **`BLOCK-Y10`** throughput · cut hand-tamp labour · keep **`d4139`** mix QC

## Concept

| Piece | Role |
|---|---|
| **Water wheel takeoff** | **`WW-2-Q`** class power · slow · high torque |
| **Cam / eccentric** | Lifts **tamp foot** or shakes **mould table** · **2–4 mm** throw · ~30–60 strokes/min |
| **Mould nest** | **`BLOCK-MOULD-1/2`** retained · dual-station grammar |
| **Pour hopper** | **Wetted box** · **~1 : 2 : 5** premix from trough · gate to mould |
| **Eject** | **Tilt table or stripper plate** · boards to **`BLOCK-CURE-PIT-1`** without hand lift |

> Vibration **densifies**; it does not replace **three lifts** or **90 d cure**.

## Site options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Pit lane north · WW belt** | Near cure pit · power already routed | Crowded with char retort |
| **Pile 4 apron · penstock branch** | Sand/gravel/lime adjacent | Longer mould carry |
| **Fabrica west margin** | Dry · room for hopper | Far from submerged pit |

**Player lean:** wherever wheel + **~3 m × 2 m** pad fits without stealing char lane — **survey before stake**.

## Build phases

1. **Power survey** — RPM · torque at proposed belt · brake for mould change.
2. **Bench frame** — oak + iron pins · mould nest level · drain to apron.
3. **Cam + foot** — forge eccentric · leather / wood foot · height stop.
4. **Hopper** — plank + clay wash · vibrate-isolate from frame with felt gap.
5. **Eject** — foot pedal or second cam · prove **×8** batch without back strain.
6. **QC** — one **`FOOT-CUBE`** class break @ 28 d · compare to hand-tamped cohort.

## Dependencies

- **`EC-1`** not required day one · **mechanical only**
- **G2 block pipeline** benefits later — **do not block north roof** for this
