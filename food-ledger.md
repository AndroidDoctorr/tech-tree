## 5 — How worried should you be?

| Concern | Level | Why |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Starvation @ home** | **Not at all** | River protein daily · **~30+ kg** dry stores · preservation stack · cool cellar |
| **Bad year / weir failure** | **Low** | Snails · pistachio · parched · barrel · smoked backlog · hunt options |
| **Coast expedition length** | **Medium** — *active sprint* | Scout bank **~38%** of target — **working as designed** |
| **Diversity / boredom** | **Medium** | Fish-heavy plate · tiny pulse plot · no honey/oil/dairy routine |
| **Scale / bronze clock** | **Medium-long** | **~2 kg grain/year class** from Bed B — expeditions scale faster than farm |

**Verdict:** Treat food like **logistics**, not **survival horror**. The valley is objectively generous for ~10,000 BC Levant — *especially* with weir + farm + salt + smoke + cellar. Your bottleneck is **stored kilograms for weeks away from the weir**, not **calories tomorrow**.
