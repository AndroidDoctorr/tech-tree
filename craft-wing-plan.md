## Hub hearth placement *(options · filed pre–Week 251)*

**Constraints:** **~5×5 m** plan · **one exterior entrance** · **three wing doors** on shared walls · **hearth + flue mass** · **circulation without crossing fire** · hub = **dirty/airlock zone** · wings = **clean**.

| Option | Layout | Pros | Cons |
|--------|--------|------|------|
| **A — Corner hearth (recommended)** | **Entrance + mudroom on S face** · hearth **NE or NW corner** · wing doors E / W / N | No door on hearth wall · flue mass in corner · hypocaust plenum fills floor · wing stubs on 3 clean walls | Corner loses ~0.8×0.8 m floor |
| **B — Corner + baffle entry** | **Exterior mudroom wraps SW** · inner door opens E into hub · hearth **NE corner** | Entry never faces hearth · best clean isolation | Slightly longer path · more timber |
| **C — Mid-wall between wings** | Hearth on **E or W wall center** between two wing doors | Shared flue cheek serves two wings | Competes with wing door headers · flue crosses door lintel zone |
| **D — Center island** | Hearth **center** · ring circulation · 4 posts + hood | **Player choice d1751** · structural roof hub · even heat · **taller roof class** | Needs columns · doors route around · tight in 5×5 — **stake early** |
| **E — Entrance-side wrap (your idea)** | **Portico/mudroom on S** · **interior door E or W** · hearth **opposite corner** | Mud stops before hub · hearth hidden from entry sightline | Plan early so inner door doesn't clip flue |
