- **Proving a method:** **risk the WEAKER unit first and let the strong one go second**, and **do not accept a hopeful sign as confirmation** — wait for the thing that can only be true if it worked

- **Apiary:** **work MID-DAY** *(foragers out = quiet hive)* · veil + tied cuffs + smoker · **render wax ON WATER, never dry** · **a skep cannot be opened — top bars are the whole game** · **obligatory calendar:** [apiary-routine.md](government/procedures/apiary-routine.md)
- **Plaster reinforcement:** **fibre, not lumber.** Flax tow / hair through the mix works *(that is what haired plaster IS)*; **embedded wood pieces swell and shrink with damp while plaster does not, and crack it from inside.** A wood lath **BACKING** with plaster cast against it is fine — wood **inside** the plaster is not
- **Commutator:** **brush slightly WIDER than the gap** *(narrower arcs, much wider shorts)* · **the gap must fall where the coil makes no voltage** — and that spot is FOUND, not calculated, so build the brush gear to rock and clamp · **brushes are bundles of fine wire, not single contacts** · **ramp only at segment ends** or the spring fatigues and snaps · **every segment at ONE radius — jig it**
- **Coil work:** **serving costs ~12–13 m of thread per METRE of wire** — spun thread length is the unit, not flax weight · **two coats, never one** · **test isolation while COMPRESSING the winding** — a film that passes loose and fails tight fails after it is buried
- **Wire drawing:** **draw · stiffen · anneal to dull red · draw again.** Copper hardens from work only and softens from heat only — **quenching does nothing but save waiting** · **wax generously:** a dry draw scores the die and the die then marks every metre after it · **modest reduction per pass** — a break costs the whole length, not the pass
- **Copper for WIRE:** **POLE the melt** — green wood, gases scrub the oxygen out. **Gassy copper hammers fine and SNAPS in the drawplate.** Test: bend a dip double cold; if it folds without cracking, it will draw
- **Leaching:** **acid INTO water, never the reverse** · bucket + lime + rinse cloth **set out before the stopper comes out** · **measure reagent appetite on 50 g and MULTIPLY** before committing to a plan · a **green** liquor = iron, and iron cycles in the cell and eats current · fix by **stirring in excess ore until fizzing stops** · **the acid is a bucket, not a fuel — current wins it back**
- **Museum:** **PULL THE SPECIMEN BEFORE THE BATCH GOES IN** — ore, glass, a superseded first · select at the moment, never in hindsight · **a museum piece is spent stock** · register: [museum.md](inventory/museum.md)
- **Bird devices:** **habituation clock is ~4 days** — a one- or two-night trip is free, the stack holds. Move them on return · **a week away in a vulnerable window is the real risk**
- **Ore hauling:** **DRESS AT THE MINE** — sort and dump waste rock at the face, never haul gangue · **cut more than you can carry and cairn the surplus** *(cutting is slow, carrying is only heavy)* · **load cap = what the team pulls the full distance without going lame** · **keep azurite separate** — it is copper AND a blue pigment
- **Dairy away-days:** **a doe in milk is a DAILY obligation — leave the kid on her and she milks herself.** Do this before any multi-day trip
- **Rope:** **heavy rope cannot be laid evenly by hand** — needs a **whirler + grooved TOP** riding the lay *(that is what a ropewalk is for)* · **a soft-laid rope is a LASH rope — never under load** · **working load = breaking ÷ 6** · ⚠ **a knot costs another third — SPLICE, do not knot**
- **Field trials:** **ALTERNATING BLOCKS, never half-a-bed vs half-a-bed** — soil varies along a bed and halving confounds treatment with position. Applies to every trial
- **Assay kit:** **thumbnail** *(soft = gypsum class)* · **vinegar fizz = carbonate** · **hydrogen flame test** — the flame is invisible so it reads clean; run a KNOWN control first; blazing yellow = soda · **charcoal + blowpipe, then wet it** — rotten eggs = sulfate · **never discard the leach residue — it is half the sample**
- **Assay:** **glowing splint relights in the fume = nitrate** · **flare on glowing charcoal = nitre** · a feeble sputter is a trace at best · **crystallise by COOLING, not by boiling dry** — cooling sorts nitre from common salt, boiling to dryness throws them down together
- **Stop short:** **two boil-overs in three days.** Pull the pot before the endpoint and let cooling finish the job — cheese *and* liquor
- **Cave building:** **cant every path tread outward and trench above the mouth** — a cut stair is a gutter and will wet the room you built it for · **no lime mortar underground** *(will not carbonate in still air)* — dry stone, shimmed with stone · **do not widen a mouth you cannot re-narrow**
- **Hemp:** **dioecious + wind-pollinated = one field is one population.** Two lines need real distance · **GHAB-ISOLATION-PLOT must be chosen before the September run**
- **Woody herbs:** **layer, do not cut** — peg a branch while attached, sever in autumn · cuttings only on **half-ripe wood in June** · rosemary from seed mostly fails
- **Cheese:** **heat to just under a simmer, PULL THE POT OFF THE FIRE, then thread the acid in.** Boiled milk sets hard, grainy and squeaky · **do not set cheese at the tail of a hero day** · **milk crock lives in the ice vault**
- **Caves:** **carry the lamp low** — a flame guttering at knee height is foul air pooling in a low spot · turn around there · **anchor the rope outside** before entering
- **Sowing:** full standing procedure lives in [player-calendar.md](checklists/calendar.md) → **Sowing — standing procedure.** Key line: **walk the beds after rolling and look for seed sitting proud**
- **Acorn:** **15 Sep – 30 Nov is a calendar hero.** It collides with grain harvest every year — flag it on farm passes like a perennial
- **Nitre:** **turn ~every 18–21 d** · **weak ammonia = good** · **a cold bed barely works** — no yield until the ground warms · dark sun-side cover in spring
- **Seed bank:** **area and seed are one plan** — check the bank before re-cutting bed area
- **Gas bells:** **not barrels upside down** — gas pushes a crown **out** of its croze · tie the crown to the hoops · seal seams **from the inside**
- **Bitumen lining:** **warm dry wood only** · linen scrim in the joints · **never cold water onto warm pitch** · re-set the saw bevel per barrel diameter
- **Blowpipe:** **H₂ lit first, then crack O₂ in** · never oxygen into an unlit tip · **the flame is nearly invisible — test with a straw, never a hand** · both traps bubbling before the tip opens
