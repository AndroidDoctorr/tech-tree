## Agent reminders

- **Post-scratch beds:** **scare-only until sow**
- **Fence masonry:** **Post-harvest horizon**
- **Nitre bed:** **turn every ~18–21 d @ FARM-CARE · damp-not-wet · no visible bloom until warm weather — not a failure**
- **Chlor-alkali:** **outdoor only · downwind · divided cell · carbon anode · never let H₂ and Cl₂ share a space** · undivided cell = bleach, not chlorine
- **HF / fluorite fine:** **HF eats glass and most bench ware** · **`FLUORITE-FINE` chemistry BLOCKED until polyethylene (or better) vessels + dedicated hood grammar** · coarse flux is forge-safe
- **Gas rig:** **purge the first draw** (line holds air between runs) · top the bath to the fill line — a low bath lets a skirt lift clear · Pb = cumulative poison, wash before eating
- **Pb anode:** **white = sulfated (dead) · brown = formed (working)**. Low current density sulfates — stronger acid, smaller plate, or more driving voltage
- **Electrolyte:** **never dump a cell** — electrolysis splits water, not acid. Top up water · boil down to recover if it must be broken up
- **Seed:** **rag-test every bank two days before the sow gate** · **read soil at seed depth**, not air, not calendar · **seal the horreum bulk bays** — an open bay took damp and halved germination · **never sow an entire elite bank — always hold a reserve**
- **SEED-ARK-1:** **SITE ✓ + SHELF ✓ CAVE-3 back half** · wax-sealed glass jars · ⚑ **drier is CALCINED GYPSUM in an open dish, not touching the seed** *(rechargeable by re-burning, and it doubles as the meter)* · **grain + pulse only — oily seed cannot be arked**
- **Cave survey:** **seeps track a bedding line — search ABOVE the wet band, not along it**. Dry floors carry nitre; wet floors wash it out
- **Plaster:** **the pan BOILS while calcining — when it stops, count 100 and pull.** Over-roast = dead · **SET ≠ DRY — damp the substrate or it steals the water and you get chalk** · plaster **into** water till it islands · **never re-temper a stiffening batch** · **warms + swells** *(casts detail; clay shrinks)* · **whey gauge doubles working time** · **store stone not powder** · **interior only**
- **Copper smelt:** **roast the carbonate first** *(cheap heat does the decomposition)* · **the ore's own iron fluxes the silica** — a stiff slag traps prills, a thin one lets them merge · **won on ATMOSPHERE not temperature** — too much air burns copper back to oxide · keep the slag, it still holds metal
- **Oils:** **olive oil NEVER dries** — non-drying, stays tacky forever. **LINSEED is the drying oil** that polymerises to a hard film. Having the wrong oil is having none
- **Check the constraint:** **before working to close a shortfall, ask whether the number is PHYSICS or ARITHMETIC.** I was 10 m short of a figure I invented the week before *(d3241)*
- **Coil gauge:** **for a fixed winding window the gauge barely changes EFFICIENCY** — finer means more turns but thinner and longer, and it nearly cancels. **Gauge sets the volts-vs-amps trade; the window and the field set the efficiency** · **never mix gauges inside one armature**
- **Fine wire:** **cheap in METAL, expensive in HOURS** — 106 m of 0.3 mm iron was 59 g and most of a day. **Plan fine-wire work by the DAY, not by the gram**
- **Measurement:** **a reading is worthless unless the instrument is PROPORTIONAL and the unit is REPRODUCIBLE** — the tangent law gives the first, the Daniell cell gives the second · **spend scarce reagent on a standard without hesitating**: reagents are consumed, a standard pays into every measurement forever · **reproducible beats true**
- **Power train:** **buy speed with RATIO, not with turns.** A belt stage costs two pulleys; a 10× winding costs a kilometre of copper **and throws the gain away as resistance** · **never a single huge step-up — small pulleys slip**
- **Thistle:** **its own isolated plot, never in or near a bed** *(player directive d3237)* · **seed FLIES, so distance is not the control** — cutting every head at full flower is, and that is the rennet stage anyway · **prefer a cardoon-type:** big heads, one clump, does not run
- **Dairy:** **acid-set = fresh only, forever.** **Rennet is the only route to a cheese that keeps** — and the plant source is **dried thistle heads, gathered in summer** · **ricotta recovers a DIFFERENT protein from the whey**, so it is free food, not a second helping
- **Seed saving:** **a plant cannot give leaf AND seed.** Designate early, stake it, take it off the kitchen list · **select for vigour + LATE bolting** · **woody herbs layer, mint divides, neither seeds**
- **Hazard thresholds:** **ANNUALIZE BEFORE YOU SET ONE.** `1−(1−p)ⁿ` against **how many times the trigger actually fires per year**, not per calendar day. **1%/day = 97%/yr. 0.3%/day = 67%/yr.** Passive dailies need thresholds in the **thousands**, not the millions · **split flavour from consequence** — common-but-harmless is a line of prose, not a hazard row
- **Vines:** **nothing you do to a vine pays off the same year.** Shade a bud this summer and it comes up **blind** next spring · **snap shoots green** · **cluster-thin after set, not at budbreak**
- **BUILDINGS** *(BC-2 — see [building-code-2.md](government/regulations/building-code-2.md))*: **build for the earthquake, because I will be there for it** — ~1 damaging quake/125 yr and no lifetime to run out · **CONFINED MASONRY:** brick panel FIRST, brick ends left TOOTHED, concrete column cast AGAINST it SECOND *(reverse the order and it is just a post next to a wall)* · **continuous ring beam, tied into every column** · **hook the bar ends, 25–40 mm cover** · **no opening within ½ a wall-height of a corner** · **tie the roof DOWN** · **RETROFIT, DO NOT REBUILD — ring beam first** · **DPC is a HARD GATE before any interior plaster** *(gypsum is soluble and will dissolve out of a wicking wall)* · **lime-sand scratch coat, scratched green** — never plaster straight onto brick · **tow IN the mix; scrim on JOINTS only** · **gypsum interior only, exterior render is LIME**
- **Jigs:** **cut a SPARE every time a jig is set up.** The jig is the expensive part; the parts are nearly free once it exists *(paid three times now)*
- **Proving a method:** **risk the WEAKER unit first and let the strong one go second**, and **do not accept a hopeful sign as confirmation** — wait for the thing that can only be true if it worked
- **Reproducible beats true** *(three times now)*: **when the correct value is unknown, FIND THE OPTIMUM BY MEASURED TRIAL instead of trying to know it** — the volt, the plaster burn ladder, and the constantan ratio
- **Apiary:** **work MID-DAY** *(foragers out = quiet hive)* · veil + tied cuffs + smoker · **render wax ON WATER, never dry** · **a skep cannot be opened — top bars are the whole game**
- **Plaster reinforcement:** **fibre, not lumber.** Flax tow / hair through the mix works *(that is what haired plaster IS)*; **embedded wood pieces swell and shrink with damp while plaster does not, and crack it from inside.** A wood lath **BACKING** with plaster cast against it is fine — wood **inside** the plaster is not
- **Commutator:** **brush slightly WIDER than the gap** *(narrower arcs, much wider shorts)* · **the gap must fall where the coil makes no voltage** — and that spot is FOUND, not calculated, so build the brush gear to rock and clamp · **brushes are bundles of fine wire, not single contacts** · **ramp only at segment ends** or the spring fatigues and snaps · **every segment at ONE radius — jig it**
- **Coil work:** **serving costs ~12–13 m of thread per METRE of wire** — spun thread length is the unit, not flax weight · **two coats, never one** · **test isolation while COMPRESSING the winding** — a film that passes loose and fails tight fails after it is buried
- **Wire drawing:** **draw · stiffen · anneal to dull red · draw again.** Copper hardens from work only and softens from heat only — **quenching does nothing but save waiting** · **wax generously:** a dry draw scores the die and the die then marks every metre after it · **modest reduction per pass** — a break costs the whole length, not the pass
- **Copper for WIRE:** **POLE the melt** — green wood, gases scrub the oxygen out. **Gassy copper hammers fine and SNAPS in the drawplate.** Test: bend a dip double cold; if it folds without cracking, it will draw
- **Leaching:** **acid INTO water, never the reverse** · bucket + lime + rinse cloth **set out before the stopper comes out** · **measure reagent appetite on 50 g and MULTIPLY** before committing to a plan · a **green** liquor = iron, and iron cycles in the cell and eats current · fix by **stirring in excess ore until fizzing stops** · **the acid is a bucket, not a fuel — current wins it back**
