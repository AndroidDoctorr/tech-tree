**Don't wait for perfection to add roofs.** Sequence:

| Order | Structure | Material | Role |
|-------|-----------|----------|------|

| **2** | Hut v1 **convert** | Existing thatch + **brick repair** | **Dry storage** — grain, lime, green brick |

| **5** | Hut v3 (Year 2?) | Wattle or **brick extension** | Only if family/size demands |

**Wattle & daub now?** **Yes for workshop/storage — no for sleeping.**

| | Brick house | Wattle workshop |
|--|-------------|-----------------|
| **Cost** | **~200+ fired** + stones + lime weeks | **Poles + ~20 kg clay** + lime skim |
| **Time** | Weeks hoard + dig | **2–3 days** |
| **Durability** | Decades with mortar | **5–15 yr** with lime daub; rats, moisture |
| **Best use** | **Living + hypocaust** | **Leather, tools, overflow store** |

**Stone:** Use **FS-01–11** + ongoing T-1 haul for **footings, suspensura, door thresholds** — not random rubble walls (slow, cold bridges). **Tier 3 grout** on stone–brick joints when lime budget allows.

**Reuse doctrine:** Every structure **extends** the last — v1 becomes store, v2 is home, workshop is **detachable**. Don't build **Kiln D** before **hut footing**.

**Long-term (*player Day 66*):** Grow **outward** → **square compound + central courtyard** (Year 2+). Pick courtyard **drain + axis** at hut v2 footing. See [materials-roadmap.md](materials-roadmap.md) § courtyard compound.
