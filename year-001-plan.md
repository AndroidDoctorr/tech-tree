## Bronze — critical path (honest)

Bronze is **not** "copper + wish." Minimum solo chain:

| Step | Depends on | Your status |
|------|------------|-------------|
| 1. Reliable **1100°C+** furnace | Kiln C + bellows | **PASS** Day 57 (~1100–1150°C est.) |
| 2. **Crucible** survives load | P-δ sand test | **PASS** Day 38 |
| 3. **Copper ore** (malachite/azurite) | Expedition | **M-07 green stain @ camp** — clue only, not ore body |
| 4. **Smelt** to metal | Charcoal × lots | Pipeline OK |
| 5. **Tin** (or arsenic copper cheat) | **Geography** | **Not local** — expedition required |
| 6. **Cast/forged** tool | Stone molds, anvil | Far future |

**Earliest realistic "bronze tool in hand":** optimistic **Year 1 Q3–Q4** if you expedition for ore in Q2; pessimistic **Year 2** if you over-build at home first.

**Faster cheat (assistant flags):** **Arsenical copper** (copper + arsenic minerals) — one metal source, toxic, not true bronze but harder tools. Still needs smelt.

## Expeditions — copper & tin (Levant / Anatolia ~10,000 BC)

| Target | Likely direction | Est. distance | Notes |
|--------|------------------|---------------|-------|
| **Copper oxides** | **East / NE** — Taurus foothills, Euphrates drainages | **80–200+ km** | Green stain at camp ≠ mine. Follow **malachite/azurite** on cliffs; assistant: copper cultures existed in Anatolia by this era |
| **Tin** | **North / NE** — Taurus; some Anatolian tin | **150–400+ km** | **Harder than copper.** No tin = no bronze — only copper |
| **Native copper** | Random river cobbles | Luck | Cold-hammer nuggets — **Phase 2a** before smelt |

**Expedition kit (before leaving):** dried food 7+ days, extra cordage, **chert backup**, lime-water canteen (pots), map copies, **respawn plan** (sleep hut only), charcoal for night fires.

**Do not expedition yet:** until **crucible + one proven smelt** at home on camp green rock or expedition sample.

| Phase | Focus | % energy |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Now → Day 60** | **80% build** (Kiln B, bricks, charcoal, lime repeat, farm) | 20% map/lime/shell |
| **Day 60–120** | **60% build** (hut v2 start, metal prep) | **40% scout** (2–3 short expeditions) |

**Rule:** No expedition longer than your **grain + fish + charcoal** buffer. You're immortal, not infinitely patient — **bronze soon** means **prove smelt at home before** walking 200 km.
